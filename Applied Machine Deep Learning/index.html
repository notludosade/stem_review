This is the advanced sequel to AI Developer, required before starting here. AI Developer's Units 4 and 5 introduced neurons, backpropagation, and CNN/RNN/Transformer concepts at an intro level; this course goes deep on the actual math and mechanics of building and training networks yourself — not just calling a pretrained one through an API.

1. In the water-pipe analogy for electricity, what does current correspond to?

Current is the rate at which charge flows, corresponding to the rate of water flow through the pipe in the analogy.

2. Fill in the blank: current equals voltage divided by resistance (I = V / R) — a rearranged form of ___.

Ohm's law, V = I × R, can be rearranged into I = V / R or R = V / I depending on which quantity is unknown.

3. A wire connecting a battery to a motor is accidentally cut, breaking the loop. What happens to the current through the motor?

A circuit needs a closed loop to carry current — breaking the loop anywhere drops current to zero everywhere in that loop.

4. Fill in the blank: a 20-volt battery is connected across a 5-ohm resistor. The current flowing through the resistor is ___ amps.

I = V / R = 20 / 5 = 4 amps, directly from Ohm's law.

5. Three 3-ohm resistors are connected in series across a battery. What is the total resistance?

Series resistances simply add: R_total = 3 + 3 + 3 = 9 ohms.

6. A string of decorative lights wired in series has 20 bulbs. One bulb's filament breaks. Answer both parts:

(a) What happens to the other 19 bulbs?

(b) Fill in the blank: because a series circuit has only one path, its total resistance is the simple ___ of each component's resistance, and breaking the loop anywhere stops current everywhere.

Because a series circuit has only one path, breaking it anywhere breaks it everywhere downstream, dropping current to zero for the entire loop.

7. Two 12-ohm resistors are connected in parallel. Answer both parts:

(a) Compared to a single 12-ohm resistor alone, what happens to the total resistance when the second one is added in parallel?

(b) Fill in the blank: the exact total resistance of these two 12-ohm resistors in parallel is ___ ohms.

Independent paths mean one component failing doesn't remove the path for every other component; 1/R = 1/12 + 1/12 = 1/6, so R = 6 ohms.

8. A car's analog speedometer needle sweeps smoothly to point at any position along its scale, including values between the marked numbers. Answer both parts:

(a) What kind of signal or output does this needle position represent?

(b) Fill in the blank: this needle's smooth motion through every intermediate value, without fixed discrete steps, is the defining feature of an ___ signal.

An analog signal varies continuously and can take on any value within a range, with no discrete steps between values.

9. A long digital data cable in a noisy industrial environment still transmits its 0s and 1s correctly, while a comparable analog audio cable in the same environment picks up audible interference. Answer both parts:

(a) What explains the digital cable's advantage?

(b) Fill in the blank: an analog signal has no way to separate "the original signal" from added noise, since its exact ___ directly carries the information.

An analog signal's exact value directly carries the information, so any noise corrupts it — a digital signal only needs a coarse threshold decision, tolerating far more noise.

10. A camera sensor captures continuously-varying light intensity and samples it at regular intervals, rounding each sample to the nearest digital brightness level, to produce a stored digital photo. Answer both parts:

(a) What is this conversion process called?

(b) Fill in the blank: converting that stored digital photo's values back into a continuously-varying signal to display on an analog screen would be called ___ conversion.

ADC samples an analog signal into digital values; DAC reverses the process — a smartphone typically does both.

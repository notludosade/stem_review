1. A resistor has 18 V across it and a current of 4.5 A through it. What is its resistance?

R = V/I = 18/4.5 = 4 Ω.

2. A tungsten wire (ρ = 5.6×10⁻⁸ Ω·m) is 4 m long with a cross-sectional area of 2×10⁻⁶ m². Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: this wire's resistance is ___ Ω.

(b) If this same wire's length were doubled while its cross-sectional area stayed fixed, its resistance would:

(a) R = ρL/A = (5.6×10⁻⁸)(4)/(2×10⁻⁶) = 2.24×10⁻⁷/2×10⁻⁶ = 0.112 Ω. (b) R is directly proportional to length, so doubling L doubles R while ρ and A stay fixed.

3. A battery with EMF 12 V and internal resistance 0.4 Ω delivers a current of 5 A. What is its terminal voltage?

V = ε − Ir = 12 − (5)(0.4) = 12 − 2 = 10 V.

4. A 15 V battery (negligible internal resistance) is connected to a single 6 Ω resistor, forming a complete loop. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the current in this circuit is ___ A.

(b) If the wire connecting the battery to the resistor were cut, opening the loop, the current would become:

(a) I = ε/R = 15/6 = 2.5 A. (b) Current requires a closed loop; cutting the wire breaks the path entirely, so current drops to zero regardless of the EMF still being present at the battery terminals.

5. Three resistors, 4 Ω, 6 Ω, and 10 Ω, are connected in series with a 20 V battery. What is the current in the circuit?

R_eq = 4 + 6 + 10 = 20 Ω, so I = V/R_eq = 20/20 = 1 A.

6. Two resistors, 12 Ω and 4 Ω, are connected in parallel across a 24 V battery. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the equivalent resistance of this combination is ___ Ω.

(b) What is the current through the 4 Ω branch alone?

(a) 1/R_eq = 1/12 + 1/4 = 4/12 = 1/3, so R_eq = 3 Ω. (b) Each branch independently sees the full 24 V: I = V/R = 24/4 = 6 A for the 4 Ω branch (the 12 Ω branch instead carries 24/12 = 2 A; total current from the battery is 6 + 2 = 8 A, matching I = V/R_eq = 24/3 = 8 A).

7. Fill in the blank: at a circuit junction, currents of 6 A and 2 A flow in, while a current of 5 A flows out along with one more unknown outgoing branch. The unknown outgoing current is ___ A.

Kirchhoff's junction rule (charge conservation): ΣI_in = ΣI_out ⟹ 6 + 2 = 5 + I_unknown ⟹ 8 = 5 + I_unknown ⟹ I_unknown = 3 A.

8. A 5 Ω resistor is in series with a parallel combination of 8 Ω and 8 Ω. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the total equivalent resistance of this network is ___ Ω.

(b) If an 18 V battery drives this combination, what is the total current supplied by the battery?

(a) The parallel pair reduces to 1/(1/8 + 1/8) = 4 Ω; adding the series 5 Ω resistor gives R_eq = 5 + 4 = 9 Ω. (b) I = V/R_eq = 18/9 = 2 A total current from the battery.

9. A resistor carries 4 A of current and has a resistance of 15 Ω. How much power does it dissipate?

P = I²R = (4)²(15) = (16)(15) = 240 W.

10. A 200 Ω resistor is connected in series with a 25 μF capacitor to form an RC circuit. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the time constant of this circuit is ___ s.

(b) After one time constant has elapsed while charging, the capacitor has reached approximately what fraction of its maximum charge?

(a) τ = RC = (200)(25×10⁻⁶) = 5×10⁻³ s = 5 ms. (b) After one time constant, a charging capacitor reaches 1 − e⁻¹ ≈ 63% of its maximum charge (a discharging capacitor would instead have dropped to about 37% of its initial charge).

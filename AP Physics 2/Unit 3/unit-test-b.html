1. A resistor has 30 V across it and a current of 2.5 A through it. What is its resistance?

R = V/I = 30/2.5 = 12 Ω.

2. A nichrome wire (ρ = 1.10×10⁻⁶ Ω·m) is 3 m long with a cross-sectional area of 5×10⁻⁷ m². Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: this wire's resistance is ___ Ω.

(b) If this wire's cross-sectional area were instead doubled while its length stayed fixed, its resistance would:

(a) R = ρL/A = (1.10×10⁻⁶)(3)/(5×10⁻⁷) = 3.3×10⁻⁶/5×10⁻⁷ = 6.6 Ω. (b) R is inversely proportional to cross-sectional area, so doubling A cuts R in half while ρ and L stay fixed.

3. A battery with EMF 9 V and internal resistance 0.6 Ω delivers a current of 4 A. What is its terminal voltage?

V = ε − Ir = 9 − (4)(0.6) = 9 − 2.4 = 6.6 V.

4. A 20 V battery (negligible internal resistance) drives a single 8 Ω resistor in a complete loop. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the current in this circuit is ___ A.

(b) If a second identical 8 Ω resistor were added in series into this same loop, the current would:

(a) I = ε/R = 20/8 = 2.5 A. (b) Adding a series resistor raises total resistance to 16 Ω; since I = ε/R, the same EMF over a larger resistance gives less current — the new current is 20/16 = 1.25 A, half of before.

5. Two resistors, 15 Ω and 25 Ω, are connected in series with a 40 V battery. What is the current in the circuit?

R_eq = 15 + 25 = 40 Ω, so I = V/R_eq = 40/40 = 1 A.

6. Two resistors, 9 Ω and 18 Ω, are connected in parallel across a 36 V battery. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the equivalent resistance of this combination is ___ Ω.

(b) What is the current through the 9 Ω branch alone?

(a) 1/R_eq = 1/9 + 1/18 = 3/18 = 1/6, so R_eq = 6 Ω. (b) I = V/R = 36/9 = 4 A for the 9 Ω branch alone (the 18 Ω branch instead carries 36/18 = 2 A; total current from the battery is 4 + 2 = 6 A, matching I = V/R_eq = 36/6 = 6 A).

7. Fill in the blank: at a circuit junction, currents of 7 A and 4 A flow in, while a current of 6 A flows out along with one more unknown outgoing branch. The unknown outgoing current is ___ A.

Kirchhoff's junction rule (charge conservation): ΣI_in = ΣI_out ⟹ 7 + 4 = 6 + I_unknown ⟹ 11 = 6 + I_unknown ⟹ I_unknown = 5 A.

8. A 3 Ω resistor is in series with a parallel combination of 6 Ω and 12 Ω. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the total equivalent resistance of this network is ___ Ω.

(b) If a 21 V battery drives this combination, what is the total current supplied by the battery?

(a) The parallel pair reduces to 1/(1/6 + 1/12) = 4 Ω; adding the series 3 Ω resistor gives R_eq = 3 + 4 = 7 Ω. (b) I = V/R_eq = 21/7 = 3 A total current from the battery.

9. A resistor has 20 V across it and a resistance of 8 Ω. How much power does it dissipate?

P = V²/R = (20)²/8 = 400/8 = 50 W.

10. A 500 Ω resistor is connected in series with an 8 μF capacitor to form an RC circuit. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the time constant of this circuit is ___ s.

(b) This same capacitor, fully charged, is now discharging through the resistor. After one time constant has elapsed, its charge has dropped to approximately what fraction of its initial charge?

(a) τ = RC = (500)(8×10⁻⁶) = 4×10⁻³ s = 4 ms. (b) After one time constant, a discharging capacitor's charge has dropped to e⁻¹ ≈ 37% of its initial value (a charging capacitor would instead have risen to about 63% of its maximum).

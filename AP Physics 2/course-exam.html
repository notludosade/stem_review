1. A gas releases 200 J of heat to its surroundings while 500 J of work is done on it, compressing it. What is the change in its internal energy?

Heat leaving means Q = −200 J; work done on the gas means the work done by the gas is W = −500 J. ΔU = Q − W = (−200) − (−500) = +300 J.

2. Fill in the blank: a gas is heated at constant volume (isochoric), absorbing 750 J of heat. Since no work is done at constant volume, ΔU = ___ J.

Isochoric means W = 0, so ΔU = Q − W = 750 − 0 = 750 J.

3. A point charge creates an electric field of 5×10⁴ N/C at a distance of 0.2 m. What is the magnitude of the charge?

E = kQ/r² ⟹ Q = Er²/k = (5×10⁴)(0.2)²/(8.99×10⁹) = 2000/8.99×10⁹ ≈ 2.2×10⁻⁷ C.

4. Fill in the blank: a point charge creates a field of magnitude 90,000 N/C at a distance of 0.3 m. At 0.6 m from the same charge (twice as far), the field's magnitude is ___ N/C.

Doubling r divides E by 2² = 4, since field strength follows an inverse-square law: 90,000/4 = 22,500 N/C.

5. A 40 Ω resistor is connected to a 10 V battery. What current flows through it?

I = V/R = 10/40 = 0.25 A.

6. A single 10 Ω resistor is connected to a 5 V battery. If the resistance were doubled while the voltage stayed the same, the current would:

At fixed V, current and resistance are inversely proportional (Ohm's law), so doubling R halves I.

7. A coil with 50 turns experiences a flux change from 0.002 Wb to 0.008 Wb over 0.05 s. What is the magnitude of the induced EMF?

ε = N(ΔΦ/Δt) = 50(0.006)/0.05 = 6 V.

8. Fill in the blank: a loop's flux is increasing steadily, and by Lenz's law the induced current creates a field opposing that increase. This opposition is required by conservation of ___ — a reinforcing current would generate energy from nothing.

A current that reinforced its own cause would create runaway, limitless energy, violating conservation of energy — that's why induced currents must oppose the change that creates them.

9. An object sits 12 cm from a converging lens with f = 6 cm. What is the image distance, and what type of image forms?

1/12 + 1/dᵢ = 1/6 → dᵢ = 12 cm; m = −12/12 = −1, a real, inverted, same-size image (object at 2f).

10. Fill in the blank: a concave mirror has f = 10 cm. An object is placed 5 cm from the mirror (inside the focal length). The image distance is ___ cm (include the sign).

1/5 + 1/dᵢ = 1/10 → dᵢ = −10 cm — a negative image distance indicates a virtual image behind the mirror.

11. Two coherent light sources of wavelength 500 nm produce a bright fringe at a point where the path difference is 1500 nm. What order (m) fringe is this?

1500 nm = 3 × 500 nm = 3λ, a whole-number multiple of λ, so d sin θ = mλ with m = 3 — third-order bright fringe.

12. Fill in the blank: a string fixed at both ends vibrates with 3 nodes between its endpoints (not counting the endpoints themselves). This is the ___th harmonic.

The nth harmonic has n−1 interior nodes; 3 interior nodes means n = 4.

13. A metal's work function is 4.0×10⁻¹⁹ J. Light of wavelength 400 nm (c = 3×10⁸ m/s, h = 6.626×10⁻³⁴ J·s) strikes it. What is the maximum kinetic energy of ejected electrons?

E = hc/λ = (6.626×10⁻³⁴)(3×10⁸)/(400×10⁻⁹) ≈ 4.97×10⁻¹⁹ J. KEmax = hf − φ = 4.97×10⁻¹⁹ − 4.0×10⁻¹⁹ ≈ 0.97×10⁻¹⁹ J.

14. Fill in the blank: an electron in hydrogen absorbs a photon and jumps from the ground state (n = 1, E = −13.6 eV) to n = 2 (E = −3.4 eV). The photon's energy was ___ eV.

E_photon = E2 − E1 = −3.4 − (−13.6) = 10.2 eV — absorption requires exactly this transition energy.

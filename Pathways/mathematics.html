The Route

1 of 4

Precalculus

Functions, polynomials, exponentials, logarithms, and trigonometry — the objects calculus is about to do things to.

 2 of 4

AP Calculus BC

Limits, derivatives, integrals, and series, following the College Board framework — computing, without yet asking why it's valid.

 3 of 4

Real Analysis A

From the field and order axioms through sequences and continuity, to a rigorous proof of the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

 4 of 4

Real Analysis B

Uniform convergence, power series, metric spaces, and a full rebuild of integration from Lebesgue measure theory.

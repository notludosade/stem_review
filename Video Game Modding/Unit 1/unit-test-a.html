1. Counter-Strike began as a mod of Half-Life that replaced nearly all of the original game's content while keeping the same underlying engine. What category of mod is this?

A total conversion replaces nearly everything about a game except the engine it runs on.

2. Fill in the blank: recoloring a car's paint without changing how it drives is an example of a cosmetic ___.

A reskin changes only appearance, with no change to underlying logic or rules.

3. What does purchasing a copy of a game actually grant a player?

A purchase grants a license under the EULA; the developer still holds copyright over the underlying work.

4. Fill in the blank: the U.S. legal doctrine that can excuse limited unlicensed use of copyrighted material, evaluated case by case rather than automatically, is called ___ ___.

Fair use is a case-by-case defense, not a blanket permission for any mod.

5. Which of these is the clearest example of a developer actively supporting modding?

An official SDK is a deliberate, active choice to support modding, unlike a neutral or hostile stance.

6. A modder wants to share a mod that changes a game's combat rules. Answer both parts:

(a) Which distribution choice carries the most legal risk?

(b) Why is that choice riskier than the alternative?

Redistributing the original developer's copyrighted assets outside of a mod that requires the base game is one of the clearest ways to cross a legal line.

7. Developers take different stances toward modding. Answer both parts:

(a) What typically motivates a developer to actively support modding?

(b) What typically motivates a developer to take a hostile stance instead?

Support is usually a business decision favoring longevity and goodwill; hostility is usually driven by competitive integrity or IP control.

8. Modding and anti-cheat systems are often in tension. Answer both parts:

(a) Why does this tension exist?

(b) What commonly happens to a developer's modding stance once competitive multiplayer becomes a priority?

Because both touch the same low-level systems, competitive integrity concerns often push a developer toward a stricter modding stance.

9. A game is no longer sold or supported by its original studio. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: this practical state is often called ___.

(b) Does this change the game's underlying copyright status?

Abandonware describes a gap in practical enforcement, not a change in the underlying legal status of the copyright.

10. Different points on the modding spectrum require different amounts of skill. Answer both parts:

(a) Which generally requires the least skill to attempt?

(b) Which generally requires the most, often involving a whole team over months or years?

The modding spectrum runs from minimal-effort reskins to total conversions that can take a full team years to build.

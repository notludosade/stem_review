1. The differential equation dy/dt = ky has general solution:

Separating dy/dt = ky and integrating always produces y = y₀e^(kt), scaled by the initial value y₀.

2. Fill in the blank: for y = y₀e^(kt) with k < 0, the half-life is t = ___ (in terms of k, with no absolute value bars).

Setting y₀e^(kt) = y₀/2 gives e^(kt) = 1/2, so kt = −ln(2) and t = −ln(2)/k — since k < 0, this is a positive time, the moment the quantity has fallen to half its value.

3. A bacteria culture grows from 500 to 1500 in 4 hours under dP/dt = kP. Answer both parts:

(a) The value of k is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the population after 8 hours is ___

(a) 1500 = 500e^(4k) → 3 = e^(4k) → k = ln(3)/4. (b) P(8) = 500e^(8k) = 500e^(2ln3) = 500·3² = 4500.

4. Newton's Law of Cooling states:

The rate of change is proportional to the gap between T and the ambient temperature T_a, not to T alone.

5. A metal rod at 300°F cools in a 60°F room with k = −0.08 (per minute). Answer both parts:

(a) T(t) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: T(5) ≈ ___ °F (nearest whole number)

(a) T₀ = 300, T_a = 60, so T(t) = 60 + (300−60)e^(−0.08t) = 60 + 240e^(−0.08t). (b) T(5) = 60 + 240e^(−0.4) ≈ 60 + 240(0.6703) ≈ 60 + 160.9 ≈ 221°F.

6. Fill in the blank: in dP/dt = rP(1 − P/K), the constant K represents the population's ___.

K is the carrying capacity — the population level growth slows to zero at as P approaches it.

7. For dP/dt = 0.4P(1 − P/500), answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the carrying capacity K = ___

(b) Growth is fastest (the inflection point) at P =

(a) Reading the equation directly, K = 500. (b) The inflection point is always at P = K/2 = 250.

8. In a mixing problem, dA/dt equals:

The amount of dissolved substance changes by exactly what flows in minus what flows out per unit time.

9. A 200-gallon tank starts with 10 lb of salt. Brine at 3 lb/gal enters at 4 gal/min; the well-mixed solution leaves at 4 gal/min. Answer both parts:

(a) The governing equation is:

(b) Fill in the blank: solving with A(0) = 10 gives A(t) = 600 + Ce^(−0.02t) with C = ___

(a) Rate in = 4(3) = 12 lb/min; rate out = 4(A/200) = 0.02A lb/min, giving dA/dt + 0.02A = 12. (b) A(0) = 600 + C = 10, so C = −590.

10. About the logistic model, answer both parts:

(a) As t → ∞, the logistic solution P(t) approaches:

(b) Fill in the blank: the logistic model differs from the plain exponential model by multiplying the growth rate by a factor of (1 − ___)

(a) The e^(rt) term dominates both numerator and denominator as t grows, so the ratio approaches K. (b) The factor (1 − P/K) is exactly what's new compared to dP/dt = rP — it drives growth to zero as P nears K.

About STEM+

STEM+ is a free, self-paced STEM curriculum: interactive courses in math and science for those who are ambitious. The menu follows how you grow: Learn holds every course by topic (Math, Technology & Computer Science, Science, Engineering & Physics, Advanced+ Courses); Practice holds the Problem Sets and Timed Mastery; Build puts the code sandboxes next to real-world Applications and capstone Projects; and Goals turns a goal into a Pathway or a personal plan. Practice and Build exist so you use what you learn, not just read it — every course pairs its lessons with unit tests, a gated course exam, and a progress report so you know where you actually stand.

How STEM+ content is reviewed

STEM+ lessons, tests, and practice questions are written with AI assistance. Automated checks run on every update: answer keys, question formats, links, and every sandbox and project test. Those checks catch broken content, but they can't prove every explanation is right, so each course shows its review status at the bottom of its pages:

  • AI generated — written with AI, passes the automated checks, human review in progress. Every course starts here.
  • Human reviewed — a person has read the course and corrected what they found, with the date shown.
  • Verified — reviewed and checked against the course's official curriculum.
  • Needs review — a reported problem is being fixed. Draft — still being written.

Human reviewed and Verified courses also show who reviewed them, what they were verified against (the official curriculum or framework a course is checked line-by-line against — for example, the College Board Course and Exam Description for an AP course), and what sources were used, right alongside the status.

Found something wrong — an answer, an explanation, a typo, a broken link, a diagram, or code? Use Report a problem at the bottom of any page. Reports go straight to the people maintaining STEM+, with the page and question attached, and you don't need an account to send one.

Progress & accounts

Everything on STEM+ is open — browse and try any course, test, Problem Set, Application, project, or sandbox without an account. Signing in saves your progress (unit tests, exams, problem sets, projects, tracks, and plans), shows your Dashboard on the homepage, and lets you generate an AI learning plan. Saved progress lives in this browser on this device; it doesn't sync between devices. Site maintainers can turn on developer mode to test gated content.

1. A servo motor measures its actual shaft angle and keeps adjusting until it matches the commanded angle. What kind of control is this?

Measuring the actual output and adjusting based on it is exactly closed-loop control.

2. A microwave runs for a fixed 90-second timer regardless of the food’s actual temperature. What kind of control is this?

A fixed timer executes a plan with no check on the actual outcome — open-loop control.

3. Fill in the blank: a cruise control system set to 55 mph, with the car currently traveling 48 mph, computes error = ___ mph.

error = setpoint − measured value = 55 − 48 = 7.

4. The Tacoma Narrows Bridge’s twisting motion grew into a catastrophic oscillation because of what kind of feedback?

The bridge’s self-reinforcing twisting is a classic example of destabilizing positive feedback with no negative feedback to counteract it.

5. Fill in the blank: a control strategy that drives an actuator to only fully on or fully off, based on the sign of the error, is called ___-___ control.

Bang-bang (on/off) control uses only the sign of the error, with no in-between output.

6. Why does a bang-bang controller tend to switch rapidly on and off when the measured value sits very close to the setpoint?

A single sharp threshold means even tiny noise-driven fluctuations near it trigger rapid, repeated switching.

7. Fill in the blank: using two separated thresholds instead of one, so a value must cross the opposite threshold before switching back, is called ___ (or a deadband).

Hysteresis is the standard fix for bang-bang chattering.

8. What effect does a narrower hysteresis deadband have, compared to a wider one?

A narrower band tightens control precision at the cost of more frequent actuator switching.

9. Why does an oven’s temperature often keep rising for a short time after its heating element switches off?

Systems with real lag keep responding for a while after the actuator changes state, causing overshoot.

10. Fill in the blank: a controller reacting only to the current error, with no way to anticipate an approaching overshoot from its rate of change, is exactly the gap Unit 6’s ___ term is designed to fill.

The derivative term gives a controller the "seeing it coming" capability bang-bang control structurally lacks.

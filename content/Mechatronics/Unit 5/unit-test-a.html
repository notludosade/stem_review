1. What is the defining structural difference between open-loop and closed-loop control?

The presence or absence of a feedback path from sensor back to controller is the entire distinction.

2. A stepper motor is commanded to move 200 steps with no sensor confirming it actually reached that position. What kind of control is this?

Executing a fixed plan with no check on the actual outcome is exactly open-loop control.

3. Fill in the blank: a thermostat set to 68°F, currently reading 60°F, computes error = ___°F.

error = setpoint − measured value = 68 − 60 = 8.

4. What is the key difference between negative and positive feedback?

Negative feedback corrects toward the setpoint; positive feedback amplifies deviation — opposite effects despite the similar names.

5. What output states can a bang-bang controller produce?

Bang-bang control is strictly two-state, with no in-between output.

6. Fill in the blank: the rapid, repeated on/off switching a bang-bang controller produces near the setpoint, caused by ordinary sensor noise crossing a single threshold, is called ___.

Chattering is the practical downside of using a single sharp on/off threshold.

7. A thermostat has an ON threshold of 65°F and an OFF threshold of 70°F. If the heater just turned on at 65°F, at what temperature will it turn off?

Hysteresis means the heater stays on until the opposite (higher) threshold is reached.

8. What is the trade-off of choosing a wider hysteresis deadband?

Fewer switches versus tighter control is the fundamental hysteresis trade-off.

9. Fill in the blank: a system’s tendency to keep responding for a while after an actuator changes state, due to thermal mass or mechanical inertia, is called ___.

Lag is what causes overshoot in bang-bang-controlled systems that don’t respond instantaneously.

10. Why is bang-bang control a poor fit for a cruise control system, even though a throttle is physically capable of smooth positions?

Restricting a smoothly-variable actuator to strict on/off throws away real capability — motivating proportional control instead.

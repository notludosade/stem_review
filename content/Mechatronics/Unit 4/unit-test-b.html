1. How many discrete levels does a 12-bit ADC have?

2^12 = 4096 discrete levels.

2. Fill in the blank: quantization error is at most half a ___ size, and can never be fully eliminated at any finite ADC resolution.

Step size sets the finest distinguishable voltage difference; quantization error is bounded by half of it.

3. What circuit would you add after a PWM output if an application genuinely needed a smooth, literal steady analog voltage, not just an equivalent average effect?

A resistor-capacitor low-pass filter smooths PWM’s rapid switching into an actual steady voltage.

4. Fill in the blank: a PWM signal with a period of 20 ms that stays HIGH for 15 ms has a duty cycle of ___%.

Duty cycle = 15 / 20 = 75%.

5. A 12 V supply drives a PWM signal at 25% duty cycle into a motor. What is the approximate average voltage delivered?

Average voltage ≈ duty cycle × supply voltage = 0.25 × 12 = 3 V.

6. For an LED (rather than a motor), why is there generally no comparable "deadzone" at very low duty cycles?

Unlike a motor’s mechanical friction, an LED has no comparable threshold effect, so its brightness tracks duty cycle fairly proportionally.

7. Fill in the blank: quantization error, electrical interference, mechanical vibration, and sensor jitter are all real sources of sensor ___.

These combined sources are what produce the random variation described as noise.

8. Three back-to-back readings of a stable, unchanging quantity are 3.01 V, 2.98 V, and 3.02 V. What does averaging them accomplish?

Averaging multiple noisy samples of a stable quantity tends to cancel out random variation, converging closer to the true value.

9. Why would a large moving-average window be a poor choice for smoothing a fast-changing joystick position signal?

Old readings linger longer in a large window’s average, causing it to respond more slowly to genuine fast changes.

10. Fill in the blank: an exponential moving average needs no buffer of past readings — just one stored running value — which is why it’s popular on memory-limited ___.

An EMA’s minimal memory footprint makes it especially practical on resource-limited microcontrollers.

1. Structurally, what is each joint of a robotic arm most similar to, among actuators covered in this course?

Each joint is essentially a servo-like closed-loop position system.

2. Fill in the blank: a position sensor that reports rotation, often as a stream of digital pulses or a directly readable absolute angle, is called an ___.

An encoder is the standard position-feedback sensor for a robotic joint.

3. What sensor gives a drone’s flight controller its measurement of current tilt and rotation rate?

The IMU is the drone’s core feedback sensor for stabilization.

4. What is an ESC (electronic speed controller), in terms of concepts from this course?

An ESC plays the same driver-IC role Unit 2’s H-bridge drivers played for brushed motors.

5. Fill in the blank: a quadcopter’s three independent rotational axes are called roll, pitch, and ___.

Roll, pitch, and yaw are the three axes a drone’s stabilization system controls, each typically with its own PID loop.

6. Why is ABS (anti-lock braking) described as bang-bang control rather than proportional control?

ABS pulses brake pressure fully on and off rapidly — a genuinely on/off decision matching Unit 5’s bang-bang definition.

7. Fill in the blank: standard cruise control typically uses a ___ controller to compute a smooth throttle correction from the gap between set speed and actual speed.

PID is the standard approach for cruise control’s smooth, continuously variable throttle correction.

8. What does adaptive cruise control add on top of standard cruise control?

Adaptive cruise control layers a distance-sensing loop on top of the standard PID speed loop.

9. What is a PLC?

A PLC is conceptually a microcontroller built to survive and remain reliable in demanding industrial conditions.

10. Fill in the blank: the traditional visual programming notation used for PLCs, originally designed to resemble relay wiring diagrams, is called ___ logic.

Ladder logic is the standard PLC programming notation, functionally solving the same control problem as text-based code.

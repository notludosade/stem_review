1. What role does an encoder play in a robotic arm’s joint?

An encoder is a position-feedback sensor, playing the same feedback role a servo’s internal potentiometer plays at smaller scale.

2. Fill in the blank: coordinating all of a robotic arm’s joints so the gripper traces a specific path through space requires ___, the mathematics relating joint angles to gripper position.

Kinematics sits on top of the per-joint control loops, coordinating them into deliberate, path-following motion.

3. What does stabilization mean for a drone’s flight controller?

Stabilization is closed-loop control applied to a physically unstable system, continuously correcting tilt.

4. Why is an open-loop (uncorrected) quadcopter not just a "worse-performing" drone, but one that effectively cannot fly?

For an inherently unstable system, closed-loop control isn’t an enhancement — it’s the only way the system functions at all.

5. Fill in the blank: a quadcopter typically runs a separate PID loop for each of its three rotational axes, all running ___ at once.

Roll, pitch, and yaw loops all run at the same time, sharing the same four motors as their combined output.

6. Why does ABS aim to keep a wheel just short of locking up, rather than applying maximum brake pressure continuously?

Preserving traction and steering control, which a fully locked wheel loses, is the entire point of ABS.

7. Fill in the blank: standard cruise control’s PID controller uses the gap between the driver’s chosen speed and the actual measured speed as its ___.

Error — setpoint minus measured value — is the quantity the PID controller reacts to, exactly as covered in Unit 6.

8. What sensor type does adaptive cruise control add, conceptually similar in role to Unit 1’s ultrasonic ranging?

A forward-facing distance sensor, with far greater range than an ultrasonic sensor, plays a conceptually similar ranging role.

9. How does ladder logic programming relate to the setup/loop pattern from Unit 3?

Ladder logic looks different from text-based code, but underneath it’s the same continuous read-inputs, apply-logic, drive-outputs loop.

10. Fill in the blank: in the conveyor-line example, gating the next step on a sensor confirming the previous step completed is a safety ___, the same priority-override pattern from Unit 7.

A safety interlock is exactly the priority/override pattern from Unit 7, chained across multiple stations.

1. A limit switch reports only pressed or not pressed. What kind of sensor signal is this?

Two discrete states with nothing meaningful in between is exactly the definition of a digital signal.

2. Fill in the blank: pairing a thermistor or photoresistor with a fixed resistor between supply voltage and ground, tapped at the midpoint, is called a voltage ___.

A voltage divider is the standard circuit for reading any simple resistive sensor.

3. Why must an ultrasonic sensor’s round-trip echo time be divided by 2 to find distance?

The measured time covers the full there-and-back trip, so one-way distance is half of it.

4. What does an H-bridge allow a microcontroller to do that it couldn’t do by wiring a motor directly to a GPIO pin?

An H-bridge’s four switches let a small control signal set the motor’s direction while a separate supply provides its real operating current.

5. Fill in the blank: a servo motor’s internal ___ lets its control circuit know the shaft’s current actual angle.

The internal potentiometer gives a servo its position-feedback capability, unlike a plain DC motor.

6. A 3D printer axis needs precise, repeatable multi-turn positioning. Which actuator fits best?

A stepper’s precise, repeatable, multi-turn positioning without a feedback sensor is exactly what a 3D printer axis needs.

7. What is the key structural difference between a microcontroller and a general-purpose computer?

Predictable, dedicated real-time control is a microcontroller’s defining advantage over a general-purpose, OS-managed computer.

8. Fill in the blank: the function used to configure a GPIO pin as INPUT, OUTPUT, or INPUT_PULLUP before the main loop runs is called ___.

pinMode() is the standard setup-phase call for configuring a GPIO pin’s role.

9. Why does pin configuration belong in setup() rather than loop()?

Setup runs exactly once for a reason — configuration is a one-time task, unlike the ongoing work in loop().

10. Fill in the blank: a PWM signal with a period of 8 ms that stays HIGH for 2 ms has a duty cycle of ___%.

Duty cycle = HIGH time / period = 2 / 8 = 25%.

11. How many discrete levels does a 10-bit ADC have?

2^10 = 1024 discrete levels.

12. What does increasing a moving average filter’s window size generally do?

A larger window averages over more readings, producing smoother but more lagging output — the core moving-average trade-off.

13. What is the defining structural difference between open-loop and closed-loop control?

The presence or absence of a feedback path is the entire distinction between the two.

14. Fill in the blank: a thermostat set to 72°F, currently reading 65°F, computes error = ___°F.

error = setpoint − measured value = 72 − 65 = 7.

15. What problem does adding hysteresis (a deadband) to bang-bang control solve?

Two separated thresholds mean ordinary noise near the setpoint no longer causes rapid, repeated switching.

16. Fill in the blank: PID stands for Proportional, Integral, and ___.

PID combines proportional, integral, and derivative terms into one continuously variable output.

17. Why does pure proportional control often leave a small, persistent steady-state error?

Sustaining a corrective output against a constant disturbance requires a persistent error under pure P control alone.

18. What causes integral windup?

Windup happens when the integral sum keeps inflating during actuator saturation, since it can’t detect the actuator is stuck.

19. Why is bang-bang control often the correct choice for a relay-driven heater?

Matching control strategy to what the actuator can physically do is the point — bang-bang fits a strictly on/off actuator well.

20. Fill in the blank: combining readings from multiple sensors measuring the same or related quantities into a single, more reliable estimate is called sensor ___.

Sensor fusion combines multiple sensors’ information into a single, more trustworthy estimate.

21. A control loop’s output consistently pushes in the wrong direction, making the system worse instead of better. What is the most likely bug?

A controller actively fighting itself is the signature of a flipped error sign, a common integration bug.

22. Fill in the blank: a position sensor that reports rotation, often as a stream of digital pulses or a directly readable angle, is called an ___.

An encoder is the standard position-feedback sensor for a robotic joint.

23. Why is ABS (anti-lock braking) described as bang-bang control?

ABS pulses brake pressure fully on and off rapidly, right at the edge of wheel lock-up — a genuinely on/off decision.

24. What is a PLC?

A PLC is conceptually a microcontroller built to survive and remain reliable in demanding industrial conditions.

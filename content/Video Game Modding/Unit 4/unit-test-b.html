1. What does UV mapping define?

UV mapping is the "unwrap" that lets a 2D texture correctly wrap onto a 3D surface.

2. Fill in the blank: a replacement texture appears stretched on a model because it doesn't match the model's existing ___.

UV mapping mismatches are the most common cause of a "looks broken but loads fine" texture swap.

3. What is audio sync?

Audio sync is the sound equivalent of a UV mapping mismatch — technically valid, but practically wrong if timing is off.

4. Fill in the blank: a mod replacing a game's voice lines and text with a translation into an unsupported language is called a ___ mod.

Localization mods expand who can actually play a game at all.

5. A bow deals 8 damage at 3 attacks per second; a crossbow deals 12 damage at 2 attacks per second. Which has higher DPS?

8 × 3 = 24 and 12 × 2 = 24 — the two weapons are actually perfectly balanced despite different stats.

6. A modder replaces a model file with a new mesh that has a different vertex count than the original. Answer both parts:

(a) What is this likely to break?

(b) Does the engine typically produce a clear, descriptive error message for this kind of mismatch?

Vertex count and rig data have to match, and a mismatch's failure mode is often confusing rather than a clean, descriptive error.

7. A modder wants to change a game's soundtrack. Answer both parts:

(a) Why is music a special case compared to most other game assets?

(b) Even the original developer may not have what regarding a licensed track?

Licensed music's rights are often narrower than a player might assume, even for the developer who shipped it.

8. A data-driven mod changes only values already exposed in config files. Answer both parts:

(a) What does this category of mod NOT require?

(b) Why is this category described as potentially high-impact despite being low-effort?

Low technical and legal barrier does not mean low impact — a single balance number can reshape an entire game.

9. A drop table defines what a chest can produce and at what odds. Answer both parts:

(a) Is a drop table typically stored as compiled code or as data?

(b) What kind of mod commonly targets drop tables to reshape a game's economy?

Drop tables are structured data, making them a natural target for the same data-driven modding approach as any other config value.

10. Unit 4 compared several asset-replacement categories on risk and effort. Answer both parts:

(a) Which category requires matching both a file format and a spatial or timing layout (UV mapping or sync) to look/sound correct?

(b) Which category from this unit has effectively no format-matching or layout concern at all?

Texture, model, and audio replacement all carry format/layout matching risk; plain config values do not.

Oct 5, 2026 · Major update

Update BETA v2.0.1 opens the full curriculum to everyone, reorganizes navigation around how students learn, expands practice to 4,800 questions, adds content reporting and review status, and introduces unit-level mastery with Pathway diagnostic readiness.

View Update BETA v2.0.1 →

Oct 7, 2026Progress now syncs across devices for signed-in students — test and exam results, problem set attempts, project and Application completion, diagnostics, lesson views, and Fluency Training scores all carry over when you sign in somewhere new. Nothing changes if you don't sign in.

Oct 7, 2026Renamed Timed Mastery to Fluency Training — same speed-practice format, a name that no longer implies it certifies mastery on its own. Lessons also now link to the real-world Applications that use their skill, and Pathway diagnostic results explain each course's readiness tier and why a given skill is recommended next.

Oct 7, 2026Added a Developer Panel where developer accounts can set each course's review status live, without a code change. Course status now has 4 levels: AI Generated, Review in Progress, Human Reviewed, and Human Verified.

Oct 6, 2026Added an automated check that verifies every course's answer keys are actually answerable, run on every update across the whole site. Course review status can now also show who reviewed a course, what curriculum it was checked against, and its sources.

Oct 4, 2026Added Timed Mastery for Precalculus and AP Calculus BC, with 500-question pools for each course, 1–20-question timed runs, mastery-adjusted speed grading, wrong-answer penalties, and saved personal bests. Published Quantum Computing, Mechatronics, and Advanced Robotics, plus the new Quantum Science and Robotics & Mechatronics pathways and capstone projects.

Aug 24, 2026AI-generated learning plans now remove duplicate courses, follow real prerequisite order, favor thorough routes, and let you skip material you already know.

Aug 23, 2026Advanced+ course pages now show their prerequisites and a personal readiness message based on the course exams you've passed.

Aug 22, 2026Rebuilt the Goals page around four clear starting points and changed generated plans and Pathways into connected roadmaps. Course pages now link directly to their related pathway, practice set, and real-world Application; developer mode is also a manual toggle that stays off until enabled.

Aug 18, 2026Added custom AI-generated learning plans: describe your own STEM goal and receive a saved roadmap built only from the site's real courses, problem sets, Applications, and projects. Generation requires sign-in and is limited to once every 24 hours.

Aug 17, 2026Expanded Applications to 9 published scenarios, including recommendation engines, epidemic modeling, GPS routing, hiring-algorithm bias, and satellite orbits. Pathways also gained course skipping and target-date pacing, with skipped work reflected correctly in the Dashboard and Learning Record.

Aug 11, 2026Added upcoming course listings for Complex Analysis A/B and B/C, AP Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, Designing/Mastering Operating System, and Advanced CB Engineering, each placed in its appropriate subject with prerequisites and difficulty.

Aug 10, 2026Course pages now explain where each course fits, recommend matching practice, and show its role in Pathways; project pages gained at-a-glance guidance.

Aug 9, 2026Every page on the site — not just the homepage — now has the persistent navigation bar and sign-in status. Nothing about how any individual page works has changed.

Aug 6, 2026Built and published 4 new courses from scratch: Thermodynamics and CAD & Prototyping (Engineering & Physics), Linear Algebra B and Topology: Fundamentals (Advanced+).

Aug 4, 2026Finished AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism (course exam + glossary) and published it alongside Quantum Physics & Optics, both previously marked "coming soon" despite being complete.

Aug 3, 2026Fixed a duplicate header and hard-to-read text left over from the new site navigation.

Aug 2, 2026Rolled the homepage onto a new site-wide navigation bar (Subjects, Tracks, Problem Sets, Sandbox, and sign-in are now always one click away). Added syntax highlighting to every sandbox code editor (Python, Java, JavaScript, C++), plus smarter backspace/indent behavior.

Aug 1, 2026Subject and track cards got their own color per category, with a subtle glow on hover. Switched sign-in to email + password accounts.

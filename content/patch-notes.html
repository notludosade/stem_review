Oct 9, 2026Launched the public content-trust and correction system. Course trust panels now keep automated checks separate from human review, and a course's Review Details page can show its reviewer, role, review date, curriculum framework, sources, assessment-by-assessment verification checklist, and correction history. Reports now retain the page, unit, lesson, and question reference; maintainers can move them through a real status workflow and publish resulting changes to the public correction log. AP Calculus BC is the first pilot, with its 2026 College Board framework and all 21 assessments—200 unit-test questions plus the 20-question course exam—registered at the honest default of zero human-verified questions until review actually happens.

Oct 8, 2026Added concept-level skill tracking to AP Calculus BC instead of treating a whole unit as one skill. Power Rule, Product Rule, Quotient Rule, Chain Rule, and Implicit Differentiation now connect lessons to 140 tagged questions in the existing bank; 60 newly checked questions expanded that course to 300 and the full library to 4,860. The course page shows first-try accuracy by concept, waits for at least three attempts before calling anything weak, and offers single-skill or combined weak-skill practice without granting formal MASTERED status. These concepts and their focused practice links also appear in global search and on the matching lessons.

Oct 8, 2026Reorganized the signed-in Dashboard so the work that matters fits near the top. The next lesson or assessment and the enrolled track now share one compact row, followed immediately by up to three skill-based Review cards; broad Problem Sets, course history, and the full mastery record remain available underneath. The same priority order collapses cleanly on smaller screens, and the Dashboard continues to use the site's existing progress and mastery data rather than introducing a second tracking system.

Oct 8, 2026Added Continue with Google and rebuilt the account page as a split-screen sign-in/create-account experience with clearer errors, preserved return destinations, responsive layout, and live curriculum totals. The OAuth flow validates Google claims and redirect paths, handles case-insensitive email lookup safely, and directs an existing password-account owner back to password sign-in instead of silently linking or duplicating the account.

Oct 8, 2026AI-generated learning plans now join the rest of cloud progress. A newly generated plan is written to the signed-in student's server record immediately, while an older browser-only plan uploads on the next successful sync; regenerating replaces the single authoritative plan instead of attempting an unsafe field-by-field merge.

Oct 7, 2026Progress now syncs across devices for signed-in students. Unit-test and course-exam results, all 20 Problem Set histories, projects, Applications, Pathway diagnostics, lesson views, Fluency Training records, and the active AI plan are pulled and merged without erasing richer progress. Writes are batched, malformed records are isolated, guest work can upload after sign-in, and a browser ownership marker prevents one student's leftover local data on a shared device from being merged into another student's account. Guests still use local browser progress normally.

Oct 7, 2026Published STEM Lite, a static, account-free edition generated from the same curriculum rather than maintained as a second copy. Its selected courses, lessons, Problem Sets, Applications, projects, calculators, and search work from a GitHub Pages project path without authentication, databases, server rendering, AI grading, or cloud sync. The full site and Lite also gained reusable Desmos graphing and scientific calculator pages, an AP Calculus Chain Rule graph preset, and readable fallbacks when a client key, browser feature, or network connection is unavailable.

Oct 7, 2026Renamed Timed Mastery to Fluency Training so a speed-practice score is not mistaken for formal mastery; the underlying 500-question pools, timed runs, penalties, personal bests, and mastery-adjusted grading remain intact. Lessons now surface the real-world Applications that use their unit skill. Pathway diagnostic results also show course-by-course Strong, Developing, or Needs Work tiers and explain why the recommended starting skill comes next.

Oct 7, 2026Added a protected Developer Panel for maintaining course review status without editing source files. Authorized maintainers can move a course through AI Generated, Review in Progress, Human Reviewed, and Human Verified; the workflow validates the evidence required by each status and keeps reviewer details, framework information, sources, and assessment verification attached to the review record instead of presenting a vague badge.

Oct 6, 2026Added global search to the persistent site header. Search indexes courses, lessons, concept and unit skills, practice, Applications, projects, sandboxes, calculators, Pathways, goals, and general pages; results are grouped by type, matching text is highlighted, common misspellings such as “derivitives” are corrected, and related content can appear alongside direct matches. Use / or Ctrl/Cmd + K anywhere, then move with the arrow keys and open a result with Enter; useful suggestions appear before typing and after an empty result.

Oct 6, 2026Unified Learning Record percentages with the shared mastery engine used by the Dashboard, diagnostics, course pages, and Applications. A course's readiness figure now comes from the same six states—Unseen, Learning, Practiced, Proficient, Mastered, and Applied—instead of a separate topic-average calculation, so students no longer see contradictory progress numbers in different parts of STEM+.

Oct 6, 2026Added site-wide answer-key, link, content-structure, and script-syntax audits to the release checks. The answer audit recomputes generated answers independently and rejects unreachable choices or malformed response keys; these automated checks run across the curriculum on every update, but are explicitly presented as structural safeguards rather than a substitute for human review. Course records can additionally name a reviewer, curriculum framework, version, and sources.

Oct 5, 2026 · Major update

Update BETA v2.0.1 opened the full curriculum to guests while reserving saved progress for signed-in students, reorganized the navigation into Learn, Practice, Build, and Goals, and expanded 20 Problem Sets to 4,800 independently audited questions. It also introduced the Report a Problem workflow, generated a 291-unit skill catalog, added the six-state mastery engine, connected Applications to the skills they use, and launched Pathway diagnostics that can raise readiness evidence to Proficient without replacing unit tests or course exams.

Oct 4, 2026Added the original Timed Mastery experience—now called Fluency Training—for Precalculus and AP Calculus BC. Each course received a separate 500-question pool, selectable runs from 1 to 20 questions, speed scoring adjusted to the student's course mastery, wrong-answer penalties, and saved personal bests. This release also published the completed Quantum Computing, Mechatronics, and Advanced Robotics courses, then connected them through new Quantum Science and Robotics & Mechatronics Pathways with their own gated capstone projects.

Aug 24, 2026Made AI-generated learning plans follow the site's real academic structure. Plans remove duplicate courses, reject catalog entries that do not exist, reorder valid selections through the prerequisite graph, favor complete preparation over artificially short routes, and let students mark already-known courses as skipped without breaking downstream progress or project requirements.

Aug 23, 2026Added course-level readiness to the shipped Advanced+ curriculum. Each advanced course page lists the courses that should come first, checks the student's saved course-exam passes, and explains whether they are ready or which prerequisite remains—without locking curious students out of browsing the material.

Aug 22, 2026Rebuilt Goals around clear starting intentions—getting ahead, preparing for college STEM, strengthening math, and taking on a challenge—plus a custom-goal route. Generated plans and all Pathways became connected roadmaps rather than unrelated card collections. Course context boxes gained direct links to matching Pathways, Problem Sets, and real-world Applications, while developer mode became an explicit manual testing toggle that stays off for ordinary students.

Aug 18, 2026Added custom AI-generated learning plans. A signed-in student can describe a goal in their own words and receive a saved roadmap assembled only from STEM+'s real courses, Problem Sets, Applications, and projects, with a reason attached to every recommendation. Structured output, catalog validation, HTML escaping, and a once-per-24-hours generation limit keep plans usable, on-site, and resistant to malformed model responses.

Aug 17, 2026Expanded Applications to nine published scenarios: viral-app scaling, A/B testing, video-call reliability, roller-coaster safety, recommendation engines, epidemic modeling, GPS routing, hiring-algorithm bias, and satellite orbits. Pathways gained a target-date pace estimate and course customization across every published route; declared skips are honored by project gates and reflected consistently in both the Dashboard and Learning Record.

Aug 11, 2026Added transparent “Coming Soon” listings for Complex Analysis A/B and B/C, AP Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, Designing/Mastering Operating System, and Advanced CB Engineering. Each placeholder was placed in the most appropriate subject, assigned a realistic difficulty tier, and given prerequisite guidance so it communicates where the planned course belongs without pretending unfinished content is available.

Aug 10, 2026Added course-context guidance to every course hub: what the course teaches, where it sits in the wider curriculum, which Pathways use it, and which practice and Applications reinforce it. Project pages gained an at-a-glance brief covering the outcome, expected preparation, and major work before students enter the full task sequence.

Aug 9, 2026Moved the curriculum into the shared Next.js site shell so every lesson, test, course, Application, project, and sandbox receives the same persistent navigation, theme, sign-in state, reporting access, and footer. The homepage became the signed-in Dashboard, and the new cross-course Learning Record summarized progress without rewriting the individual interactive pages underneath.

Aug 6, 2026Built and published four complete courses: Thermodynamics and CAD & Prototyping under Engineering & Physics, plus Linear Algebra B and Topology: Fundamentals under Advanced+. Each release includes ordered lessons, two unit-test versions per unit, a gated course exam, glossary/reference material where appropriate, and links from the subject and Pathway catalogs.

Aug 4, 2026Finished the remaining AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism units, course exam, and glossary, then published the course on the Science hub. Quantum Physics & Optics was published alongside it, correcting the catalog state where both complete courses were still labeled “Coming Soon.”

Aug 3, 2026Corrected migration leftovers from the new shared shell: removed a duplicated page header and fixed low-contrast text that became difficult to read under the site-wide navigation and theme styles.

Aug 2, 2026Introduced the first persistent site-wide navigation, keeping Subjects, Tracks, Problem Sets, Sandbox, and account access one click away from the homepage. All Python, Java, JavaScript, and C++ sandbox editors gained language-aware syntax highlighting plus editor behavior students expect: Tab and Shift+Tab indentation, automatic indentation after block openings, brace pairing, smart outdent, and backspace dedent.

Aug 1, 2026Established the visual identity system for subject, Pathway, difficulty, Problem Set, and Sandbox cards, including category colors and accessible hover/focus feedback. Authentication moved to first-party email-and-password accounts so saved student work could be associated with a real account rather than a device-only identity.

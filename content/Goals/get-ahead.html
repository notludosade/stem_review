Get Ahead in School

Work ahead of whatever your class is covering right now. Start wherever you already are — skip a course entirely if you've already got it down — and each one below builds straight into the next.

The Ladder

1 of 4

Algebra/Geometry Fundamentals Review

Linear and quadratic equations, factoring, exponents, similarity, and the Pythagorean theorem. Skip this if you're already solid on it.

 2 of 4

Precalculus

Functions, polynomials, exponentials, logarithms, and trigonometry — everything calculus is about to assume you already know.

 3 of 4

AP Calculus BC

Limits, derivatives, integrals, and series, following the College Board framework. Most students see this junior or senior year — this gets you there early.

 4 of 4

Multivariable Calculus

Calculus in more than one variable. Past this point you're ahead of the standard high school sequence entirely, not just early on it.

Once You're Ahead

Working through calculus doesn't have to mean stopping there. The Mathematics Pathway picks up right after AP Calculus BC and ends in a real capstone project — proving a calculus result you've used without ever seeing why it's true.

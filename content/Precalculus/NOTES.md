# Notes

- Wants a **fast, steady pace** with genuine depth — prefer tightly-scoped lessons delivered in quick succession over infrequent long ones. Don't pad for length.
- **Wants learning to be mostly interactive** (stated 2026-07-10, after Lesson 1). Lesson 1 was read-then-quiz; going forward, favor hands-on manipulables with a live feedback loop (draggable points, live-computed function machines, explorable graphs) as the *primary* vehicle for each lesson. Reading blocks should be short connective tissue between widgets, not the main event. End-of-lesson quizzes are still fine for spaced retrieval, but should be secondary/shorter now that the interactive widgets carry most of the concept-building.

- Shared interactive widgets live in `assets/interactive.js` (function machine, draggable point-on-curve, draggable vertical-line test) — reuse and extend these rather than one-off scripting per lesson.

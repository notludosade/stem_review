1. A tank of water heats up at a rate proportional to the difference between its boiling point of 100°C and its current temperature T. Which differential equation models this situation?

"Proportional to the difference between the boiling point and the current temperature" translates directly to k(100 − T); since the water is heating toward 100° rather than away from it, k > 0.

2. In the slope field for dy/dx = x + y, the segment at the point (1, −1) is:

x + y = 1 + (−1) = 0 at that point, so the segment is flat.

3. Fill in the blank: For dy/dx = x − y with y(0) = 2 and step size h = 0.5, one Euler step gives y(0.5) ≈ ___

The slope at (0, 2) is 0 − 2 = −2, so y₁ = y₀ + h·f(x₀, y₀) = 2 + 0.5(−2) = 1.

4. Separating the differential equation dy/dx = y/x (for x, y > 0) gives:

Dividing both sides by y and multiplying by dx moves every y-term to the left and every x-term to the right, ready to integrate independently.

5. Solving dy/dt = ky by separation of variables always produces a solution of the form:

Separating gives (1/y)dy = k dt, and integrating and exponentiating produces y = y₀e^(kt), scaled by the initial value y₀ with k in the exponent multiplied by t.

6. Consider whether y = 4e^(−3x) solves the differential equation y′ + 3y = 0. Answer both parts.

(a) What is y′?

(b) Fill in the blank: substituting y and y′ into y′ + 3y gives ___

(a) Differentiating 4e^(−3x) with the chain rule gives −3 · 4e^(−3x) = −12e^(−3x). (b) Substituting: −12e^(−3x) + 3(4e^(−3x)) = −12e^(−3x) + 12e^(−3x) = 0, confirming y = 4e^(−3x) is a solution.

7. For the slope field of dy/dx = y − x, answer both parts.

(a) At the point (2, 5), the slope shown by the field is:

(b) Fill in the blank: at the point (0, −4), the slope shown by the field is ___

(a) y − x = 5 − 2 = 3. (b) y − x = −4 − 0 = −4. Each segment's slope comes directly from evaluating y − x at that point.

8. Solve dy/dx = 3x²y with y(0) = 2. Answer both parts.

(a) Separating variables gives:

(b) Fill in the blank: applying y(0) = 2, the particular solution is y = ___ · e^(x³)

(a) Dividing by y and multiplying by dx separates all y-terms from all x-terms. (b) Integrating gives ln|y| = x³ + C₁, so y = Ae^(x³); substituting y(0) = 2 gives A = 2, so y = 2e^(x³).

9. A bacteria culture grows at a rate proportional to its size, starting at 150 and reaching 600 after 5 hours. Answer both parts.

(a) Fill in the blank: the growth constant k ≈ ___ (round to three decimals)

(b) Fill in the blank: using that value of k, the population after 10 hours is ___

(a) 600 = 150e^(5k) → 4 = e^(5k) → k = ln(4)/5 ≈ 0.277. (b) Since 10k = 2ln(4) = ln(16), e^(10k) = 16, so P(10) = 150(16) = 2400 — the population quadruples every 5 hours, so it quadruples twice over 10 hours: 150 → 600 → 2400.

10. A population is modeled by dP/dt = 0.3P(1 − P/800). Answer both parts.

(a) The carrying capacity K is:

(b) Fill in the blank: growth is fastest at P = ___

(a) K is the constant directly inside the (1 − P/K) factor; here it's 800. (b) The logistic curve's inflection point — where growth is fastest — occurs at P = K/2 = 800/2 = 400.

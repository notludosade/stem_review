1. What is limx→2 (x² − 4)/(x − 2)?

Factor: (x−2)(x+2)/(x−2) = x+2 for x≠2, so the limit as x→2 is 2+2 = 4.

2. Fill in the blank: for f to be continuous at x = a, three conditions must hold — f(a) is defined, limx→a f(x) exists, and limx→a f(x) = ___.

Continuity requires the limit to actually equal the function's value at that point, not just exist.

3. What is the derivative of f(x) = 3x² + 2x?

Power rule term by term: d/dx(3x²) = 6x, d/dx(2x) = 2, giving 6x + 2.

4. Fill in the blank: if f is differentiable at x = a, then f must also be ___ at x = a (though the reverse implication can fail, e.g. at a corner or cusp).

Differentiability is a stronger condition than continuity — every differentiable function is continuous, but not every continuous function is differentiable.

5. Differentiate y = √(x³ + 4). y′ equals:

√u = u^(1/2) differentiates to (1/(2√u))·u′; with u = x³+4, u′ = 3x², giving 3x²/(2√(x³+4)).

6. Fill in the blank: let f(x) = (2x + 1)⁵. Then f′(x) = 10(2x + 1)⁴, so f′(0) = ___.

f′(0) = 10(2·0+1)⁴ = 10(1)⁴ = 10.

7. A spherical balloon is being inflated. Which equation relates its volume V to its radius r?

A sphere's volume is V = (4/3)πr³ — the formula that must be differentiated with respect to t before any related-rates numbers go in.

8. Fill in the blank: an inverted conical tank has r = h/3 (similar triangles), so V = πh³/27. Water is pumped in at dV/dt = 10 ft³/min, giving dV/dt = (π/9)h²·dh/dt. When h = 6 ft, dh/dt ≈ ___ ft/min (to two decimal places).

dh/dt = 9(dV/dt)/(πh²) = 9(10)/(π·36) = 2.5/π ≈ 0.80 ft/min.

9. The Extreme Value Theorem guarantees an absolute maximum and minimum exist for f on [a, b] provided f is:

Continuity on a closed, bounded interval is the only requirement — not differentiability, not monotonicity, not concavity.

10. Fill in the blank: a cylindrical can must hold 500 cm³ (V = πr²h = 500), with surface area S(r) = 2πr² + 1000/r. Minimizing gives S′(r) = 4πr − 1000/r² = 0, so r³ = 250/π ≈ 79.58, and the minimizing radius is r ≈ ___ cm (to two decimal places).

r³ ≈ 79.58 gives r ≈ 4.30 cm.

11. ∫0π sin(x) dx equals:

An antiderivative is F(x) = −cos(x). F(π) − F(0) = −(−1) − (−1) = 1 + 1 = 2.

12. Fill in the blank: for ∫12 (3x²)/(x³ + 1) dx, using u = x³ + 1 (bounds u = 2 to u = 9), the integral equals ln(___) (simplify the fraction inside).

∫₂⁹ (1/u) du = ln|u| from 2 to 9 = ln9 − ln2 = ln(9/2).

13. Solve the separable differential equation dy/dx = xy with y(0) = 1. What is y in terms of x?

Separating gives dy/y = x dx, so ln|y| = x²/2 + C; since y(0) = 1, C = 0, giving y = e^(x²/2).

14. Fill in the blank: in a logistic model dP/dt = kP(1 − P/L), the constant L represents the population's ___.

L is the carrying capacity — the population level the model approaches as t → ∞, since growth slows to zero as P approaches L.

15. Revolving the region under y = x³ on [0, 1] around the x-axis gives a volume of:

V = π∫₀¹(x³)²dx = π∫₀¹x⁶dx = π[x⁷/7]₀¹ = π/7.

16. Fill in the blank: revolve the region between y = 2√x (outer) and y = x (inner) on [0, 4] around the x-axis. Since R²−r² = 4x−x², the volume is π∫₀⁴[4x−x²]dx = ___ (use "pi" for π).

π∫₀⁴[4x−x²]dx = π[2x²−x³/3]₀⁴ = π(32−64/3) = 32π/3.

17. The polar point (r, θ) = (4, π/3) converts to which rectangular coordinates?

x = r·cos(θ) = 4·(1/2) = 2, y = r·sin(θ) = 4·(√3/2) = 2√3.

18. For r(t) = ⟨t², t³⟩, what is r′(t)?

A vector-valued function differentiates componentwise: d/dt(t²) = 2t, d/dt(t³) = 3t².

19. Applying the ratio test to Σ 3ⁿ/n! gives a limit L = 0. What does this mean for the series?

The ratio test concludes absolute convergence whenever L < 1, including L = 0 — factorial growth in the denominator crushes any fixed exponential base.

20. Fill in the blank: the geometric series Σ (from n=0 to ∞) of 3(1/4)ⁿ converges to a/(1−r) = 3/(1−1/4) = ___.

3/(1−1/4) = 3/(3/4) = 4.

1. The average value of f(x) = x² − 1 on [0, 3] is:

favg = (1/3)∫₀³(x² − 1)dx = (1/3)[x³/3 − x]₀³ = (1/3)(9 − 3) = (1/3)(6) = 2 — dividing the integral's value of 6 by the interval width of 3, not leaving it undivided (6), dividing by the wrong width (3), or mishandling the −1 term (1).

2. A particle moves with velocity v(t) = t² − 2t − 3 ft/sec on [0, 4]. Answer both parts.

(a) The displacement of the particle over [0, 4] is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled over [0, 4] is ___ (as a fraction)

(a) Displacement = ∫₀⁴v(t)dt = F(4) − F(0), where F(t) = t³/3 − t² − 3t: F(4) = −20/3 and F(0) = 0, so displacement = −20/3. (b) v(t) = (t − 3)(t + 1) is negative on [0, 3) and positive on (3, 4], so distance = |F(3) − F(0)| + |F(4) − F(3)| = 9 + 7/3 = 34/3 — larger than the magnitude of the displacement because the particle reversed direction at t = 3.

3. Fill in the blank: A pump fills a pool at r(t) = 3√t gallons/minute. The amount that flows in during the first 4 minutes is ___ gallons

∫₀⁴ 3√t dt = 2t^(3/2) evaluated from 0 to 4 = 2(8) = 16 gallons — the Net Change Theorem applied directly to the given rate function.

4. Find the area between y = 8 − x² and y = 2x. Answer both parts.

(a) The intersection points of y = 8 − x² and y = 2x are at x =:

(b) Fill in the blank: the area between the curves is ___

(a) Setting 8 − x² = 2x gives x² + 2x − 8 = (x + 4)(x − 2) = 0, so x = −4 and x = 2. (b) Testing x = 0 shows the parabola sits above the line on this interval, so Area = ∫₋₄²[(8 − x²) − 2x]dx = [8x − x³/3 − x²]₋₄² = 28/3 − (−80/3) = 36.

5. Two curves cross at exactly four points on an interval. Finding the total area between them requires:

Four crossings create three subintervals between consecutive pairs, and the curve on top can differ across them — each of the three pieces needs its own top-minus-bottom integrand, not a single unsplit integral, a split matching the number of crossings themselves, or an average of the crossing locations.

6. The base region is bounded by y = √x and the x-axis on [0, 9]. Cross sections perpendicular to the x-axis are equilateral triangles. Answer both parts.

(a) A(x), the cross-sectional area at a given x, equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the volume of the solid is ___ (as a fraction with √3, e.g. "a√3/b")

(a) s(x) = √x, so an equilateral triangle cross section has area A(x) = (√3/4)s² = (√3/4)(√x)² = (√3/4)x. (b) V = ∫₀⁹(√3/4)x dx = (√3/4)[x²/2]₀⁹ = (√3/4)(81/2) = 81√3/8.

7. Fill in the blank: Revolving the region under y = x² on [0, 1] around the x-axis gives a volume of ___ (use "pi" for π)

R(x) = x², so V = π∫₀¹(x²)²dx = π∫₀¹x⁴dx = π[x⁵/5]₀¹ = π/5.

8. Revolve the region between y = x (outer) and y = x³ (inner) on [0, 1] around the x-axis. Answer both parts.

(a) The volume integral (before evaluating) is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the volume is ___ (as a fraction, use "pi" for π)

(a) R(x) = x (outer) and r(x) = x³ (inner), so the integrand is R(x)² − r(x)² = x² − x⁶ — not the unsquared radii, the subtraction in the wrong order, or an addition instead of subtraction. (b) V = π∫₀¹[x² − x⁶]dx = π[x³/3 − x⁷/7]₀¹ = π[1/3 − 1/7] = 4π/21.

9. Which integral computes the arc length of y = x² + 1 on [1, 4]?

f′(x) = 2x, so [f′(x)]² = 4x², and the arc length integrand is √(1 + 4x²) — not with the derivative left unsquared (√(1 + 2x)), the square root dropped entirely (1 + 4x²), or the derivative's coefficient of 2 forgotten (√(1 + x²)).

10. A particle's position is x(t) = 9t², y(t) = 6t³ for t in [0, 1]. Answer both parts.

(a) The particle's speed, √([x′(t)]² + [y′(t)]²), simplifies to:

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled over [0, 1] is ___ (exact form)

(a) x′(t) = 18t and y′(t) = 18t², so speed = √(324t² + 324t⁴) = √(324t²(1 + t²)) = 18t√(1 + t²) for t ≥ 0 — not with the wrong power of t outside the root, a missing t factor, or a doubled coefficient. (b) Distance = ∫₀¹18t√(1 + t²)dt; substituting w = 1 + t² (dw = 2t dt) gives 9∫₁²√w dw = 6[w^(3/2)]₁² = 6(2√2 − 1) = 12√2 − 6.

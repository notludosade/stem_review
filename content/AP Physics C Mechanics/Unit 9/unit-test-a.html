1. Classical mechanics is generally an accurate approximation for:

Quantum effects dominate microscopic systems, and relativistic corrections matter near light speed; ordinary macroscopic motion is the classical regime.

2. In an inertial reference frame, a force-free particle:

This is Newton's first law and the defining property of an inertial frame.

3. Fill in the blank: a bob constrained to a fixed-length planar pendulum has ___ degree of freedom.

The constraint x² + y² = ℓ² leaves only one independent coordinate, usually θ.

4. Why is θ preferable to separate x and y coordinates for an ideal pendulum?

x = ℓ sinθ and y = −ℓ cosθ always satisfy x² + y² = ℓ².

5. For the conservative systems studied in this unit, which expression defines the Lagrangian?

Kinetic minus potential energy is the standard conservative-system Lagrangian.

6. Fill in the blank: for m = 2 kg and k = 8 N/m, Euler–Lagrange gives ẍ + ___x = 0.

mẍ + kx = 0, so ẍ + (k/m)x = ẍ + 4x = 0.

7. The exact equation obtained from the pendulum Lagrangian is:

T = ½mℓ²θ̇² and U = mgℓ(1 − cosθ) yield mℓ²θ̈ + mgℓsinθ = 0.

8. If ∂L/∂q = 0, the Euler–Lagrange equation guarantees that:

Euler–Lagrange becomes dp_q/dt = ∂L/∂q = 0.

9. In L = ½m(ṙ² + r²θ̇²) − U(r), θ is absent. Its conserved conjugate momentum is:

p_θ = ∂L/∂θ̇ = mr²θ̇, the familiar angular momentum.

10. Fill in the blank: a particle has m = 2 kg, speed 3 m/s, and U = 5 J. For this standard system, H = T + U = ___ J.

T = ½mv² = ½(2)(9) = 9 J, so H = 9 + 5 = 14 J.

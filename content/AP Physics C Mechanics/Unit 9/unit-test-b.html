1. Which situation most clearly requires physics beyond classical mechanics?

Atomic energy levels are quantum; the other examples are well described by classical mechanics under ordinary conditions.

2. Why are apparent forces introduced in an accelerating reference frame?

Newton's familiar form applies directly in inertial frames; apparent forces compensate for a non-inertial frame's acceleration.

3. Fill in the blank: a bead constrained to move on a fixed circular wire in a plane has ___ degree of freedom.

One angle locates the bead anywhere on the circle; the radial coordinate is fixed by the constraint.

4. A generalized coordinate must be:

A generalized coordinate can be an angle, distance, or another convenient independent descriptor.

5. Which is the Euler–Lagrange equation for a generalized coordinate q?

This equation generates one equation of motion for each independent generalized coordinate.

6. For a free particle with L = ½mẋ², applying Euler–Lagrange shows that:

x is absent from L, so d(mẋ)/dt = 0 and linear momentum is conserved.

7. With the bottom chosen as U = 0, the pendulum potential energy is:

The bob rises by h = ℓ(1 − cosθ), so U = mgh = mgℓ(1 − cosθ).

8. Translation symmetry is associated with conservation of:

If shifting an isolated system leaves L unchanged, the corresponding linear momentum is conserved.

9. Fill in the blank: when q is cyclic, its conjugate ___ pq = ∂L/∂q̇ is conserved.

The quantity conjugate to a generalized coordinate is its generalized momentum.

10. Fill in the blank: a 4 kg particle moves at 2 m/s where U = 4 J. For this standard system, H = T + U = ___ J.

T = ½(4)(2²) = 8 J, so H = 8 + 4 = 12 J.

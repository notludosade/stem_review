Finding Eigenvectors

Once λ is known, its eigenvectors are the nonzero vectors in null(A−λI).

← Lesson 16 Lesson 18 → Contents Glossary →

MethodFor each eigenvalue λ, row-reduce A−λI and solve (A−λI)v=0. All solutions form the eigenspace; exclude v=0 when naming eigenvectors.
Worked ExampleFor A=[[2,1],[1,2]] and λ=3, A−3I=[[-1,1],[1,-1]], so y=x. Eigenspace span{(1,1)}. For λ=1, eigenspace span{(1,−1)}.
Why This MattersAn eigenvalue says how much; its eigenvectors say which directions experience that scaling.

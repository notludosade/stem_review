# Resources: Data Handling: CB

## Primary sources
- **Mode Analytics SQL Tutorial** (mode.com/sql-tutorial) — a clear, standard-SQL-first walkthrough of SELECT, joins, aggregation, and subqueries, used as the primary grounding for Unit 3.
- **PostgreSQL Documentation** (postgresql.org/docs) — the reference for standard SQL syntax and semantics where precision matters (join types, NULL handling, GROUP BY rules).
- **OpenIntro Statistics** (openintro.org/book/os) — a free, rigorous-but-applied statistics textbook; the primary grounding for Unit 4's descriptive statistics, distributions, correlation, confidence intervals, and regression content.
- **"How Charts Lie" by Alberto Cairo** — the standard trade reference on chart design and how visualizations mislead, grounding Unit 6.
- **Google's "Data Cleaning" and "Data Preparation" guidance** (within the Google Data Analytics Certificate curriculum, coursera.org) — practical, industry-standard framing for Unit 5's cleaning and preparation content.
- **IAPP (International Association of Privacy Professionals)** (iapp.org) — plain-language overviews of GDPR, CCPA, and data governance concepts for Unit 7.
- **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook** (bls.gov/ooh) — authoritative descriptions of data analyst, data engineer, and related role definitions and outlooks for Unit 8.

## Wisdom communities
- r/dataanalysis and r/analytics (Reddit) — practitioners discussing real workplace data problems, tool choices, and career questions.
- r/SQL (Reddit) — for query-specific questions once Unit 3's fundamentals are solid.
- Data Analyst / Business Intelligence groups on LinkedIn — useful for portfolio feedback and role-specific career questions once Unit 8 is reached.
- Local or virtual chapters of DAMA International (Data Management Association) — for those going deeper into data governance specifically.

## Gaps
- No single textbook covers spreadsheets, SQL, statistics, cleaning, visualization, ethics, and careers together — this course synthesizes across tool-specific tutorials (Mode, PostgreSQL docs), a statistics textbook (OpenIntro), and industry career resources (BLS, IAPP), since the material is inherently a synthesis of several adjacent fields.
- Spreadsheet-specific instruction (Excel vs. Google Sheets vs. others) varies by product; this course teaches the underlying concepts (functions, lookups, pivot tables) in tool-agnostic terms, noting where a specific product's naming differs.
- Data governance and privacy regulation content is necessarily a simplified overview — actual compliance work requires consulting current regulation text and, in a real workplace, legal counsel.

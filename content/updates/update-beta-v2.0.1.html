STEM+ · Major Update · Oct 5, 2026

Update BETA v2.0.1

A major step toward making STEM+ an open learning system that can guide what you learn, show what you know, and give you somewhere meaningful to apply it.

← All patch notes Your Dashboard →

Release summary STEM+ is now open to explore without an account, easier to navigate, much larger in practice coverage, more transparent about content quality, and able to turn saved work into unit-by-unit mastery and Pathway readiness.

What changed

Open

The full curriculum is open to everyone

Courses, tests, Problem Sets, Applications, projects, and sandboxes can now be explored without an account. Signing in is only required when you want STEM+ to save your work. New visitors see a clear introduction to the learning journey, while signed-in students land on their Dashboard.

Navigate

The site now follows how learning actually happens

The top menu is organized into Learn, Practice, Build, and Goals. Courses live under Learn; Problem Sets and Timed Mastery live under Practice; sandboxes, Applications, and capstones live under Build; and Pathways and personal plans live under Goals. Students can also choose an active Track that guides Continue Learning on the Dashboard.

Practice

Problem Sets expanded to 4,800 questions

Every published Problem Set grew from 60 to 240 questions across 20 courses. Topic filters, explanations, saved progress, and Timed Mastery remain in place, and existing progress carries over.

20 problem sets · 240 questions each · 4,800 total

Trust

Reporting and content review are now visible

Every page includes a Report a problem action, and every Problem Set explanation can report its specific question. Course pages show whether content is AI generated, human reviewed, verified, in draft, or needs review, while automated checks now cover the complete site on every update.

Mastery

Progress now means more than completion

Each course unit is now a tracked skill with six states: Unseen, Learning, Practiced, Proficient, Mastered, and Applied. Signed-in students see these states on course pages and in a Dashboard mastery summary. Every Pathway now includes a short diagnostic built from checked Problem Set questions; its results contribute evidence up to Proficient, calculate Pathway readiness, and recommend where to start without replacing unit tests or course exams.

By the numbers

  • 40 courses across five subject areas.
  • 1,067 lessons with interactive examples, checks, and assessments.
  • 4,800 practice questions across 20 Problem Sets.
  • 10 Pathways, each ending in a capstone project.
  • 9 Applications connecting course ideas to real-world problems.

What stays the same

Nothing was removed from the curriculum. Existing saved progress still works, every course remains self-paced, and mastery is calculated from work already stored in this browser. Unit tests and course exams still decide Mastered status; diagnostics and practice help identify readiness without bypassing those assessments.

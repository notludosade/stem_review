Algebra/Geometry Fundamentals Review

A refresher on the algebra and geometry Precalculus assumes — linear and quadratic equations, factoring, exponents, similarity, and the Pythagorean theorem. No prerequisite — this is the most basic, entry-level course on the math side of the site.

Lesson 1

The addition/subtraction and multiplication/division properties of equality, and the two special cases (no solution, all real numbers) they can reveal.

 Lesson 2

Solving like an equation, plus the one rule that flips the sign — and reading open vs. closed circles on a number-line graph.

 Lesson 3

Systems of Two Linear Equations

Finding where two lines meet with substitution or elimination, and what it means for a system to have no solution or infinitely many.

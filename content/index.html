About

STEM+ is a free, self-paced STEM curriculum: interactive courses in math and science for those who are ambitious. Subjects are organized by topic (Math, Technology & Computer Science, Science, Engineering & Physics, Advanced+ Courses); Tracks cut across them with curated Pathways, capstone Projects, and real-world Applications. Problem Sets and the Code Sandbox exist for practice, not just reading — every course pairs its lessons with unit tests, a gated course exam, and a progress report so you know where you actually stand.

1. A point charge of +2 μC creates an electric field at a distance of 0.15 m. What is the field's magnitude? (k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C²)

E = kQ/r² = (8.99×10⁹)(2×10⁻⁶)/(0.15)² = 17,980/0.0225 ≈ 7.99×10⁵ N/C.

2. A uniform field E = 800 N/C strikes a flat surface of area 0.05 m² at 45° to the surface's normal. What is the flux through the surface?

Φ_E = EA cos θ = (800)(0.05)(cos 45°) = (40)(0.7071) ≈ 28.3 N·m²/C.

3. Fill in the blank: a uniformly charged sphere carries total charge 12 μC. At r = R/3 (inside the sphere), the enclosed charge Q_enc = Q(r/R)³ = ___ μC (2 decimal places).

Q_enc = Q(r/R)³ = 12(1/3)³ = 12/27 ≈ 0.44 μC.

4. A point charge of 2 μC sits at the origin. What is the potential at r = 0.25 m? (k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C²)

V = kQ/r = (8.99×10⁹)(2×10⁻⁶)/(0.25) = 17,980/0.25 = 71,920 V.

5. A ring of radius 0.4 m carries total charge 8 μC. What is the potential on its axis at a point x = 0.3 m from the ring's center? (r = √(x²+R²) = 0.5 m)

V = kQ/r = (8.99×10⁹)(8×10⁻⁶)/(0.5) = 71,920/0.5 = 143,840 V — using r, not x, since every dq on the ring is that same distance from the axial point.

6. Fill in the blank: an isolated conductor in electrostatic equilibrium has one point on its surface measured at V = 850 V. At any other point on that same conductor's surface, V = ___ V.

A conductor in equilibrium is a single equipotential object — every point on its surface (or interior) shares the same V.

7. A parallel-plate capacitor has plate area 0.03 m² and separation 0.002 m. What is its capacitance? (ε₀ = 8.85×10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²))

C = ε₀A/d = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(0.03)/(0.002) = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(15) ≈ 1.33×10⁻¹⁰ F ≈ 133 pF.

8. Fill in the blank: a 6 μF capacitor is charged to 80 V. The energy stored is ___ J (2 decimal places).

U = ½CV² = ½(6×10⁻⁶)(80)² = ½(6×10⁻⁶)(6400) = 0.0192 ≈ 0.02 J.

9. A dielectric with κ = 5 is inserted into an isolated capacitor (battery disconnected first, Q fixed), which had C₀ = 4 pF and V₀ = 200 V. What is the new voltage?

With Q fixed, C rises to κC₀, so V = Q/C falls to V₀/κ = 200/5 = 40 V.

10. A copper wire (n ≈ 8.5×10²⁸ electrons/m³) carries I = 3 A through a cross-section A = 2×10⁻⁶ m² (q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C per electron). What is the drift velocity?

v_d = I/(nqA) = 3/[(8.5×10²⁸)(1.6×10⁻¹⁹)(2×10⁻⁶)] = 3/27,200 ≈ 1.10×10⁻⁴ m/s.

11. Fill in the blank: a tungsten wire (ρ = 5.6×10⁻⁸ Ω·m) is 2 m long with cross-sectional area 4×10⁻⁷ m². Its resistance is ___ Ω (2 decimal places).

R = ρL/A = (5.6×10⁻⁸)(2)/(4×10⁻⁷) = 1.12×10⁻⁷/4×10⁻⁷ = 0.28 Ω.

12. Three resistors, each 4 Ω, are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:

1/R_eq = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 3/4, so R_eq = 4/3 ≈ 1.33 Ω.

13. In applying Kirchhoff's loop rule, traversing a resistor in the direction of the assumed current contributes to the loop-rule sum as:

Moving through a resistor in the direction current is assumed to flow is a drop in potential, contributing −IR to Σ(voltage changes) = 0.

14. Fill in the blank: a series RC circuit has R = 5000 Ω and C = 400 μF. Its time constant is τ = ___ s.

τ = RC = (5000)(400×10⁻⁶) = 2 s.

15. A capacitor charged to V₀ = 20 V discharges through a resistor, with τ = 4 s for the circuit. At t = 4 s (= τ), the voltage is approximately:

V(τ) = V₀e⁻¹ = 20(0.3679) ≈ 7.36 V.

16. An electron (q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C, m = 9.11×10⁻³¹ kg) moves at v = 3×10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a field B = 0.4 T. What is its orbit radius?

r = mv/(qB) = (9.11×10⁻³¹)(3×10⁶)/[(1.6×10⁻¹⁹)(0.4)] = 2.733×10⁻²⁴/6.4×10⁻²⁰ ≈ 4.27×10⁻⁵ m.

17. A rectangular loop 0.08 m × 0.04 m (area = 0.0032 m²) carries I = 3 A and sits with its plane parallel to a field B = 0.5 T (θ = 90°, maximum-torque orientation). What is the torque on the loop?

τ = IAB sin θ = (3)(0.0032)(0.5)(sin 90°) = 0.0048 N·m.

18. Fill in the blank: an ideal solenoid has n = 1200 turns/m carrying I = 5 A. The interior field is B ≈ ___ ×10⁻³ T (2 decimal places).

B = μ₀nI = (4π×10⁻⁷)(1200)(5) ≈ 7.54×10⁻³ T.

19. A conducting rod of length 0.5 m slides at 4 m/s perpendicular to a uniform 0.6 T field, along frictionless rails. What is the motional EMF?

EMF = BLv = (0.6)(0.5)(4) = 1.2 V.

20. Fill in the blank: an RL circuit has L = 0.3 H and R = 60 Ω. Its time constant τ is ___ ms.

τ = L/R = 0.3/60 = 0.005 s = 5 ms.

21. Maxwell's Ampère–Maxwell law adds the displacement current term μ₀ε₀ dΦ_E/dt to account for:

Without the displacement current term, a surface cutting a charging capacitor's wire versus one passing between its plates give contradictory answers for the same loop; the added term supplies the "missing" current wherever changing electric flux crosses the surface instead.

1. Newton's second law with Hooke's restoring force (mx″ = −kx) rearranges to:

Dividing mx″ = −kx by m and setting ω² = k/m gives x″ + ω²x = 0 — the plus sign is what produces oscillation.

2. Fill in the blank: for m = 2 kg and k = 32 N/m, ω = √(k/m) = ___ rad/s

ω = √(32/2) = √16 = 4.

3. A spring-mass system oscillates with a decaying (not growing or constant) envelope when:

A positive damping rate less than ω₀ gives complex roots with a negative real part — decaying oscillation, the underdamped case.

4. The boundary case between oscillating and non-oscillating damped motion is:

β = ω₀ makes the discriminant exactly zero — a repeated root, sitting exactly between the complex-root and real-distinct-root cases.

5. Fill in the blank: in the undamped forced case x″ + ω₀²x = f₀cos(ωt), the response amplitude grows without bound exactly when ω equals ___

When the driving frequency matches the natural frequency ω₀, the forcing term duplicates the homogeneous solution, triggering resonance.

6. A 1 kg mass on a spring with k = 9 N/m starts at x(0) = 2, released from rest. Answer both parts:

(a) ω equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the period T = 2π/ω = ___

(a) ω = √(9/1) = 3. (b) T = 2π/3.

7. A damped spring has β = 4, ω₀ = 5. Answer both parts:

(a) This system is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the damped frequency ω_d = √(ω₀² − β²) = ___

(a) β = 4 < ω₀ = 5, so underdamped. (b) ω_d = √(25 − 16) = √9 = 3.

8. A critically damped system has β = ω₀ = 4, x(0) = 5, x′(0) = 0. Answer both parts:

(a) The general solution form is:

(b) Fill in the blank: applying the initial conditions gives C₂ = ___

(a) A repeated root r = −4 gives x(t) = (C₁ + C₂t)e^(−4t). (b) x(0) = C₁ = 5. x′(0) = C₂ − 4C₁ = 0, so C₂ = 4(5) = 20.

9. For x″ + 16x = 18cos(2t) (so ω₀ = 4, ω = 2). Answer both parts:

(a) Since ω ≠ ω₀, this is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the steady-state response is x_p(t) = ___·cos(2t)

(a) ω = 2 differs from ω₀ = 4, so the response stays bounded. (b) x_p(t) = [f₀/(ω₀²−ω²)]cos(ωt) = [18/(16−4)]cos(2t) = 1.5cos(2t).

10. A damped forced system has ω₀ = 5, β = 2, f₀ = 20, driven exactly at ω = 5. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: M(5) = f₀/√[(ω₀²−ω²)² + (2βω)²] = ___

(b) Since damping is present, the amplitude at this driving frequency is:

(a) At ω = ω₀ = 5, the first term vanishes, leaving M(5) = 20/√(0 + (2·2·5)²) = 20/20 = 1. (b) Any positive damping keeps the denominator away from zero, so the amplitude stays finite even exactly at resonance.

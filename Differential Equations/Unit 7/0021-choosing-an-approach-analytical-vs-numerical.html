Choosing an Approach — Analytical vs. Numerical

Classify first, then choose exact formulas, qualitative reasoning, numerical approximation—or a combination.

← Lesson 20 Contents Course Exam → Glossary →

Decision FrameworkIdentify order, linearity, coefficients, and initial/boundary data. Try structure-matched analytical tools. Use phase/slope analysis for behavior. Use numerical methods when exact forms are unavailable or impractical—and validate with step refinement and known invariants.
ExamplesSeparable growth: integrate exactly. Constant-coefficient second order: characteristic roots. Discontinuous forcing with an IVP: Laplace transform. Nonlinear coupled system: equilibria plus numerical integration.
Why This MattersA method is useful only when its assumptions match the equation and its error is understood.

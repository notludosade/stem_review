Slope Fields and Qualitative Behavior

A grid of tiny tangent segments shows solution behavior before any formula is found.

← Lesson 19 Lesson 21 → Contents Glossary →

Slope FieldAt each point (t,y), draw a short segment with slope f(t,y). Solution curves follow these local directions without crossing when uniqueness conditions hold.
Autonomous EquilibriaFor y′=f(y), equilibria solve f(y)=0. Sign changes show stability: arrows pointing toward an equilibrium make it stable; arrows pointing away make it unstable.
Why This MattersQualitative analysis reveals equilibria, growth, decay, and long-term limits even when exact integration fails.
Try It — Logistic y′=y(1−y/K)

Check your recall

1. Equilibria of y′=f(y) satisfy:

At equilibrium, y stays constant, so y′=0.

2. For y′=y(1−y/5), y=5 is:

Below 5 solutions rise; above 5 they fall.

3. Logistic y′=y(1−y/K) has how many equilibria when K>0?

y=0 and y=K.

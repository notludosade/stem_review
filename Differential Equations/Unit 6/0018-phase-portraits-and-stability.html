Phase Portraits and Stability

Eigenvalues turn a matrix system into a geometric picture of trajectories over time.

ClassificationFor X′=AX: both real parts negative gives a sink; both positive gives a source; opposite signs give a saddle; nonzero imaginary parts produce rotation, with real part controlling inward or outward spiraling.
Trace–Determinant PlaneFor 2×2 A, λ²−Tλ+D=0. D<0 means saddle. D>0 and T<0 favors stability; T>0 favors instability. Discriminant T²−4D separates nodes from spirals.
Why This MattersStability can often be read without solving every initial-value problem.

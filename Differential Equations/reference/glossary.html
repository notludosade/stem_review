Analytical Methods

Order

The highest derivative appearing in a differential equation.

Integrating Factor

A multiplier turning a first-order linear ODE into a product derivative.

Characteristic Equation

Polynomial whose roots determine constant-coefficient solution form.

Laplace Transform

Integral transform converting differentiation and initial data into algebra.

Systems and Numerical Methods

Phase Portrait

Picture of system trajectories in state space.

Equilibrium

A constant solution where the derivative vanishes.

Euler's Method

First-order numerical method using repeated tangent-line steps.

Slope Field

Grid of short segments showing local solution slopes.

Unit 7 Test — Version B

Putting It Together. 10 questions covering every lesson in this unit. Score 90% or higher to clear this unit.

← Course Contents ← Version A

1. Fill in the blank: deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and time to restore service together make up the ___ metrics.

These four measures are the shared standard this unit uses to evaluate any pipeline design.

2. Why might a small startup's pipeline run full Continuous Deployment with no release gate, while a regulated fintech backend keeps one?

Trust in the pipeline and regulatory constraints drive the release gate decision, not the underlying technology.

3. A high-traffic consumer application's observability setup genuinely uses logs, metrics, and traces together. Why?

The three signals stay complementary regardless of scale — a high-traffic app just needs all three more visibly.

4. What determines whether a canary release strategy is actually safe for a given team to adopt?

Canary's advantage depends entirely on monitoring good enough to actually catch a problem in a small traffic slice.

5. Fill in the blank: a recurring, seemingly-reasonable habit that keeps causing the same kind of damage is called an ___.

An anti-pattern is defined by its recurrence and surface reasonableness, not by being a single obvious mistake.

6. A team's on-call rotation still restarts the same kind of failed job by hand every week, instead of ever fixing why it fails. Answer both parts:

(a) This repeated, low-value manual work is called...

(b) Left unaddressed as the system grows, this kind of work tends to...

Toil doesn't resolve itself — it scales with the system until someone automates the root cause away.

7. A team's deploy pipeline is fully automated end to end, except a human still has to manually approve every release through a slow ticket queue. What does this recreate?

The wall is an organizational handoff — a slow manual approval step is that same handoff, regardless of how automated everything around it is.

8. What does a blameless postmortem focus on instead of identifying which individual to blame?

A blameless postmortem examines process and system causes, not individual fault.

9. According to this unit's closing lesson, what was the actual goal of this entire course?

This closes the loop with Unit 1, Lesson 2 — culture is the actual point, and tools exist to support it.

10. Fill in the blank: canary releases and a solid rollback plan both work to shrink the DORA metric called ___ — how often a deployed change actually causes a failure.

Change failure rate is one of the four DORA metrics, directly improved by safer deployment strategies.

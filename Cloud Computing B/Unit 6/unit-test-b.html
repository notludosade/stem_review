1. A team needs to know whether p99 latency has been trending upward over the last month, cheaply and without storing every individual request. Which signal fits best?

Metrics are the cheap, sampled signal built for exactly this kind of trend question.

2. Fill in the blank: following a single request's entire path across multiple services, stitched into one timeline built from spans, is called a ___.

A trace is the signal that follows one request across every service it touches.

3. Why does an alert that fires constantly for conditions nobody can act on eventually make a system less safe, not more?

Alert fatigue is what erodes the safety benefit alerting is supposed to provide in the first place.

4. Fill in the blank: rotating page responsibility among a defined group of engineers on a set cadence, instead of one person always being on the hook, is the purpose of an ___.

An on-call rotation exists specifically to share the burden of being paged evenly.

5. A system is degraded in a way that's affecting real users. What is the formally declared event that kicks off a coordinated response called?

An incident is the formal declaration that replaces uncoordinated, independent investigation.

6. An error rate metric spikes at 9:41 a.m. Answer both parts:

(a) Finding the actual stack trace behind one failing request requires...

(b) If the request passes through four services, isolating which one is actually failing requires...

A metric flags that something's wrong; a log and a trace are what actually explain where and why.

7. A trace shows a request spending 400ms in an authentication service and 1200ms in a payments service. Answer both parts:

(a) Each of those two per-service timing records is called a...

(b) Tracing every single request this way at full production volume is generally...

Spans make up a trace, and full tracing at scale is expensive enough that sampling is standard practice.

8. A team notices their on-call engineers have started silencing notifications on their phones overnight. Answer both parts:

(a) This behavior is a symptom of...

(b) Fixing it generally requires...

Silencing notifications is a classic sign of alert fatigue, fixed by reducing noise and non-actionable alerts.

9. A ten-person team sets up a weekly rotation so a different engineer is primarily on-call each week. Answer both parts:

(a) This structure exists mainly to...

(b) Compared to one engineer being permanently on-call forever, this rotation is...

Rotation is specifically about distributing the on-call burden across a team, not concentrating it.

10. During a declared incident, one engineer is named to track status, decide when to escalate, and call the incident resolved. Answer both parts:

(a) This role is called the...

(b) This person is...

The incident commander's job is coordination, which is a distinct role from actually debugging the fix.

1. What is the key difference between a log and a metric?

Logs capture detailed individual events; metrics capture cheap, sampled trends over time.

2. Fill in the blank: within a trace, the record of how long one request spent inside one particular service is called a ___.

A trace is built from spans, one per service the request passed through.

3. What is alert fatigue?

Alert fatigue erodes trust in alerts, the same way a flaky test erodes trust in a red build.

4. Fill in the blank: a schedule where responsibility for responding to pages rotates among a defined group of engineers is called an ___.

An on-call rotation spreads the burden of being paged evenly across a team.

5. What is the incident commander's role during an incident?

The incident commander coordinates; someone else may well be the one actually debugging.

6. A metrics dashboard shows checkout's error rate spike at 2:03 p.m. Answer both parts:

(a) Confirming the exact error message from one specific failed request requires...

(b) If checkout calls two other services to complete an order, finding which one caused the delay requires...

Each signal answers a different question: metrics show the trend, logs show one event's detail, traces show cross-service flow.

7. A request travels through three microservices before completing. Answer both parts:

(a) The trace following this request end to end is built out of individual...

(b) Capturing a full trace for every single request at real production scale is typically...

Traces are built from spans and are typically sampled due to their cost at scale.

8. A CPU-usage alert pages someone at 3 a.m. every night, with no clear next step attached. Answer both parts:

(a) This alert is best described as...

(b) The right fix here is to...

A non-actionable alert is a source of fatigue, not safety, regardless of how well-tuned its threshold is.

9. A team gives one engineer permanent, 24/7 responsibility for every page, with no rotation ever. Answer both parts:

(a) This arrangement...

(b) A proper on-call rotation instead...

A rotation exists specifically to spread the burden of being paged, not concentrate it on one person.

10. An error spikes, and three engineers separately start investigating without declaring an incident or naming anyone in charge. Answer both parts:

(a) This is risky because...

(b) Declaring an incident and naming an incident commander would instead...

A named incident commander is what turns a scramble into a coordinated response.

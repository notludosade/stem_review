Multivariable Calculus Glossary

← Contents

Gradient

Vector of partial derivatives.

Jacobian

Local derivative matrix or coordinate area scale.

Line integral

Integral along a curve.

Surface integral

Integral over a parametrized surface.

Divergence

Local net outward flow.

Curl

Local rotation.

Green's Theorem

Planar boundary circulation equals interior curl.

Stokes' Theorem

Boundary circulation equals surface curl flux.

Divergence Theorem

Closed flux equals volume divergence.

Lagrange multiplier

Scalar relating objective and constraint gradients.

Derivatives and Coordinates

Gradient

Vector of partial derivatives pointing toward steepest increase.

Directional Derivative

The dot product of a gradient with a unit direction.

Jacobian

Derivative matrix whose determinant measures local coordinate scaling.

Lagrange Multiplier

Scalar λ in ∇f=λ∇g at a regular constrained extremum.

Vector Calculus

Line Integral

An integral accumulating a scalar quantity or vector-field work along a curve.

Surface Integral

An integral over a parametrized surface using its local area scale.

Divergence

Scalar measuring local net outward flow.

Curl

Vector measuring local rotation.

Green's Theorem

Planar boundary circulation equals accumulated scalar curl inside.

Stokes' Theorem

Boundary circulation equals curl flux through a spanning surface.

Divergence Theorem

Outward flux through a closed surface equals integrated divergence inside.

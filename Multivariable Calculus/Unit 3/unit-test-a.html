1. For a unit vector u, the directional derivative Duf equals:

The directional derivative is the dot product of the gradient with a unit direction vector.

2. Fill in the blank: at a point where ∇f = ⟨3, 4⟩, the maximum possible directional derivative in any direction is ___

The maximum of D_u f over all unit directions is |∇f| = √(9+16) = √25 = 5.

3. The tangent plane to z = f(x,y) at (a,b) is given by:

The tangent plane mirrors the single-variable tangent line, with one linear correction term per partial derivative.

4. At a critical point, if D = fxxfyy − (fxy)² < 0, the point is:

D < 0 always signals a saddle point — curvature that disagrees by direction — regardless of the sign of f_xx.

5. A critical point of f(x,y) is a point where:

Just like single-variable calculus, a critical point is a necessary (not sufficient) condition for a local extremum: the gradient must vanish or fail to exist.

6. For f(x,y) = x² + y² at (3, 4), in the direction u = ⟨1, 0⟩, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: ∇f(3,4) = ___ (format: <a,b>)

(b) Fill in the blank: Duf(3,4) = ___

(a) ∇f = ⟨2x,2y⟩, so ∇f(3,4) = ⟨6,8⟩. (b) D_u f = ⟨6,8⟩ · ⟨1,0⟩ = 6 — exactly f_x(3,4), since u is the x-direction.

7. For f(x,y) = x²y at (a,b) = (2, 1), answer both parts:

(a) The linearization L(x,y) at (2,1) is:

(b) Fill in the blank: L(2.1, 1.2) = ___

(a) f(2,1)=4, f_x=2xy=4, f_y=x²=4, so L(x,y) = 4 + 4(x−2) + 4(y−1). (b) L(2.1,1.2) = 4 + 4(0.1) + 4(0.2) = 4 + 0.4 + 0.8 = 5.2.

8. For f(x,y) = x² − y² at the critical point (0,0), answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: D = fxxfyy − (fxy)² = ___

(b) The point (0,0) is:

(a) f_xx = 2, f_yy = -2, f_xy = 0, so D = (2)(-2) - 0 = -4. (b) D < 0 means a saddle point — f increases along the x-axis and decreases along the y-axis through the origin.

9. For f(x,y) = x² + y² at (1, 0), answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: ∇f(1,0) = ___ (format: <a,b>)

(b) The directional derivative Duf(1,0) is exactly 0 for u equal to:

(a) ∇f = ⟨2x,2y⟩, so ∇f(1,0) = ⟨2,0⟩. (b) D_u f = 0 exactly when u ⊥ ∇f; ⟨0,1⟩ · ⟨2,0⟩ = 0, matching the level curve's tangent direction there.

10. For f(x,y) = x² + y² at (1,2), answer both parts:

(a) A normal vector to the tangent plane at (1,2) is:

(b) Fill in the blank: z = f(a,b) + fx(a,b)(x−a) + fy(a,b)(y−b) at (1,2) gives z-intercept-style constant term f(1,2) = ___

(a) The normal is ⟨f_x, f_y, -1⟩ = ⟨2,4,-1⟩ (any nonzero scalar multiple, like ⟨-2,-4,1⟩, is equally valid). (b) f(1,2) = 1 + 4 = 5.

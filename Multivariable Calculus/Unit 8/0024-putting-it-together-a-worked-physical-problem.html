Putting It Together — A Worked Physical Problem

A source field through a sphere connects vector fields, divergence, triple integrals, and geometry.

← Lesson 23 Contents Course Exam → Glossary →

ProblemFind outward flux of F=⟨x,y,z⟩ through sphere x²+y²+z²=R².
SynthesisDirectly, F·n=R and area is 4πR², giving 4πR³. By Divergence Theorem, ∇·F=3 and sphere volume is 4πR³/3, again giving 4πR³.
Why This MattersChoosing the representation is the real skill: geometry can replace algebra, and an integral theorem can replace a harder integral.

Curl and Divergence, Physically

Divergence detects sources and sinks; curl detects local spinning.

← Lesson 21 Lesson 23 → Contents Glossary →

DefinitionsFor F=⟨P,Q,R⟩, ∇·F=Px+Qy+Rz. Its curl is ∇×F=⟨Ry−Qz, Pz−Rx, Qx−Py⟩.
ExamplesF=⟨x,y⟩ has divergence 2 and curl 0: flow expands without spinning. F=⟨−y,x⟩ has divergence 0 and scalar curl 2: flow spins without expanding.
Why This MattersFluid flow, electromagnetism, and the integral theorems turn these derivatives into measurable flux and circulation.

2. For F=⟨−y,x⟩, divergence is ___.

3. Curl measures:

Curl points along the local axis of rotation.

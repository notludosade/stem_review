1. For r=⟨u,v,0⟩, |rᵤ×rᵥ| = ___.

Cross product is k.

2. Closed-loop circulation of a conservative field is ___.

Potential returns to its start value.

3. For F=⟨−y,x,0⟩, div F = ___.

All matching partials vanish.

4. Boundary orientation and normal follow the ___.

Right-hand rule links orientations.

5. Divergence is integrated over a ___.

It is a triple integral over enclosed volume.

6. Parametrized surface formula.

Partial derivatives are tangent vectors.

7. P=y,Q=−x.

Qx−Py=−1−1=−2.

8. F=⟨2x,2y,2z⟩.

6·4π/3=8π.

9. Stokes connects two integrals.

Boundary line circulation equals surface curl flux.

10. Reverse orientation.

Orientation reversal multiplies oriented integral by −1.

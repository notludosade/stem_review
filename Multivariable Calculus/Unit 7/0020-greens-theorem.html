Green's Theorem

Circulation around a plane curve equals accumulated rotation inside it.

← Lesson 19 Lesson 21 → Contents Glossary →

Green's TheoremFor positively oriented boundary C of region D, ∮C P dx + Q dy = ∬D(Qx − Py) dA.
Worked ExampleFor F=⟨−y,x⟩ around a unit circle, Qx−Py=1−(−1)=2. Therefore circulation is ∬D2 dA=2π.
Why This MattersA difficult boundary integral can become an easy area integral—and it previews Stokes' Theorem.

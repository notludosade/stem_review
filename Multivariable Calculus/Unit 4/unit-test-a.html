1. For f(x,y) ≥ 0 on R, ∬R f dA represents:

A nonnegative double integral is the direct 3D analog of a nonnegative single integral being an area — here it's a volume.

2. Fill in the blank: ∬R (2x + y) dA over R = [0,1] × [0,2] equals ___

∫₀¹∫₀² (2x+y) dy dx = ∫₀¹ [2xy + y²/2]₀² dx = ∫₀¹ (4x + 2) dx = [2x² + 2x]₀¹ = 2 + 2 = 4.

3. Fubini's theorem states that for continuous f on a rectangle R:

For continuous f on a rectangle, the order of integration is a free choice — both orders compute the same double integral.

4. A Type II region is described as:

Type II swaps the roles of x and y from Type I: constant bounds on y, curve bounds on x.

5. If f(x,y,z) = 1 everywhere on solid E, ∭E f dV equals:

Integrating the constant 1 over a solid sums up ΔV over every small box — exactly the region's volume.

6. For f(x,y) = xy² over R = [0,1] × [0,3], answer both parts:

(a) ∫₀³ xy² dy equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: ∬R xy² dA = ___

(a) ∫₀³ xy² dy = x[y³/3]₀³ = x(9) = 9x. (b) ∫₀¹ 9x dx = 9/2 = 4.5.

7. The region D is bounded by y = x² and y = 2x. Answer both parts:

(a) These curves intersect at:

(b) Fill in the blank: for D = {0 ≤ x ≤ 2, x² ≤ y ≤ 2x}, the area ∬D 1 dA = ___

(a) x² = 2x gives x(x−2) = 0, so x = 0 or x = 2. (b) ∫₀²(2x − x²)dx = [x² − x³/3]₀² = 4 − 8/3 = 4/3.

8. For D = {0 ≤ x ≤ 2, x² ≤ y ≤ 2x}, answer both parts:

(a) ∬D y dA is set up as:

(b) Fill in the blank: this double integral equals ___

(a) The lower bound must be listed first for the inner integral. (b) ∫x²2x y dy = 2x² − x⁴/2, and ∫₀²(2x² − x⁴/2)dx = 16/3 − 16/5 = 32/15 ≈ 2.133.

9. E is the solid under z = 9 − x² − y², above the xy-plane. Answer both parts:

(a) The innermost integral's bounds for z are:

(b) Fill in the blank: the shadow region D (where 9 − x² − y² ≥ 0) is a disk of radius ___

(a) The solid runs from the xy-plane up to the paraboloid. (b) 9 − x² − y² ≥ 0 means x² + y² ≤ 9, a disk of radius 3.

10. For f(x,y,z) = x²yz over the box [0,2] × [0,1] × [0,3], answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: ∫₀² x² dx = ___

(b) Fill in the blank: ∭ x²yz dV = ___

(a) ∫₀² x² dx = [x³/3]₀² = 8/3. (b) Since the integrand and box both separate: (8/3)(∫₀¹y dy)(∫₀³z dz) = (8/3)(1/2)(9/2) = 6.

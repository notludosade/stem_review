1. In polar coordinates, the double integral area element dA equals:

The extra factor of r accounts for arc length growing with radius, even for a fixed Δθ.

2. Fill in the blank: the area of a disk of radius 5, computed as ∫₀^(2π)∫₀⁵ r dr dθ, equals ___ (use "pi" for π)

∫₀⁵ r dr = 25/2, then ∫₀^(2π) 25/2 dθ = 25π — matching πr² = π(25).

3. In cylindrical coordinates, the volume element dV equals:

Cylindrical reuses the plane polar area element and simply multiplies by dz.

4. In spherical coordinates, the volume element dV equals:

The ρ² sin φ factor is the spherical Jacobian, vanishing at the poles (φ = 0, π) and largest at the equator.

5. For x = x(u,v), y = y(u,v), the Jacobian J = ∂(x,y)/∂(u,v) is:

The Jacobian is the determinant of the matrix of first partial derivatives.

6. The region D is the right half of the disk x² + y² ≤ 9 (where x ≥ 0). Answer both parts:

(a) In polar coordinates, D is described by:

(b) Fill in the blank: ∬D 1 dA (the area of D) = ___

(a) The right half of the disk needs the full radius 0 to 3, but only angles pointing rightward, -π/2 to π/2. (b) This is half of π(3²) = 9π, so the area is 9π/2 = 4.5π.

7. Computing the volume of a solid ball of radius 2 via ∭ ρ² sin φ dρ dθ dφ, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: ∫₀^π sin φ dφ = ___

(b) Fill in the blank: the total volume equals ___

(a) ∫₀^π sin φ dφ = [-cos φ]₀^π = 1-(-1) = 2. (b) V = (2π)(2)(∫₀²ρ²dρ) = (2π)(2)(8/3) = 32π/3.

8. E is the solid cylinder x² + y² ≤ 4, 0 ≤ z ≤ 5. Answer both parts:

(a) The volume, in cylindrical coordinates, is set up as:

(b) Fill in the blank: this volume equals ___

(a) The r factor from the polar area element is required even though the solid is a plain cylinder. (b) V = (base area)(height) = π(2²)(5) = 20π, matching (2π)(2²/2)(5).

9. For T(u,v) = (2u+v, u−3v), answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: J = ___

(b) Fill in the blank: |J| = ___

(a) ∂x/∂u=2, ∂x/∂v=1, ∂y/∂u=1, ∂y/∂v=-3, so J = (2)(-3) - (1)(1) = -7. (b) |J| = 7.

10. Using T(u,v) = (2u+v, u−3v) (|J| = 7) to map the unit square S = [0,1] × [0,1] to a region R, answer both parts:

(a) The area of R, ∬R 1 dx dy, equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the area of R = ___

(a) The general substitution formula, with f = 1. (b) Since |J| = 7 is constant, area of R = 7 × (area of S) = 7 × 1 = 7.

1. Converting a double integral to polar coordinates, dA is replaced with:

The factor of r is required because arc length at radius r grows with r for the same angular width.

2. Fill in the blank: the area of a disk of radius 4, computed as ∫₀^(2π)∫₀⁴ r dr dθ, equals ___ (use "pi" for π)

∫₀⁴ r dr = 8, then ∫₀^(2π) 8 dθ = 16π — matching πr² = π(16).

3. The cylindrical volume element dV is:

z carries over unchanged, and x,y contribute the usual polar area element r dr dθ.

4. The spherical volume element dV is:

ρ² sin φ is largest at the equator (φ = π/2) and vanishes at the poles.

5. The Jacobian J = ∂(x,y)/∂(u,v) for x = x(u,v), y = y(u,v) is defined as:

The Jacobian is the 2×2 determinant of the transformation's partial derivatives.

6. The region D is the upper half of the disk x² + y² ≤ 16 (where y ≥ 0). Answer both parts:

(a) In polar coordinates, D is described by:

(b) Fill in the blank: ∬D 1 dA (the area of D) = ___

(a) The upper half needs the full radius but only angles 0 to π (pointing upward). (b) Half of π(4²) = 16π is 8π.

7. Computing the volume of a solid ball of radius 3 via ∭ ρ² sin φ dρ dθ dφ, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: ∫₀^π sin φ dφ = ___

(b) Fill in the blank: the total volume equals ___

(a) ∫₀^π sin φ dφ = [-cos φ]₀^π = 1-(-1) = 2. (b) V = (2π)(2)(∫₀³ρ²dρ) = (2π)(2)(9) = 36π.

8. E is the solid cylinder x² + y² ≤ 9, 0 ≤ z ≤ 4. Answer both parts:

(a) The volume, in cylindrical coordinates, is set up as:

(b) Fill in the blank: this volume equals ___

(a) The r factor from the polar area element is required. (b) V = (base area)(height) = π(3²)(4) = 36π.

9. For T(u,v) = (3u−v, u+2v), answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: J = ___

(b) Fill in the blank: |J| = ___

(a) ∂x/∂u=3, ∂x/∂v=-1, ∂y/∂u=1, ∂y/∂v=2, so J = (3)(2) - (-1)(1) = 6+1 = 7. (b) J is already positive, so |J| = 7.

10. Using T(u,v) = (3u−v, u+2v) (|J| = 7) to map the rectangle S = [0,2] × [0,1] to a region R, answer both parts:

(a) The area of R, ∬R 1 dx dy, equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the area of R = ___

(a) The general substitution formula, with f = 1. (b) |J| = 7 is constant, and the area of S is 2×1 = 2, so the area of R = 7 × 2 = 14.

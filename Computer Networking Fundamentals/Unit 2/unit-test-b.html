1. Why is circuit switching considered wasteful for a typical phone call?

Exclusive reservation for the full call duration, silence included, is the source of the waste.

2. Fill in the blank: in packet switching, data is broken into small, independently addressed chunks called ___, each of which can take its own route.

Each packet carries its own destination address and travels independently of other packets from the same message.

3. What new problem does packet switching introduce that circuit switching never had to solve?

Because packets can take independent paths, ordering and loss recovery become new problems that a dedicated circuit never faced.

4. A laptop switches from Wi-Fi to a wired connection and every website keeps working with no other changes needed. What principle does this demonstrate?

A lower-layer change stays contained to that layer, thanks to layering — the layers above never had to know or care.

5. Fill in the blank: the TCP/IP layer responsible for getting a packet from source device to destination device, addressed with IP addresses, is the ___ layer.

The Internet layer (IP) handles device-to-device delivery, distinct from the Transport layer's program-to-program delivery.

6. Comparing the OSI and TCP/IP models. Answer both parts:

(a) How many layers does the TCP/IP model have?

(b) Which OSI layers does TCP/IP's single "Application" layer combine?

TCP/IP's Application layer collapses OSI's top three layers into one, while its Link layer collapses OSI's bottom two.

7. What does decapsulation refer to?

Decapsulation is the reverse of encapsulation — unwrapping headers on arrival, one layer at a time.

8. Fill in the blank: the Protocol Data Unit (PDU) name for data wrapped in a Transport-layer header is a ___.

Segment (Transport), packet (Internet), and frame (Link) are each layer's own name for its PDU.

9. In the nesting-doll analogy for encapsulation. Answer both parts:

(a) What does the smallest, innermost doll represent?

(b) What does opening the assembled doll from the outside in represent?

The innermost doll is the original message before any headers were added; unwrapping it from the outside mirrors decapsulation's outermost-first order.

10. Fill in the blank: the structured metadata a layer adds to the front of its payload — addresses, sizes, and similar information — is called a ___.

Each layer adds its own header, formatted according to that layer's own protocol.

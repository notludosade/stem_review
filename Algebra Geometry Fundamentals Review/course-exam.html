1. Solve: 6x − 4 = 20. What is x?

Add 4 to both sides: 6x = 24. Divide by 6: x = 4.

2. Solve: −2x ≥ 10

Dividing both sides by −2 flips the inequality sign: x ≤ −5.

3. Fill in the blank: solving the system y = x + 2 and y = −x + 8 gives x = ___.

x + 2 = −x + 8 → 2x = 6 → x = 3.

4. For ax² + bx + c = 0 to be a quadratic equation, which condition is required?

If a were 0, the x² term would vanish and the equation would just be linear — a ≠ 0 is what makes it quadratic.

5. Solve by factoring: x² − 4x − 12 = 0

x² − 4x − 12 factors as (x − 6)(x + 2) = 0, since −6 and 2 multiply to −12 and add to −4.

6. Fill in the blank: for 2x² + 5x − 3 = 0, the discriminant b² − 4ac equals ___.

Discriminant = 5² − 4(2)(−3) = 25 + 24 = 49.

7. Simplify: x⁴ · x⁵

Multiplying powers with the same base adds the exponents: 4 + 5 = 9.

8. Add: (2x² + 3x − 5) + (x² − 4x + 2)

Combine like terms: (2x² + x²) + (3x − 4x) + (−5 + 2) = 3x² − x − 3.

9. Fill in the blank: (12x³ − 6x²) ÷ 6x simplifies to ___.

12x³/6x = 2x², and −6x²/6x = −x, giving 2x² − x.

10. What is the GCF of 16x³ and 24x²?

The numeric GCF of 16 and 24 is 8; the lowest shared power of x between x³ and x² is x².

11. Factor: x² + x − 20

5 and −4 multiply to −20 and add to 1.

12. Fill in the blank: x² − 81 factors as ___.

81 = 9², so x² − 81 = (x + 9)(x − 9), a difference of squares.

13. Two angles are supplementary. One measures 105°. What does the other measure?

Supplementary angles sum to 180°: 180 − 105 = 75°.

14. A rectangle is 12 units long and 5 units wide. What is its area?

Area = length × width = 12 × 5 = 60 square units.

15. Fill in the blank: Triangle ABC ~ Triangle DEF, with AB = 3, DE = 12, and BC = 5. EF = ___.

Scale factor = DE/AB = 12/3 = 4. EF = BC × 4 = 5 × 4 = 20.

16. A right triangle has legs 5 and 12. What is the hypotenuse?

c² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169, so c = √169 = 13.

17. A triangle has sides 10, 11, and 15. Is it a right triangle?

10² + 11² = 100 + 121 = 221, but 15² = 225 — they're not equal, so by the converse this is NOT a right triangle.

18. Fill in the blank: the distance between (2, 3) and (10, 9) is ___.

d = √[(10−2)² + (9−3)²] = √[8² + 6²] = √[64 + 36] = √100 = 10.

Adding, Subtracting, and Multiplying Polynomials

Combining polynomials always reduces to two familiar moves: match up like terms, and distribute carefully so nothing gets left out.

← Lesson 7 Lesson 9 → Contents Glossary →

A polynomial is a sum of terms, each a coefficient times a variable raised to a whole-number power (like 3x² − 5x + 7). Combining two polynomials with +, −, or × always comes back to matching up like terms — terms with the exact same variable part.

Adding and subtracting: match like terms

Definition Like terms have the identical variable part (same variable, same exponent) — 3x² and −5x² are like terms; 3x² and 3x are not, even though the coefficient matches. Adding or subtracting polynomials only ever combines coefficients of like terms; unlike terms stay separate, permanently.
Add: (3x² − 5x + 7) + (x² + 2x − 4) Group like terms: (3x² + x²) + (−5x + 2x) + (7 − 4) = 4x² − 3x + 3.
Subtract: (5x² + 3x − 1) − (2x² − 4x + 6) Distribute the minus sign across every term of the second polynomial first: 5x² + 3x − 1 − 2x² + 4x − 6. Then combine: (5x² − 2x²) + (3x + 4x) + (−1 − 6) = 3x² + 7x − 7.
Why the subtraction sign trips people up Subtracting a polynomial means subtracting every one of its terms, not just the first one — −(2x² − 4x + 6) becomes −2x² + 4x − 6, with every sign flipped. Forgetting to flip the last term's sign is the single most common mistake in this lesson.

Multiplying: distribute every term against every term

Multiplying two binomials means every term in the first must multiply every term in the second. Step through it below.

Try It — Multiply (x + 3)(x + 5), Term by Term

Click "Next Step" to begin.

FOIL, as a memory aid For two binomials, FOIL names the four products in order: First×First, Outer×Outer, Inner×Inner, Last×Last. FOIL is just a checklist for "multiply everything by everything" in the specific case of two two-term polynomials — it doesn't work as-is once either polynomial has three or more terms, though "multiply everything by everything" still does.
A trinomial times a binomial: (x² + 2x − 1)(x + 3) Distribute the whole first polynomial against each term of (x + 3):
x·(x² + 2x − 1) = x³ + 2x² − x
3·(x² + 2x − 1) = 3x² + 6x − 3
Add and combine like terms: x³ + (2x² + 3x²) + (−x + 6x) − 3 = x³ + 5x² + 5x − 3.

Check your recall

No peeking back up — answer from memory first, then check.

1. Add: (2x² + 3x − 1) + (4x² − x + 5)

Combine like terms: (2x² + 4x²) + (3x − x) + (−1 + 5) = 6x² + 2x + 4.

2. Subtract: (4x² − 2x + 6) − (x² + 3x − 2)

Distribute the minus sign: 4x² − 2x + 6 − x² − 3x + 2. Combine: (4x² − x²) + (−2x − 3x) + (6 + 2) = 3x² − 5x + 8.

3. Multiply: (x + 4)(x − 2)

FOIL: x·x + x·(−2) + 4·x + 4·(−2) = x² − 2x + 4x − 8 = x² + 2x − 8.

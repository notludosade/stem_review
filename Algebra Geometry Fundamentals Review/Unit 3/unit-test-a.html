1. Simplify: x⁶ · x²

Multiplying powers with the same base adds the exponents: 6 + 2 = 8.

2. Simplify: x¹⁰ / x⁴

Dividing powers with the same base subtracts the exponents: 10 − 4 = 6.

3. Simplify: 3⁻²

A negative exponent means "take the reciprocal": 3⁻² = 1/3² = 1/9.

4. Fill in the blank: (x⁵)² = x^___.

Raising a power to a power multiplies the exponents: 5 × 2 = 10.

5. Simplify: (3x² + 2x − 4) − (x² − 5x + 1)

Distribute the minus sign: 3x² + 2x − 4 − x² + 5x − 1. Combine: (3x² − x²) + (2x + 5x) + (−4 − 1) = 2x² + 7x − 5.

6. Multiply (x + 2)(x + 6). Answer both parts:

(a) What is the Outer × Outer product in FOIL?

(b) What is the fully expanded product?

FOIL: x·x + x·6 + 2·x + 2·6 = x² + 6x + 2x + 12 = x² + 8x + 12.

7. Divide (12x³ − 8x² + 4x) ÷ 4x. Answer both parts:

(a) What is 12x³ ÷ 4x?

(b) What is the fully simplified quotient?

Dividing every term by 4x: 12x³/4x = 3x², −8x²/4x = −2x, 4x/4x = 1, giving 3x² − 2x + 1.

8. Given 5x² + 3x − 2x² + 7 − 4x, answer both parts:

(a) Which term is a like term with 5x²?

(b) What is the fully simplified expression?

5x² and −2x² are like terms (5x² − 2x² = 3x²), as are 3x and −4x (3x − 4x = −x); 7 has no other constant to combine with. Result: 3x² − x + 7.

9. Answer both parts:

(a) What is 12⁰?

(b) x⁰ = 1 falls directly out of applying which law to xⁿ/xⁿ?

Any nonzero number to the zero power is 1. It follows from the quotient rule: xⁿ/xⁿ = xⁿ⁻ⁿ = x⁰, and anything divided by itself is also 1.

10. A rectangle's perimeter is P = 2(3x + 4) + 2(x − 1). Answer both parts:

(a) What is P, fully simplified?

(b) If x = 5, what is P?

2(3x + 4) = 6x + 8 and 2(x − 1) = 2x − 2; adding gives 8x + 6. At x = 5: 8(5) + 6 = 46.

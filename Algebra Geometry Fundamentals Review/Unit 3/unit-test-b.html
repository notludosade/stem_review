1. Simplify: x⁷ · x³

Multiplying powers with the same base adds the exponents: 7 + 3 = 10.

2. Simplify: x¹² / x⁵

Dividing powers with the same base subtracts the exponents: 12 − 5 = 7.

3. Simplify: 2⁻⁴

A negative exponent means "take the reciprocal": 2⁻⁴ = 1/2⁴ = 1/16.

4. Fill in the blank: (x³)⁴ = x^___.

Raising a power to a power multiplies the exponents: 3 × 4 = 12.

5. Simplify: (4x² − 3x + 2) − (2x² + x − 5)

Distribute the minus sign: 4x² − 3x + 2 − 2x² − x + 5. Combine: (4x² − 2x²) + (−3x − x) + (2 + 5) = 2x² − 4x + 7.

6. Multiply (x + 5)(x + 3). Answer both parts:

(a) What is the Outer × Outer product in FOIL?

(b) What is the fully expanded product?

FOIL: x·x + x·3 + 5·x + 5·3 = x² + 3x + 5x + 15 = x² + 8x + 15.

7. Divide (20x³ − 15x² + 5x) ÷ 5x. Answer both parts:

(a) What is 20x³ ÷ 5x?

(b) What is the fully simplified quotient?

Dividing every term by 5x: 20x³/5x = 4x², −15x²/5x = −3x, 5x/5x = 1, giving 4x² − 3x + 1.

8. Given 6x² + 4x − 3x² + 9 − 2x, answer both parts:

(a) Which term is a like term with 6x²?

(b) What is the fully simplified expression?

6x² and −3x² are like terms (6x² − 3x² = 3x²), as are 4x and −2x (4x − 2x = 2x); 9 has no other constant to combine with. Result: 3x² + 2x + 9.

9. Answer both parts:

(a) What is 25⁰?

(b) x⁰ = 1 falls directly out of applying which law to xⁿ/xⁿ?

Any nonzero number to the zero power is 1. It follows from the quotient rule: xⁿ/xⁿ = xⁿ⁻ⁿ = x⁰, and anything divided by itself is also 1.

10. A rectangle's perimeter is P = 2(4x + 2) + 2(x − 3). Answer both parts:

(a) What is P, fully simplified?

(b) If x = 4, what is P?

2(4x + 2) = 8x + 4 and 2(x − 3) = 2x − 6; adding gives 10x − 2. At x = 4: 10(4) − 2 = 38.

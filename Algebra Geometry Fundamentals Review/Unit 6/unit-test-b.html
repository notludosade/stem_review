1. A right triangle has legs 8 and 15. What is the hypotenuse?

c² = 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289, so c = √289 = 17.

2. A right triangle has hypotenuse 20 and one leg 12. What is the other leg?

b² = 20² − 12² = 400 − 144 = 256, so b = √256 = 16.

3. A triangle has sides 6, 8, and 11. Is it a right triangle?

6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100, but 11² = 121 — they're not equal, so by the converse this is NOT a right triangle.

4. Fill in the blank: (8, 15, ___) is a Pythagorean triple.

8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289 = 17², so (8, 15, 17) is a Pythagorean triple.

5. Fill in the blank: the distance between (0, 0) and (7, 24) is ___.

d = √(7² + 24²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25.

6. A triangle has sides 5, 9, and 11 (11 is the longest). Answer both parts:

(a) Which is bigger: a² + b² (= 106) or c² (= 121)?

(b) What kind of triangle is it?

Since a² + b² (106) < c² (121), the angle opposite the longest side exceeds 90° — the triangle is obtuse.

7. A 17-foot ladder leans against a wall with its base 8 feet from the wall. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the height squared (h²)?

(b) How high up the wall does the ladder reach?

h² = 17² − 8² = 289 − 64 = 225, so h = √225 = 15 feet.

8. Find the distance between (4, 2) and (10, 10). Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: dx = ___.

(b) Fill in the blank: the distance is ___.

dx = 10 − 4 = 6, dy = 10 − 2 = 8. d = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10.

9. The triple (7, 24, 25) is scaled by a factor of 3. Answer both parts:

(a) What does the first term (7) become?

(b) What does the hypotenuse term (25) become?

Scaling every term of (7, 24, 25) by 3 gives (21, 72, 75).

10. A rectangular field is 18 m by 24 m. Answer both parts about its diagonal path:

(a) What is the diagonal length squared?

(b) What is the diagonal length?

diagonal² = 18² + 24² = 324 + 576 = 900, so diagonal = √900 = 30 m.

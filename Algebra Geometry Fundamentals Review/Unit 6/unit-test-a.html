1. A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. What is the hypotenuse?

c² = 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225, so c = √225 = 15.

2. A right triangle has hypotenuse 25 and one leg 7. What is the other leg?

b² = 25² − 7² = 625 − 49 = 576, so b = √576 = 24.

3. A triangle has sides 8, 15, and 17. Is it a right triangle?

8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289, and 17² = 289 — they're equal, so by the converse this is a right triangle.

4. Fill in the blank: (9, 12, ___) is a Pythagorean triple (it's (3, 4, 5) scaled by 3).

Scaling every term of (3, 4, 5) by 3 gives (9, 12, 15).

5. Fill in the blank: the distance between (0, 0) and (9, 12) is ___.

d = √(9² + 12²) = √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15.

6. A triangle has sides 6, 7, and 10 (10 is the longest). Answer both parts:

(a) Which is bigger: a² + b² (= 85) or c² (= 100)?

(b) What kind of triangle is it?

Since a² + b² (85) < c² (100), the angle opposite the longest side exceeds 90° — the triangle is obtuse.

7. A 15-foot ladder leans against a wall with its base 9 feet from the wall. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the height squared (h²)?

(b) How high up the wall does the ladder reach?

h² = 15² − 9² = 225 − 81 = 144, so h = √144 = 12 feet.

8. Find the distance between (3, 1) and (11, 7). Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: dx = ___.

(b) Fill in the blank: the distance is ___.

dx = 11 − 3 = 8, dy = 7 − 1 = 6. d = √(8² + 6²) = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10.

9. The triple (5, 12, 13) is scaled by a factor of 4. Answer both parts:

(a) What does the first term (5) become?

(b) What does the hypotenuse term (13) become?

Scaling every term of (5, 12, 13) by 4 gives (20, 48, 52).

10. A rectangular park is 24 m by 10 m. Answer both parts about its diagonal path:

(a) What is the diagonal length squared?

(b) What is the diagonal length?

diagonal² = 24² + 10² = 576 + 100 = 676, so diagonal = √676 = 26 m.

1. Two angles are complementary. One measures 62°. What does the other measure?

Complementary angles sum to 90°: 90 − 62 = 28°.

2. Two lines cross, forming vertical angles. One measures 73°. What does its vertical angle measure?

Vertical angles — the opposite pair formed by two crossing lines — are always equal.

3. Which statement about congruent and similar figures is true?

Congruence requires matching shape AND size; similarity only requires matching shape, so congruence is the special case of similarity with scale factor 1.

4. Fill in the blank: a rectangle is 7 units long and 3 units wide. Its area is ___ square units.

Area = length × width = 7 × 3 = 21 square units.

5. Fill in the blank: a circle has radius 6. Its area, in terms of π, is ___ (write as, e.g., 10pi).

Area = πr² = π(6)² = 36π.

6. A triangle with area 2 square units is scaled by a factor of 5. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the new area?

(b) By what factor did each side length scale?

Area scales by k²: 2 × 5² = 2 × 25 = 50. Side lengths scale by k itself, which is 5.

7. Triangle ABC ~ Triangle DEF, with AB = 5, DE = 15, and BC = 9. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the scale factor from ABC to DEF?

(b) What is EF?

Scale factor = DE/AB = 15/5 = 3. So EF = BC × 3 = 9 × 3 = 27.

8. A transversal crosses two parallel lines. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the relationship where angles in the same position at each intersection are equal is called ___ angles.

(b) Fill in the blank: if one angle measures 118°, its corresponding angle also measures ___°.

Corresponding angles occupy matching positions at each intersection and are always equal — so the corresponding angle is also 118°.

9. A rectangular prism measures 5 by 4 by 3. Answer both parts:

(a) What is its volume?

(b) If every dimension is doubled, what is the new volume?

Volume = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60. Doubling every dimension scales volume by 2³ = 8: 60 × 8 = 480.

10. A rectangle 8 by 5 has a semicircular region of radius 2 removed from one side. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the rectangle's area, before removing anything?

(b) What is the area after removing the semicircle (½πr² ≈ 6.28)?

Rectangle area = 8 × 5 = 40. Semicircle area = ½π(2)² ≈ 6.28. Composite area = 40 − 6.28 ≈ 33.7.

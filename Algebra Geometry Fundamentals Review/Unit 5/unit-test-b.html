1. Two angles are complementary. One measures 51°. What does the other measure?

Complementary angles sum to 90°: 90 − 51 = 39°.

2. Two lines cross, forming vertical angles. One measures 84°. What does its vertical angle measure?

Vertical angles — the opposite pair formed by two crossing lines — are always equal.

3. A figure is scaled by a factor of exactly 1. What can you conclude?

A scale factor of 1 means no resizing occurred at all — the figure matches in both shape and size, which is exactly congruence.

4. Fill in the blank: a rectangle is 9 units long and 4 units wide. Its area is ___ square units.

Area = length × width = 9 × 4 = 36 square units.

5. Fill in the blank: a circle has radius 5. Its area, in terms of π, is ___ (write as, e.g., 10pi).

Area = πr² = π(5)² = 25π.

6. A triangle with area 4 square units is scaled by a factor of 3. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the new area?

(b) By what factor did each side length scale?

Area scales by k²: 4 × 3² = 4 × 9 = 36. Side lengths scale by k itself, which is 3.

7. Triangle GHI ~ Triangle JKL, with GH = 4, JK = 12, and HI = 7. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the scale factor from GHI to JKL?

(b) What is KL?

Scale factor = JK/GH = 12/4 = 3. So KL = HI × 3 = 7 × 3 = 21.

8. A transversal crosses two parallel lines. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the relationship where angles are on opposite sides of the transversal, between the two parallel lines, and are equal is called ___ interior angles.

(b) Fill in the blank: if one angle measures 65°, its alternate interior angle also measures ___°.

Alternate interior angles sit on opposite sides of the transversal between the parallel lines and are always equal — so the alternate interior angle is also 65°.

9. A rectangular prism measures 6 by 2 by 5. Answer both parts:

(a) What is its volume?

(b) If every dimension is tripled, what is the new volume?

Volume = 6 × 2 × 5 = 60. Tripling every dimension scales volume by 3³ = 27: 60 × 27 = 1620.

10. A rectangle 10 by 4 has a semicircular region of radius 3 removed from one side. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the rectangle's area, before removing anything?

(b) What is the area after removing the semicircle (½πr² ≈ 14.14)?

Rectangle area = 10 × 4 = 40. Semicircle area = ½π(3)² ≈ 14.14. Composite area = 40 − 14.14 ≈ 25.9.

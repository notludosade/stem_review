Perimeter, Area, and Volume Formulas

A small formula set that covers most everyday shapes — and the dimensional intuition (1D, 2D, 3D) that keeps them straight.

← Lesson 14 Lesson 16 → Contents Glossary →

Perimeter measures the distance around a 2D shape's boundary (a length, 1D). Area measures the space a 2D shape covers (2D, in square units). Volume measures the space a 3D solid fills (3D, in cubic units). Keeping straight which dimension a formula measures prevents most formula mix-ups.

The core formula set

Formulas Rectangle: Perimeter = 2l + 2w, Area = lw
Triangle: Perimeter = a + b + c, Area = ½bh
Circle: Circumference = 2πr, Area = πr²
Rectangular prism: Volume = lwh

Why π shows up for circles but nowhere else here π is the fixed ratio between any circle's circumference and its diameter — the same number for every circle, no matter its size. Rectangles and prisms are built from straight edges, so no such constant ratio is needed; circles are the one shape in this set defined by curvature, which is exactly where π comes from.
Worked example: a composite figure A rectangle 10 by 6 has a semicircular bite of radius 3 removed from one side. Area = rectangle area − semicircle area = (10 × 6) − ½π(3)² = 60 − 14.14 ≈ 45.86 square units. Composite figures are almost always just addition or subtraction of the basic shapes above.

Check your recall

No peeking back up — answer from memory first, then check.

1. A rectangle is 9 units long and 5 units wide. What is its area?

Area = length × width = 9 × 5 = 45 square units.

2. A circle has radius 4. What is its circumference (leave in terms of π)?

Circumference = 2πr = 2π(4) = 8π.

3. A rectangular prism measures 5 by 3 by 2. What is its volume?

Volume = l × w × h = 5 × 3 × 2 = 30 cubic units.

4. Fill in the blank: a triangle has base 8 and height 6. Its area is ___ square units.

Area = ½bh = ½(8)(6) = 24 square units.

5. Fill in the blank: the measurement of the 3D space a solid fills, measured in cubic units, is called ___.

Volume is the 3D counterpart to a 2D shape's area, measured in cubic (not square) units.

1. What is the GCF of 20x³ and 30x²?

The numeric GCF of 20 and 30 is 10; the lowest shared power of x between x³ and x² is x².

2. Factor: x² + 10x + 21

3 and 7 multiply to 21 and add to 10.

3. Factor: x² + 2x − 24

6 and −4 multiply to −24 and add to 2.

4. Fill in the blank: x² − 100 factors as ___.

100 = 10², so x² − 100 = (x + 10)(x − 10).

5. Fill in the blank: x² + 14x + 49 factors as ___.

49 = 7², and 14 = 2 × 7, so this is a perfect square trinomial: (x + 7)².

6. Factor 15x³ − 25x² completely. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the GCF?

(b) What is the complete factored form?

GCF = 5x². Dividing each term: 15x³/5x² = 3x, −25x²/5x² = −5, giving 5x²(3x − 5).

7. Factor x² − x − 30. Answer both parts:

(a) Which number pair multiplies to −30 and adds to −1?

(b) What is the factored form?

−6 and 5 multiply to −30 and add to −1, giving (x − 6)(x + 5).

8. Factor 4x² − 9. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: 4x² is (___)².

(b) Fill in the blank: the full factored form is ___.

4x² = (2x)² and 9 = 3², so 4x² − 9 = (2x + 3)(2x − 3), a difference of squares.

9. Compare x² + 8x + 16 and x² + 9x + 16. Answer both parts:

(a) Which one IS a perfect square trinomial?

(b) What is its factored form?

16 = 4², and 2 × 4 = 8 (matching the middle term of the first trinomial, not the second), so only x² + 8x + 16 = (x + 4)².

10. A square has area x² − 20x + 100 square feet. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the side length, as an expression?

(b) If x = 15, what is the side length?

x² − 20x + 100 = (x − 10)², so the side length is x − 10. At x = 15: 15 − 10 = 5.

1. What is the GCF of 18x⁴ and 24x³?

The numeric GCF of 18 and 24 is 6; the lowest shared power of x between x⁴ and x³ is x³.

2. Factor: x² + 11x + 24

3 and 8 multiply to 24 and add to 11.

3. Factor: x² + 3x − 28

7 and −4 multiply to −28 and add to 3.

4. Fill in the blank: x² − 121 factors as ___.

121 = 11², so x² − 121 = (x + 11)(x − 11).

5. Fill in the blank: x² + 16x + 64 factors as ___.

64 = 8², and 16 = 2 × 8, so this is a perfect square trinomial: (x + 8)².

6. Factor 21x³ − 14x² completely. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the GCF?

(b) What is the complete factored form?

GCF = 7x². Dividing each term: 21x³/7x² = 3x, −14x²/7x² = −2, giving 7x²(3x − 2).

7. Factor x² − 2x − 35. Answer both parts:

(a) Which number pair multiplies to −35 and adds to −2?

(b) What is the factored form?

−7 and 5 multiply to −35 and add to −2, giving (x − 7)(x + 5).

8. Factor 9x² − 16. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: 9x² is (___)².

(b) Fill in the blank: the full factored form is ___.

9x² = (3x)² and 16 = 4², so 9x² − 16 = (3x + 4)(3x − 4), a difference of squares.

9. Compare x² + 10x + 25 and x² + 11x + 25. Answer both parts:

(a) Which one IS a perfect square trinomial?

(b) What is its factored form?

25 = 5², and 2 × 5 = 10 (matching the middle term of the first trinomial, not the second), so only x² + 10x + 25 = (x + 5)².

10. A square has area x² − 24x + 144 square feet. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the side length, as an expression?

(b) If x = 20, what is the side length?

x² − 24x + 144 = (x − 12)², so the side length is x − 12. At x = 20: 20 − 12 = 8.

# Notes

- Follows the College Board's 2025-26 AP Physics 1 framework: 8 units, with Fluids as Unit 8 (moved from AP Physics 2 starting with the May 2025 exam) and circular motion/gravitation folded into Unit 2 (Force and Translational Dynamics) rather than given its own unit. See MISSION.md.
- Static labeled SVG figures by default (free-body diagrams, motion graphs, ray-style force diagrams), matching Real Analysis A and Precalculus. A handful of lessons reuse `assets/interactive.js` (`mountParametricCurve` for projectile motion, drag-based position/time graphs for oscillation) where the exploration is worth it — not attempted on every lesson.
- Unit numbering restarts at 1 for this course (not College Board's continuous 1-8 / 9-15 numbering across Physics 1 and 2), matching this site's convention of every course's own index.html starting fresh at Unit 1 (e.g. Computer Programming 2 doesn't continue Computer Programming 1's unit numbers).

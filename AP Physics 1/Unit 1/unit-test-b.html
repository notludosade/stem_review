1. Fill in the blank: A delivery drone flies 500 m north, then flies 200 m back south. The drone's ___ for this trip is 300 m north, even though it traveled 700 m of actual path.

Displacement compares only the final position to the initial position (500 north, then 200 back gives a net 300 m north), regardless of how much path length was actually covered along the way.

2. A position-time graph shows a straight horizontal line for a 5 s interval. During this interval, the object's velocity is:

A horizontal segment on a position-time graph has zero slope. Since slope equals velocity, the object is momentarily at rest for that whole interval.

3. A cyclist's initial velocity, acceleration, and elapsed time are all known, but her total displacement is not given and is not needed to find her final velocity. Which kinematic equation applies?

Of the four kinematic equations, v = v₀ + at is the only one that doesn't contain Δx — exactly the variable missing here.

4. Which of the following is a scalar quantity, not a vector?

Speed is magnitude only, with no direction attached, which is exactly what makes it a scalar. Velocity, acceleration, and displacement all carry direction and are vectors.

5. Two identical balls are launched at the same speed, one at 20° above horizontal and the other at 70° above horizontal, both landing at the same height they launched from. How do their ranges compare?

Range is proportional to sin(2θ). Here 2θ is 40° for one ball and 140° for the other, and sin(140°) = sin(180° − 140°) = sin(40°) — angles that sum to 90° always give equal ranges, even with different flight times and heights.

6. A hiker starts at x = 0 m, reaches x = 80 m at t = 20 s, then walks back to x = 50 m at t = 30 s. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the hiker's average velocity for the entire 30 s?

(b) Fill in the blank: the hiker's average speed for the entire 30 s, rounded to two decimal places, is ___ m/s.

(a) Average velocity = Δx/Δt = (50 − 0)/30 ≈ 1.67 m/s. (b) Total distance covered is 80 m (out) + 30 m (back) = 110 m, so average speed = 110/30 ≈ 3.67 m/s.

7. An object's velocity is a constant −5 m/s for 3 s, then increases linearly to 0 m/s over the following 2 s. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the object's total displacement over the full 5 s, found from the area under its velocity-time graph?

(b) During the final 2 s, while velocity increases from −5 m/s to 0 m/s, the object's acceleration is:

(a) The first 3 s is a rectangle below the axis: −5 × 3 = −15 m. The next 2 s is a triangle, still below the axis (velocity is negative throughout): −½ × 2 × 5 = −5 m. Total: −15 + (−5) = −20 m. (b) a = Δv/Δt = (0 − (−5))/2 = +2.5 m/s² — positive, which is opposite in sign to the object's negative velocity, exactly what slowing down looks like here.

8. A ball rolls down a ramp, starting from rest and accelerating uniformly at 1.5 m/s² for 8 s. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the ball's velocity at the end of the 8 s?

(b) Fill in the blank: the ball's displacement during these 8 s is ___ m.

(a) v = v₀ + at = 0 + 1.5(8) = 12 m/s. (b) Δx = v₀t + ½at² = 0 + ½(1.5)(8²) = ½(1.5)(64) = 48 m.

9. A ball is thrown straight up at 29.4 m/s from ground level. Using g = 9.8 m/s² and taking up as positive, answer both parts:

(a) How long does it take the ball to reach its highest point?

(b) Fill in the blank: the ball's maximum height above the ground is ___ m.

(a) At the peak v = 0, so from v = v₀ + at: 0 = 29.4 + (−9.8)t → t = 3 s. (b) H = v₀²/(2g) = 29.4²/19.6 = 44.1 m.

10. A projectile is launched at 24.5 m/s at an angle of 53° above horizontal, landing at the same height it launched from. Using g = 9.8 m/s², sin 53° ≈ 0.8, and cos 53° ≈ 0.6, answer both parts:

(a) What is the initial vertical component of velocity, v0y?

(b) Fill in the blank: the projectile's total time of flight is ___ s.

(a) v₀y = v₀ sin θ = 24.5(0.8) = 19.6 m/s; the 14.7 m/s distractor is actually v₀x = 24.5(0.6). (b) T = 2v₀y/g = 2(19.6)/9.8 = 4 s, from the symmetry of time up and time down.

1. Let h(t) map each hour t of the day to the number of visitors who arrived during that hour. Why does this qualify as a function of t?

The definition only restricts inputs: each hour (input) must map to exactly one visitor count (output). Repeated outputs across different hours are completely fine.

2. Fill in the blank: The domain of f(x) = √(x − 3) is all real numbers x such that x ≥ ___.

A square root needs a nonnegative radicand, so x − 3 ≥ 0, which means x ≥ 3.

3. If f(x) = 2x + 1 and f(3) = 7, what is f⁻¹(7)?

The inverse undoes f exactly: since f sends 3 to 7, f⁻¹ must send 7 back to 3.

4. The graph of a polynomial touches the x-axis at x = 2 and turns back around, without crossing it. What does this indicate about the zero at x = 2?

Touching and turning back (instead of crossing through) is the signature of an even-multiplicity zero — the graph stays on the same side of the x-axis.

5. Fill in the blank: A degree-5 polynomial with real coefficients has exactly ___ complex zeros, counting multiplicity.

The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra guarantees exactly as many complex zeros (with multiplicity) as the degree — 5 zeros for a degree-5 polynomial.

6. For f(x) = (3x² + 1) / (x² − 4), what is the horizontal asymptote?

Numerator and denominator have equal degree, so the asymptote is the ratio of leading coefficients: 3/1 = 3.

7. What is log₂(32)?

log₂(32) asks "2 to what power gives 32?" Since 2⁵ = 32, the answer is 5.

8. Fill in the blank: log(7) + log(3) = log(___).

The product rule turns a sum of logs into the log of a product: log(7) + log(3) = log(7 × 3) = log(21).

9. Solve for x: 3ˣ = 81.

81 = 3⁴, and since both sides share base 3, the exponents must match: x = 4.

10. What is sin(210°)?

210° is in Quadrant III with reference angle 30°. Sine is negative there, so sin(210°) = −sin(30°) = −1/2.

11. Fill in the blank: In a right triangle, if the side opposite angle θ has length 5 and the hypotenuse has length 13, then sin(θ) = 5/___.

Sine is opposite over hypotenuse, so sin(θ) = 5/13 — the blank is the hypotenuse length, 13.

12. What is the amplitude of y = 4sin(2x) − 1?

Amplitude is the coefficient multiplying sine, 4 — the −1 is a vertical shift and doesn't affect how far the curve stretches from its midline.

13. What is arccos(−1/2), restricted to the interval [0, π]?

cos(2π/3) = −1/2, and 2π/3 falls inside arccos's restricted range [0, π], so it's the one valid answer.

14. Fill in the blank: If sin(θ) = 3/5 and θ is in Quadrant I, then cos(θ) = ___/5.

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, so cos²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25, giving cos θ = 4/5 (positive, since Quadrant I keeps cosine positive).

15. If sin(θ) = 3/5 and cos(θ) = 4/5, what is sin(2θ)?

sin(2θ) = 2 sin θ cos θ = 2(3/5)(4/5) = 24/25.

16. Solve 2sin(x) − 1 = 0 for x in [0, 2π).

sin(x) = 1/2 has two solutions in [0, 2π): π/6 in Quadrant I and its supplement 5π/6 in Quadrant II, where sine is also positive.

17. A Ferris wheel's height is modeled by h(t) = 20sin((π/15)(t − 7.5)) + 22, with t in minutes. What is the wheel's maximum height?

Sine maxes out at 1, so the maximum height is the amplitude plus the vertical shift: 20 + 22 = 42.

18. Fill in the blank: For the system x + y = 10 and x − y = 2, the value of x is ___.

Adding the two equations eliminates y: 2x = 12, so x = 6.

19. For the system 3x + 2y = 7, x − y = 1, what is the determinant D of the coefficient matrix?

D = |3 2; 1 −1| = (3)(−1) − (2)(1) = −3 − 2 = −5.

20. How many distinct ways can you arrange 4 different books on a shelf?

Arranging 4 distinct items in order is a permutation of all 4: 4! = 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24.

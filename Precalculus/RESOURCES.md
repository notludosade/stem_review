# Precalculus Resources

## Knowledge

- [OpenStax Precalculus 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/precalculus-2e)
  Free, peer-reviewed textbook. Chapters 1-4 cover function types (polynomial, rational, exponential, log), 5-8 cover trigonometry (unit-circle-first approach). Use for: primary reading, formal definitions, end-of-section practice sets. Mirrored at [Math LibreTexts](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Precalculus/Precalculus_2e_(OpenStax)) if openstax.org is ever unreachable.
- [Paul's Online Math Notes (Lamar University)](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/)
  Paul Dawkins' notes, covering Algebra/Precalc through Calc III. Terse, well-organized, no fluff. Use for: a crisp second explanation when a concept needs re-reading, and for the [cheat sheets](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/extras/cheatsheets_tables.aspx) (esp. the Trig Cheat Sheet) as quick-lookup references.
- [Professor Leonard — "Precalculus: College Algebra/Trigonometry" (YouTube playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDesaqWTN6ESsmwELdrzhcGiRhk5DjwLP)
  Full lecture-style walkthroughs, ~70 hours, explicitly built to prep students for Calc 1. Use for: seeing a topic taught step-by-step when reading alone isn't landing.
- [Khan Academy — Precalculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus)
  Bite-sized lessons with instant-feedback practice problems. Use for: quick drilling between lessons here, especially for procedural fluency (not conceptual depth).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/learnmath](https://www.reddit.com/r/learnmath/)
  General but active and well-moderated math help community. Use for: posting a specific problem you're stuck on and getting a worked explanation from another learner or teacher.

## Gaps

- No subreddit/community found specifically scoped to precalculus (vs. general math help). Revisit if r/learnmath turns out to be too noisy or too advanced/basic for precalc-specific questions.

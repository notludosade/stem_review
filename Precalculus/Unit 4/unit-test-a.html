1. For a 210° angle, answer both parts:

(a) Convert 210° to radians:

(b) Fill in the blank: 210° lies in the third quadrant with a reference angle of ___°.

(a) 210 · (π/180) reduces to 7π/6. Flipping the fraction, landing on 240°'s radian measure (4π/3), or double-counting to 7π/3 gives the three distractors. (b) In QIII, the reference angle is the angle's distance from 180°: 210° − 180° = 30°.

2. For the angle 120°, answer both parts:

(a) sin(120°) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: cos(120°) = ___.

(a) 120° is in QII with reference angle 60°; sin is positive in QII, so sin(120°) = sin(60°) = √3/2 — not the reference angle's cosine value, and not negative. (b) cos is negative in QII, so cos(120°) = −cos(60°) = −1/2.

3. Fill in the blank: if cos θ = 3/5 and θ is in the first quadrant, then sin θ = ___.

By the Pythagorean Identity, sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25, so sin θ = 4/5 (positive, since θ is in QI).

4. Answer both parts about where cot θ is undefined:

(a) cot θ is undefined wherever:

(b) Which other trig function is undefined at exactly those same values?

(a) cot θ = cos θ/sin θ, so it's undefined exactly where its denominator, sin θ, equals 0 — at 0°, 180°, 360°, and coterminal angles. (b) csc θ = 1/sin θ has the same denominator, so it shares the exact same restriction — tan θ and sec θ are undefined where cos θ = 0 instead.

5. A right triangle has opposite leg 8, adjacent leg 15, and hypotenuse 17. Answer both parts:

(a) tan θ equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: sin θ = ___.

(a) tan θ = opposite/adjacent = 8/15. The distractors name the reciprocal (cot), or ratios that pull in the hypotenuse instead. (b) sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse = 8/17.

6. A 12 ft ladder leans against a wall, making a 50° angle with the ground. The height it reaches is 12·sin(50°) ≈ ___ ft (nearest tenth).

Height = 12·sin(50°) ≈ 12 × 0.766 ≈ 9.2 ft, using the side opposite the given angle over the hypotenuse (the ladder).

7. For y = 5cos(3x) + 2, answer both parts:

(a) The amplitude is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the period is ___.

(a) Amplitude is |A|, the coefficient on the cosine term — here 5. The +2 only shifts the midline, and the 3 controls the period, not the height. (b) Period = 2π/|B|, and here B = 3, so period = 2π/3.

8. sin x is symmetric about the:

sin is an odd function (sin(−x) = −sin(x)), which means its graph is symmetric about the origin — cos x is the one symmetric about the y-axis instead.

9. The period of y = tan(x) is ___.

Shifting x by π flips the sign of both sin x and cos x, and those flips cancel in the ratio tan x = sin x/cos x, so the pattern repeats after only π rather than 2π.

10. arccos(−1/2) equals:

cos(2π/3) = −1/2, and 2π/3 falls inside arccos's range [0, π] — 4π/3 gives the same cosine value but lies outside that range, so it isn't the inverse function's output.

1. For an angle of 5π/4 radians, answer both parts:

(a) Convert to degrees:

(b) Fill in the blank: this angle lies in the third quadrant with a reference angle of ___°.

(a) (5π/4) · (180/π) reduces to 225°. The distractors are nearby angles that don't come from applying the correct conversion factor. (b) In QIII, the reference angle is the angle's distance from 180°: 225° − 180° = 45°.

2. For the angle 210°, answer both parts:

(a) cos(210°) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: sin(210°) = ___.

(a) 210° is in QIII with reference angle 30°; cos is negative in QIII, so cos(210°) = −cos(30°) = −√3/2. (b) sin is also negative in QIII, so sin(210°) = −sin(30°) = −1/2.

3. Fill in the blank: if sin θ = 5/13 and θ is in the first quadrant, then cos θ = ___.

By the Pythagorean Identity, cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ = 1 − 25/169 = 144/169, so cos θ = 12/13 (positive, since θ is in QI).

4. Answer both parts about shared restrictions among the trig functions:

(a) tan θ and sec θ share the same restriction because both are undefined wherever:

(b) Which pair of functions instead shares the restriction sin θ = 0?

(a) tan θ = sin θ/cos θ and sec θ = 1/cos θ both have cos θ in the denominator, so both blow up exactly where cos θ = 0 — at 90°, 270°, and coterminal angles. (b) cot θ = cos θ/sin θ and csc θ = 1/sin θ both have sin θ in the denominator instead, so they're undefined together wherever sin θ = 0 — at 0°, 180°, 360°, and coterminal angles.

5. A right triangle has adjacent leg 7, opposite leg 24, and hypotenuse 25. Answer both parts:

(a) cos θ equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: sin θ = ___.

(a) cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse = 7/25. 24/25 is sin θ, and the other two distractors mix in the wrong pair of sides. (b) sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse = 24/25.

6. The angle of elevation to the top of a 40 ft tower, from a point on the ground, is 35°. The horizontal distance from that point to the base of the tower is 40/tan(35°) ≈ ___ ft (nearest whole number).

tan(35°) ≈ 0.700, and 40/0.700 ≈ 57.1 ft — the adjacent (horizontal) leg, found from the opposite side and the tangent ratio.

7. The period of y = cos(4x) is:

Period = 2π/|B|, and here B = 4, so period = 2π/4 = π/2 — a much faster cycle than the parent cos x.

8. For y = 2sin(x) − 3, answer both parts:

(a) The midline is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the amplitude is ___.

(a) The vertical shift D sets the midline, y = D — here D = −3, so the wave oscillates around y = −3, between y = −5 and y = −1. (b) Amplitude is |A|, the coefficient on the sine term — here 2, matching the distance from the midline to each extreme (−5 to −3, and −3 to −1).

9. The period of y = sec(x) is ___.

sec x = 1/cos x inherits cos x's period directly, since flipping a single term's sign (rather than a ratio of two terms) doesn't cancel anything at half a period — so it stays 2π, unlike tan and cot.

10. arctan(1) equals:

tan(π/4) = 1, and π/4 falls inside arctan's range (−π/2, π/2).

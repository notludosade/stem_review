1. A system of two linear equations has identical slopes and identical y-intercepts. This system is:

Identical slope and intercept means the two lines coincide entirely, so every point on the line is a shared solution — infinitely many solutions describes a dependent (and therefore consistent) system.

2. Fill in the blank: If elimination on a 3×3 system produces a row that reduces to 0 = 0, the system has ___ solutions.

A statement that's always true (0 = 0) means one equation was redundant — the three planes share an entire line, or coincide, giving infinitely many solutions rather than one or none.

3. Decompose (3x + 2)/[(x − 1)(x + 4)] into partial fractions: (3x + 2)/[(x − 1)(x + 4)] = A/(x − 1) + B/(x + 4). Answer both parts:

(a) What is A, the constant over (x − 1)?

(b) What is B, the constant over (x + 4)?

(a) Writing 3x + 2 = A(x + 4) + B(x − 1) and substituting x = 1 gives 3(1) + 2 = A(5), so A = 1. (b) Substituting x = −4 into the same identity gives 3(−4) + 2 = B(−4 − 1), so −10 = −5B, giving B = 2.

4. If A = [[2, −3], [1, 5]], what is 3A?

Scalar multiplication multiplies every entry by the scalar: 3(2) = 6, 3(−3) = −9, 3(1) = 3, 3(5) = 15.

5. For A = [[1, 2], [0, 3]] and B = [[2, 1], [1, 4]], answer both parts about the product AB:

(a) Fill in the blank: entry (1,1) of AB equals ___.

(b) Fill in the blank: entry (2,2) of AB equals ___.

(a) Entry (1,1) pairs row 1 of A with column 1 of B: (1)(2) + (2)(1) = 4. (b) Entry (2,2) pairs row 2 of A with column 2 of B: (0)(1) + (3)(4) = 12.

6. For A = [[3, 1], [2, 4]], answer both parts:

(a) Is A invertible, and what is its determinant ad − bc?

(b) What is the determinant of 2A?

(a) ad − bc = (3)(4) − (1)(2) = 10, which is nonzero, so A is invertible — a zero determinant is the only case that blocks inversion. (b) Scaling a 2×2 matrix by k scales its determinant by k²: det(2A) = 2² · det(A) = 4 · 10 = 40.

7. Using Cramer's Rule for 2x + y = 7, x − 3y = −2 (D = −7), what is Dy, the determinant with the constants substituted into the y-column?

Substituting the constants (7, −2) into the y-column gives Dy = |2 7; 1 −2| = (2)(−2) − (7)(1) = −4 − 7 = −11.

8. A geometric sequence has a₁ = 5 and r = −2. Answer both parts:

(a) What is a₅ (the 5th term)?

(b) Fill in the blank: a₆ (the 6th term) = ___.

(a) aₙ = a₁ · r^(n−1), so a₅ = 5 · (−2)⁴ = 5 · 16 = 80 — the negative ratio raised to an even power turns positive. (b) a₆ = 5 · (−2)⁵ = 5 · (−32) = −160 — an odd power of a negative ratio flips it back negative.

9. The infinite geometric series 12 + 6 + 3 + 1.5 + … has common ratio r = 1/2. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: its sum converges to S = a₁/(1 − r) = ___.

(b) An infinite geometric series converges to a finite sum only when its common ratio r satisfies:

(a) S = a₁/(1 − r) = 12/(1 − 0.5) = 12/0.5 = 24, since |r| = 1/2 is less than 1 so the series converges. (b) When |r| ≥ 1, the terms don't shrink toward zero, so the partial sums grow without bound instead of approaching a finite limit — convergence requires |r| < 1.

10. A bag holds 6 red marbles and 4 blue marbles. Drawing 2 marbles at random without replacement, what is the probability both are red?

C(6,2)/C(10,2) = 15/45 = 1/3 — the ratio of ways to choose 2 red marbles from 6 to ways to choose any 2 marbles from all 10.

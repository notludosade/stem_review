1. Dividing sin²x + cos²x = 1 through by sin²x produces which identity?

Dividing sin²x + cos²x = 1 through by sin²x gives 1 + cot²x = csc²x. Dividing by cos²x instead gives the 1 + tan²x = sec²x sibling identity.

2. Tangent is odd and secant is even. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: Because tangent is an odd function, tan(−x) = ___.

(b) Since cosine is even, which of these equals sec(−x)?

(a) An odd function satisfies f(−x) = −f(x). Tangent's graph has 180° rotational symmetry about the origin, so tan(−x) = −tan x. (b) Secant is the reciprocal of cosine, an even function, so secant inherits that evenness: sec(−x) = 1/cos(−x) = 1/cos(x) = sec(x).

3. With 105° = 60° + 45°, answer both parts:

(a) Using cos(α + β) = cosα cosβ − sinα sinβ, what is cos(105°)?

(b) Using sin(α + β) = sinα cosβ + cosα sinβ, what is sin(105°)?

(a) cos105° = cos60°cos45° − sin60°sin45° = (1/2)(√2/2) − (√3/2)(√2/2) = √2/4 − √6/4 = (√2 − √6)/4 — negative, since 105° lands in Quadrant II where cosine is negative. (b) sin105° = sin60°cos45° + cos60°sin45° = (√3/2)(√2/2) + (1/2)(√2/2) = √6/4 + √2/4 = (√6 + √2)/4 — positive, since sine is positive throughout Quadrant II.

4. tan(α + β) equals:

Tangent's sum formula pairs a plus on top with a minus in the denominator. The first distractor is actually tan(α−β); the other two mismatch the sign pattern entirely.

5. If sinθ = 3/5 and cosθ = 4/5, answer both parts:

(a) What is sin 2θ?

(b) Fill in the blank: cos 2θ = ___.

(a) sin 2θ = 2 sinθ cosθ = 2(3/5)(4/5) = 24/25. (b) cos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ = (4/5)² − (3/5)² = 16/25 − 9/25 = 7/25.

6. Fill in the blank: The reduction formula states sin²θ = (1 − cos2θ) / ___.

Solving cos2θ = 1 − 2sin²θ for sin²θ gives sin²θ = (1 − cos2θ)/2 — the reduction formula used to trade a squared function for a plain cosine at double the angle.

7. Answer both parts about the product-to-sum formulas:

(a) sinα sinβ equals:

(b) cosα cosβ equals:

(a) Subtracting the two cosine sum/difference formulas isolates 2 sinα sinβ; dividing by 2 gives ½[cos(α−β) − cos(α+β)]. (b) Adding those same two formulas instead isolates 2 cosα cosβ; dividing by 2 gives ½[cos(α−β) + cos(α+β)] — the sign is the only difference between the two identities.

8. Which formula correctly expresses cosα + cosβ as a product?

cosα + cosβ pairs two cosines of the half-sum and half-difference with a positive sign. The sine-based and negative options each name one of the other three sum-to-product formulas instead.

9. To solve 3cos²x − cosx − 2 = 0 algebraically, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the standard first step is the substitution u = ___.

(b) After substituting, the equation becomes 3u² − u − 2 = 0. What are its solutions for u?

(a) The equation contains only cosx (and its square) at a single angle, so substituting u = cosx turns it into an ordinary quadratic to factor. (b) 3u² − u − 2 factors as (3u + 2)(u − 1) = 0, giving u = −2/3 or u = 1 — both within the valid range [−1, 1] for cosx, so both lead to real solutions for x.

10. A city's average monthly temperature has a January low of 20°F and a July high of 70°F. What is the midline D of a sinusoidal model for this data?

The midline is the average of the maximum and minimum: D = (70 + 20)/2 = 45°F.

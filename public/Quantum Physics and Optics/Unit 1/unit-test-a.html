1. Light with frequency 6×10¹⁴ Hz travels through vacuum. What is its wavelength?

λ = c/f = (3×10⁸)/(6×10¹⁴) = 5×10⁻⁷ m = 500 nm.

2. According to Huygens' principle, the new position of a wavefront a short time later is found by:

The envelope tangent to every secondary wavelet — not any single point or average — defines the new wavefront.

3. Light in air (n = 1.00) strikes water (n = 1.33) at an incidence angle of 40°. What is the refraction angle?

sinθ₂ = (1.00/1.33) sin40° = (0.752)(0.643) ≈ 0.483, so θ₂ ≈ 28.9°.

4. Fill in the blank: the number of complete wave cycles passing a fixed point per second is called ___.

Frequency f is cycles per second, the reciprocal of the period T.

5. Using Huygens' wavelets, why does the law of reflection (θᵢ = θᵣ) hold exactly, with no bending?

Refraction's bending comes from a speed mismatch crossing into a new medium; reflected wavelets stay in the original medium at the same speed, so there is no such mismatch.

6. A wave has period T = 0.02 s. Answer both parts:

(a) What is its frequency?

(b) Fill in the blank: if this wave travels at 340 m/s, its wavelength is ___ m.

(a) f = 1/T = 1/0.02 = 50 Hz. (b) λ = v/f = 340/50 = 6.8 m.

7. A plane (flat) wavefront moves through open space with no obstacles. Answer both parts:

(a) What shape does the next wavefront have, according to Huygens' construction?

(b) Fill in the blank: each point on the wavefront acts as a source of secondary ___.

(a) The tangent envelope to wavelets from a flat wavefront is itself flat, reproducing ordinary straight-line propagation. (b) These secondary wavelets are the building blocks of the envelope construction.

8. Light travels from glass (n = 1.50) into air (n = 1.00) at an incidence angle of 20°, measured from the normal inside the glass. Answer both parts:

(a) Compared to the incidence angle, the refraction angle in air will be:

(b) Fill in the blank: sinθ₂ = (n₁/n₂) sinθ₁ = (1.50/1.00) sin20° ≈ ___ (round to 2 decimal places).

(a) Going from a denser to a less-dense medium bends the ray away from the normal. (b) sinθ₂ = 1.50 × sin20° = 1.50 × 0.342 ≈ 0.51, giving θ₂ ≈ 30.7°.

9. A medium has index of refraction n = 1.60. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the speed of light in this medium?

(b) Fill in the blank: the index of refraction is defined as n = c/___.

(a) v = c/n = (3×10⁸)/1.60 ≈ 1.875×10⁸ m/s. (b) n = c/v, the ratio of light's speed in vacuum to its speed in the medium.

10. A light ray strikes a glass surface, producing both a reflected ray and a refracted ray. Answer both parts:

(a) Which of the two rays changes its direction due to a change in wave speed?

(b) Fill in the blank: the bending of a wave's direction when it crosses into a new medium is called ___.

(a) The refracted ray crosses into a new medium and changes speed, which is exactly what causes its direction to bend; the reflected ray never leaves the original medium. (b) This bending is refraction, governed by Snell's law.

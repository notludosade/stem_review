1. Two masses experience a gravitational force of 180 N at separation r₀. What is the force at separation 3r₀?

F ∝ 1/r², so tripling r divides F by 3² = 9: 180/9 = 20 N.

2. Fill in the blank: gravity is a ___ force — always directed along the line joining the two masses — which is why it exerts zero torque about that line.

A central force is always parallel to the position vector r, guaranteeing τ = r × F = 0.

3. Fill in the blank: a planet orbits at radius r₀ with period T₀ = 3 years. Another planet orbits the same star at radius 4r₀. Its period is ___ years.

T² ∝ r³, so (T/T₀)² = 4³ = 64 → T/T₀ = 8 → T = 8(3) = 24 years.

4. An object has total mechanical energy E > 0 relative to a planet. This means the object is:

Positive total energy means the object has more than enough kinetic energy to overcome the potential energy well and reach r → ∞ with speed to spare.

5. Which formula gives the minimum launch speed needed to escape a planet's gravity entirely (mass M, radius R)?

v_esc comes from setting total energy E = 0: ½mv² − GMm/R = 0 → v = √(2GM/R).

6. Two masses experience a force of 200 N at separation r₀. Answer both parts:

(a) By what factor does the force scale when the separation increases to 5r₀?

(b) Fill in the blank: the force at 5r₀ is ___ N.

(a) F ∝ 1/r², so a 5× distance scales force by 1/5² = 1/25. (b) 200/25 = 8 N.

7. An orbiting body has aphelion distance 7 times its perihelion distance. Answer both parts:

(a) Why does the body's speed increase as it approaches perihelion?

(b) Fill in the blank: vperihelion/vaphelion = ___.

(a) Conservation of angular momentum forces v to grow as r shrinks. (b) mv_pr_p = mv_ar_a → v_p/v_a = r_a/r_p = 7.

8. Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula relates orbital period T to orbital radius r for a circular orbit?

(b) Fill in the blank: if a second orbit has radius 9× the first, its period is ___× the first period.

(a) Setting gravity equal to the centripetal force requirement and solving for T² gives T² = (4π²/GM)r³. (b) (T₂/T₁)² = 9³ = 729 → T₂/T₁ = 27.

(a) As a planet's mass M increases while its radius R stays fixed, escape velocity:

(b) A planet has 2GM/R = 2.25 × 10⁸ m²/s². Fill in the blank: its escape velocity is ___ km/s.

(a) v_esc = √(2GM/R) — larger M in the numerator means larger v_esc. (b) v_esc = √(2.25×10⁸) = 15,000 m/s = 15 km/s.

10. An object has KE = 650 J and gravitational potential energy U = −900 J at some point. Answer both parts:

(a) Is this object bound or unbound?

(b) Fill in the blank: the object's total mechanical energy is ___ J.

(a) E = KE + U = 650 + (−900) = −250 J, negative, so the object is bound. (b) E = −250 J.

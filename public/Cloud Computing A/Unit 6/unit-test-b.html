1. A news site's server fleet automatically grows during a traffic spike and shrinks afterward, with no engineer touching a setting. What property is this an example of?

Automatic, demand-driven capacity change is exactly what elasticity means.

2. Fill in the blank: a rule like "add another server whenever average CPU exceeds 70%" is called a ___.

A scaling policy is the rule an auto scaling group follows to decide when to add or remove servers.

3. What is a key limitation of vertical scaling that horizontal scaling avoids?

Vertical scaling is capped by the biggest available instance size and typically needs a restart, unlike horizontal scaling.

4. Why is it useful that a cloud bill is itemized by resource, rather than presented as one flat number?

Itemization turns the bill into direct, traceable feedback on specific architecture and design choices.

5. Why is egress pricing described as an "asymmetry" compared to ingress?

The free-in, priced-out pattern is exactly the asymmetry that can produce a surprising bill.

6. Lesson 16's auto scaling maximum and Lesson 9's storage tier choice both connect directly to Unit 6's cost lesson. Answer both parts:

(a) What bill line does the auto scaling maximum most directly cap?

(b) What bill line does moving data to a cold storage tier most directly reduce?

Auto scaling's maximum caps the compute line; storage tiering reduces the storage line — each earlier decision maps to a specific bill item.

7. Fill in the blank: a broad geographic area, like "US East," containing multiple availability zones, is called a ___.

A region is the broader geographic grouping that contains multiple physically independent availability zones.

8. Why does automatic failover matter more than a process requiring a human to notice and intervene?

Speed and consistency are exactly why automatic failover outperforms a manual, human-dependent process.

9. Why does deploying a fleet across three availability zones instead of one improve reliability?

Physical independence between AZs is exactly what lets the surviving zones keep serving traffic during a localized failure.

10. Fill in the blank: storage durability's multiple data copies, load balancing's multiple servers, and multi-AZ deployment's multiple facilities are all specific applications of the same general principle: ___.

Redundancy — never relying on just one copy of anything critical — is the common thread across every one of these techniques.

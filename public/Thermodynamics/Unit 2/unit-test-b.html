1. A gas releases Q = −150 J of heat (heat leaves the gas) while W = −40 J (work is done on the gas). What is ΔU?

ΔU = Q − W = −150 − (−40) = −150 + 40 = −110 J.

2. How much heat is needed to raise 2.0 kg of water by 30°C? (c_water ≈ 4186 J/(kg·K))

Q = mcΔT = (2.0)(4186)(30) = 251,160 J ≈ 251 kJ.

3. In an isolated calorimetry mixture, if all else is equal, the substance with the smaller mass will experience:

Since Q = mcΔT, the same magnitude of heat exchanged spread across a smaller mass produces a proportionally larger ΔT.

4. n = 2.5 mol of an ideal gas is at P = 120,000 Pa and T = 320 K. What is its volume?

V = nRT/P = (2.5)(8.314)(320)/(120,000) = 6651.2/120,000 ≈ 0.0554 m³.

5. A process in which a gas's volume stays exactly constant throughout is called:

"Isochoric" (isovolumetric) means constant volume — isobaric is constant pressure, isothermal is constant temperature.

6. A gas undergoes a process with Q = −110 J and W = −180 J. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: ΔU for this process is ___ J.

(b) A second process reaches that same ΔU with Q = 0 instead. What must W be for this second process?

(a) ΔU = Q − W = −110 − (−180) = 70 J. (b) With Q = 0 and ΔU still 70 J: W = Q − ΔU = 0 − 70 = −70 J.

7. 0.4 kg of water at 100°C is fully vaporized into steam (L_v ≈ 2.26×10⁶ J/kg). Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the heat required is ___ kJ (whole number).

(b) Compared to melting that same 0.4 kg of ice (L_f ≈ 3.34×10⁵ J/kg), vaporizing it requires:

(a) Q = mL_v = (0.4)(2.26×10⁶) = 904,000 J = 904 kJ. (b) Melting requires Q = mL_f = (0.4)(3.34×10⁵) = 133,600 J ≈ 133.6 kJ — vaporization takes roughly 6.8 times more heat.

8. 0.3 kg of water at 80°C is mixed with 0.7 kg of water at 12°C in an insulated container. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the equilibrium temperature is T_f = ___ °C.

(b) Which sample experiences the larger temperature change?

(a) 0.3(80 − T_f) = 0.7(T_f − 12) → 24 − 0.3T_f = 0.7T_f − 8.4 → 32.4 = T_f. (b) The hot sample swings from 80 to 32.4 (ΔT = 47.6), while the cold sample swings from 12 to 32.4 (ΔT = 20.4) — the smaller mass changes more.

9. An ideal gas has P = 2×10⁵ Pa, V = 0.028 m³, and n = 1.5 mol. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: its temperature is T ≈ ___ K.

(b) If n were instead doubled while P and V stayed fixed, T would:

(a) T = PV/(nR) = (2×10⁵)(0.028)/[(1.5)(8.314)] = 5600/12.471 ≈ 449.0 K. (b) T is inversely proportional to n at fixed P and V, so doubling n halves T.

10. An ideal gas with n = 1 mol has P = 1.2×10⁵ Pa held fixed while its volume is 0.03 m³. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: at this instant, its temperature is T ≈ ___ K.

(b) Since P is held fixed as V changes, this process traces which shape on a PV diagram?

(a) T = PV/(nR) = (1.2×10⁵)(0.03)/[(1)(8.314)] = 3600/8.314 ≈ 433.0 K. (b) Constant pressure with changing volume is exactly the definition of isobaric — a horizontal line on a PV diagram.

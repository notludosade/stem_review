1. Fill in the blank: a kiln reads 200°F. Converted to Celsius, this is ___ °C (2 decimal places).

°C = (5/9)(°F − 32) = (5/9)(168) ≈ 93.33°C.

2. A sealed, rigid, thermally insulated container of gas is stirred by an internal paddle wheel driven by an external motor until its temperature rises. Treating the gas as the system, which best describes Q and W?

Insulation blocks heat regardless of the temperature rise, so Q = 0; the paddle wheel does mechanical work on the gas without the boundary moving, so W (work done by the gas) is negative.

3. A wall (k = 0.9 W/(m·K)) has area 2.5 m² and thickness 0.12 m, with a 25 K temperature difference across it. What is the conduction rate?

Q̇ = kAΔT/L = (0.9)(2.5)(25)/(0.12) = 56.25/0.12 = 468.75 W.

4. A gas absorbs Q = 430 J of heat while doing W = 180 J of work on its surroundings. What is ΔU?

ΔU = Q − W = 430 − 180 = 250 J.

5. Fill in the blank: melting 0.6 kg of ice (L_f ≈ 3.34×10⁵ J/kg) requires Q = ___ kJ (1 decimal place).

Q = mL_f = (0.6)(3.34×10⁵) = 200,400 J = 200.4 kJ.

6. n = 2.5 mol of an ideal gas is at P = 1.8×10⁵ Pa and T = 310 K. What is its volume?

V = nRT/P = (2.5)(8.314)(310)/(1.8×10⁵) ≈ 6443.4/180,000 ≈ 0.0358 m³.

7. A gas expands at a constant pressure P = 1.8×10⁵ Pa from V₁ = 0.012 m³ to V₂ = 0.03 m³. What is the work done by the gas?

W = PΔV = (1.8×10⁵)(0.03 − 0.012) = (1.8×10⁵)(0.018) = 3240 J.

8. Air (γ = 1.4) starts at P₁ = 9×10⁴ Pa, V₁ = 0.028 m³, and is adiabatically compressed to V₂ = 0.014 m³. What is P₂?

P₂ = P₁(V₁/V₂)ᵞ = (9×10⁴)(2)^1.4 ≈ (9×10⁴)(2.639) ≈ 2.38×10⁵ Pa.

9. Fill in the blank: a rectangular clockwise cycle has ΔV = 0.018 m³ and ΔP = 1.6×10⁵ Pa. The net work done by the gas is ___ J.

Net work = enclosed area = ΔV × ΔP = (0.018)(1.6×10⁵) = 2880 J.

10. A proposed device would move heat from a cold reservoir to a hot reservoir with no work input and no other effect. This directly violates:

The Clausius statement specifically forbids heat flowing "uphill," cold to hot, with no work input and no other effect.

11. A gas absorbs Q = 630 J of heat during a reversible isothermal process at T = 180 K. What is ΔS?

ΔS = Q/T = 630/180 = 3.5 J/K.

12. Fill in the blank: for 7 distinguishable particles independently placed in the left or right half of a box, the number of microstates matching "3 particles left, 4 right" is Ω = C(7,3) = ___.

C(7,3) = 7!/(3!4!) = 35.

13. An engine absorbs Q_h = 1200 J and exhausts Q_c = 780 J per cycle. What is its efficiency?

e = 1 − Q_c/Q_h = 1 − 780/1200 = 1 − 0.65 = 0.35.

14. A Carnot engine operates between T_h = 680 K and T_c = 408 K. What is e_Carnot?

e_Carnot = 1 − T_c/T_h = 1 − 408/680 = 1 − 0.60 = 0.40.

15. A refrigerator removes Q_c = 280 J using W = 70 J of work. What is COP_R?

COP_R = Q_c/W = 280/70 = 4.0.

16. In the van der Waals equation (P + an²/V²)(V − nb) = nRT, the constant b corrects for:

b represents the volume real molecules physically take up, shrinking the space available for them to move in — the a term is the attraction correction instead.

17. Fill in the blank: melting 0.35 kg of ice (L_f ≈ 3.34×10⁵ J/kg) requires Q = ___ kJ (1 decimal place).

Q = mL_f = (0.35)(3.34×10⁵) = 116,900 J = 116.9 kJ.

18. Fill in the blank: an engine operates between T_h = 580 K and T_c = 290 K, absorbing Q_h = 5200 J at actual efficiency e = 0.33 (so W = 1716 J, Q_c = 3484 J). The total entropy change ΔS_total = −Q_h/T_h + Q_c/T_c = ___ J/K (2 decimal places).

ΔS_total = −5200/580 + 3484/290 ≈ −8.966 + 12.014 ≈ 3.05 J/K — positive, confirming irreversibility, consistent with this engine's 33% falling below its e_Carnot = 1 − 290/580 = 50% ceiling.

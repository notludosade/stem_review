1. A dimension is written 32.00 ± 0.05mm. What is the lower limit?

Lower limit = nominal − tolerance = 32.00 − 0.05 = 31.95mm.

2. Fill in the blank: two dimensions stack end to end: 25.00 ± 0.06mm and 11.00 ± 0.02mm. The worst-case total tolerance is ___ mm (2 decimal places).

Worst-case stack-up sums the individual tolerances: 0.06 + 0.02 = 0.08mm.

3. A hole ranges from 8.00mm to 8.03mm. A shaft ranges from 7.95mm to 7.98mm. What type of fit is this?

Since the shaft is always smaller than the hole (7.98mm maximum vs. 8.00mm minimum), every valid pair has a guaranteed gap — a clearance fit.

4. Fill in the blank: in the United States, GD&T is formalized as the ASME ___ standard.

ASME Y14.5 is the standard governing Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing symbols and definitions.

5. Which GD&T control specifies how close a surface comes to exactly 90° relative to a datum?

Perpendicularity is an orientation tolerance, defining a real tolerance zone for how close to 90° a feature actually is.

6. Two dimensions stack end to end: 25.00 ± 0.06mm and 11.00 ± 0.02mm. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the RSS (statistical) stack-up estimate, √(0.06² + 0.02²), is ___ mm (2 decimal places).

(b) In which situation is the worst-case number the safer one to design against?

(a) √(0.06² + 0.02²) = √0.004 ≈ 0.06mm. (b) Worst-case is the guaranteed-safe bound, which matters most for small batches or safety-critical fits where the statistics of RSS are less trustworthy.

7. A design needs a bearing race permanently gripped inside its housing by friction alone, with no fasteners. Answer both parts:

(a) Which fit type guarantees this?

(b) Fill in the blank: if a clearance fit were mistakenly used instead, the race would ___ rather than stay gripped in place.

(a) Only an interference fit guarantees the friction grip a permanently pressed race requires. (b) A clearance fit's guaranteed gap would leave the race loose instead of gripped.

8. A hole is located with ±0.15mm in both x and y instead of true position tolerancing. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the maximum deviation allowed along the diagonal direction is √(0.15² + 0.15²) ≈ ___ mm (2 decimal places).

(b) Roughly what percentage more deviation does this allow along the diagonal compared to straight along either axis?

(a) √(0.15² + 0.15²) = √0.045 ≈ 0.21mm. (b) 0.21 / 0.15 ≈ 1.414 — about 41% more, the same ratio regardless of the tolerance's actual size, since it comes from √2.

9. A batch of assemblies passes inspection when checked against nominal dimensions alone, but a meaningful fraction fail to fit once fully assembled. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the most likely explanation?

(b) Fill in the blank: a dimension's target value, before tolerance is applied, is called its ___ size.

(a) Checking only nominal dimensions misses real-world variation that tolerance stack-up accounts for. (b) The target value is the nominal size.

10. A designer compares locating a hole with ± x/± y dimensions versus a true position callout. Answer both parts:

(a) What shape is the tolerance zone created by separate ± x and ± y dimensions?

(b) Fill in the blank: a true position tolerance is measured relative to one or more ___, giving it a fixed, unambiguous reference.

(a) Independent ± x and ± y dimensions bound a square region. (b) Position tolerances are always referenced to datums, which is part of what makes them unambiguous.

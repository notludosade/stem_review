1. What distinguishes a total conversion from an asset addition?

Total conversions sit at the far end of the modding spectrum, replacing nearly everything but the underlying engine.

2. Fill in the blank: the license agreement a player accepts on installing a game, setting the legal terms of use, is abbreviated ___.

A purchase grants a license under the EULA's terms, not ownership of the underlying copyrighted work.

3. Why do anti-cheat systems and modding often end up in tension?

Since both operate on the same low-level systems, competitive integrity concerns often push developers toward a stricter stance.

4. What is the primary engineering reason studios bundle game assets into one large archive file?

Performance — fast sequential reads — is the main engineering reason for archive formats; light obfuscation is a secondary effect.

5. Fill in the blank: the raw 3D geometry of a model — points, edges, and surfaces — is called its ___.

A mesh defines shape; a separate rig or skeleton defines how that shape moves.

6. Why are config-file edits generally the lowest-risk entry point to modding?

A plain-text config value is editable with nothing but a text editor, unlike binary assets or code.

7. Why is Lua the most common embedded scripting language across the games industry?

Lua's design goals — small, fast, embeddable — are why it dominates game modding, from World of Warcraft to Roblox.

8. Fill in the blank: a function registered to automatically run when a specific game event fires is called a ___.

A callback runs once its registered event fires, without the original game code needing to know a mod exists.

9. What does a modding framework provide on top of a game's raw scripting API?

Frameworks sit on top of the API, solving problems — like load order and dependencies — individual mods would otherwise each reinvent.

10. A replacement texture loads without error, but appears stretched and misplaced on the model. What is the most likely cause?

A valid image can still look wrong if it doesn't correspond correctly to the model's UV layout.

11. Fill in the blank: the metric combining a weapon's damage and attack speed into one number reflecting actual gameplay impact is ___.

DPS is what a careful balance pass actually compares, rather than raw damage numbers alone.

12. Why is redistributing a game's original licensed music inside a mod's download particularly risky?

Licensed music carries a separate rights layer beyond the game's own copyright.

13. What does "black-box observation" mean when applied to a compiled game?

A black box is understood only through observable inputs and outputs, since the original source isn't available.

14. Fill in the blank: before reaching for a decompiler or memory scanner, the highest-value debugging step is usually checking a game's ___.

A community wiki often already contains the answer, saved from someone else's earlier trial and error.

15. What specifically does DMCA Section 1201 make illegal?

Section 1201's trigger is defeating a technical protection, independent of whether the resulting use would otherwise be fair.

16. What should a mod's distributed package generally never include?

Bundling the original game's own files risks the redistribution problem Unit 1 first raised.

17. Fill in the blank: when two independently-made mods both modify the same underlying game element and something breaks, this is called a mod ___.

Realistic compatibility testing against popular mods is how conflicts like this get caught before a wide release.

18. According to the course's capstone lesson, what makes a mod truly "ready to publish responsibly"?

The capstone ties the technical skills from Units 2 through 6 back to the legal and ethical boundary Unit 1 opened with.

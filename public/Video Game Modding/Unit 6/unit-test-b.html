Unit 6 Test — Version B

Building & Sharing a Mod. 10 questions covering every lesson in this unit. Score 80% or higher to clear this unit.

1. What information does a mod manifest typically declare?

A manifest is metadata about the mod itself, distinct from its actual asset or script files.

2. Fill in the blank: under semantic versioning, incrementing the ___ version number signals a change that breaks compatibility with what came before.

MAJOR specifically signals a breaking change, distinct from MINOR (new feature) or PATCH (bug fix).

3. Two mods both hook the same event and both edit the same config value. What does this create a risk of?

Two mods touching the same underlying element is the definition of a mod conflict risk.

4. Fill in the blank: the formal software-engineering practice of checking that a change hasn't broken something that used to work is called ___ testing.

Mod compatibility testing is an informal version of this same idea, applied to combinations of mods.

5. What is a mod hosting platform?

Choosing a hosting platform is the practical first step of publishing a finished, packaged mod.

6. A modder is deciding what to include in a distribution package. Answer both parts:

(a) Should the package include the original game's own executable?

(b) What lets a mod loader identify and manage the package without a human reading the readme first?

Clean packages exclude the original game's own files and include a manifest for programmatic identification.

7. A new mod turns out to conflict with a very popular existing mod. Answer both parts:

(a) What kind of fix does Unit 4 already describe for exactly this situation?

(b) Where should this fix, and any other change, be documented for users?

A compatibility patch fixes the conflict; a changelog documents it for anyone comparing versions.

8. A capstone mod is ready for release. Answer both parts:

(a) What earlier unit's boundary does the publishing lesson explicitly circle back to?

(b) What must a mod avoid to stay within that boundary while publishing?

The capstone ties technical readiness directly back to the same legal boundary Unit 1 opened the course with.

9. A mod incorporates a texture pack originally made by a different modder. Answer both parts:

(a) What does responsible publishing require here?

(b) Besides fairness, what else can make attribution a real requirement rather than just a courtesy?

Attribution is often both an ethical norm and a literal license term the borrowed content was shared under.

10. A published mod starts receiving bug reports from users. Answer both parts:

(a) What does this lesson describe as the responsible response?

(b) Are these kinds of community norms written into a game's EULA?

Community norms operate alongside, but separately from, the formal legal terms covered earlier in the course.

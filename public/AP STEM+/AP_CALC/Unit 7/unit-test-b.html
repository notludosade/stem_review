1. A cup of tea cools at a rate proportional to the difference between its temperature T and the refrigerator's 40°F temperature. Which differential equation models this situation?

"Proportional to the difference between the temperature and the surrounding 40°" translates to k(T − 40); since the tea is cooling toward 40° rather than away from it, k < 0.

2. In the slope field for dy/dx = 2y − x, the segment at the point (4, 2) is:

2y − x = 2(2) − 4 = 0 at that point, so the segment is flat.

3. Fill in the blank: For dy/dx = x + y with y(0) = 1 and step size h = 0.25, one Euler step gives y(0.25) ≈ ___

The slope at (0, 1) is 0 + 1 = 1, so y₁ = y₀ + h·f(x₀, y₀) = 1 + 0.25(1) = 1.25.

4. Separating the differential equation dy/dx = y²x gives:

Dividing both sides by y² and multiplying by dx moves every y-term to the left and every x-term to the right, ready to integrate independently.

5. A quantity satisfying dy/dt = ky with k < 0 will:

In y = y₀e^(kt), a negative k shrinks the exponent's contribution as t grows, driving the quantity toward zero — the signature of decay.

6. Consider whether y = 5e^(2x) solves the differential equation y′ − 2y = 0. Answer both parts.

(a) What is y′?

(b) Fill in the blank: substituting y and y′ into y′ − 2y gives ___

(a) Differentiating 5e^(2x) with the chain rule gives 2 · 5e^(2x) = 10e^(2x). (b) Substituting: 10e^(2x) − 2(5e^(2x)) = 10e^(2x) − 10e^(2x) = 0, confirming y = 5e^(2x) is a solution.

7. For the slope field of dy/dx = 2y − x, answer both parts.

(a) At the point (1, 3), the slope shown by the field is:

(b) Fill in the blank: at the point (3, 1), the slope shown by the field is ___

(a) 2y − x = 2(3) − 1 = 5. (b) 2y − x = 2(1) − 3 = −1. Each segment's slope comes directly from evaluating 2y − x at that point.

8. Solve dy/dx = 4x³y with y(0) = 6. Answer both parts.

(a) Separating variables gives:

(b) Fill in the blank: applying y(0) = 6, the particular solution is y = ___ · e^(x⁴)

(a) Dividing by y and multiplying by dx separates all y-terms from all x-terms. (b) Integrating gives ln|y| = x⁴ + C₁, so y = Ae^(x⁴); substituting y(0) = 6 gives A = 6, so y = 6e^(x⁴).

9. A radioactive isotope decays at a rate proportional to the amount present. A sample starts at 300 grams and decays to 75 grams after 12 days. Answer both parts.

(a) Fill in the blank: the decay constant k ≈ ___ (round to three decimals, negative value)

(b) Fill in the blank: using that value of k, the amount remaining after 24 days is ___ grams

(a) 75 = 300e^(12k) → 0.25 = e^(12k) → k = ln(0.25)/12 ≈ −0.116. (b) Since 24k = 2ln(0.25), e^(24k) = 0.25² = 0.0625, so the amount is 300(0.0625) = 18.75 g — the sample quarters every 12 days, so it quarters twice over 24 days: 300 → 75 → 18.75.

10. A population is modeled by dP/dt = 0.6P(1 − P/1200). Answer both parts.

(a) The carrying capacity K is:

(b) Fill in the blank: growth is fastest at P = ___

(a) K is the constant directly inside the (1 − P/K) factor; here it's 1200. (b) The logistic curve's inflection point — where growth is fastest — occurs at P = K/2 = 1200/2 = 600.

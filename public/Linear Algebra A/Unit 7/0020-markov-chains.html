Markov Chains

A transition matrix moves a probability vector one time step at a time.

DefinitionFor column probability states xk, a column-stochastic matrix P has nonnegative entries and columns summing to 1. Evolution is xk+1=Pxk.
Steady StateA stationary distribution satisfies Pπ=π: it is a λ=1 eigenvector normalized so entries sum to 1.
Why This MattersWeather models, queues, ranking systems, and population movement reuse this one matrix model.

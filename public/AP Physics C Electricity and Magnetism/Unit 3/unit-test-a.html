1. A parallel-plate capacitor has A = 0.01 m² and d = 0.002 m. What is its capacitance? (ε₀ = 8.85×10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²))

C = ε₀A/d = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(0.01)/(0.002) = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(5) = 44.25×10⁻¹² F ≈ 44.3 pF.

2. If a parallel-plate capacitor's plate area A is doubled while separation d stays fixed, its capacitance:

C = ε₀A/d is directly proportional to A, so doubling the area doubles the capacitance.

3. A 6 μF capacitor is charged to 40 V. How much energy does it store?

U = ½CV² = ½(6×10⁻⁶)(40)² = ½(6×10⁻⁶)(1600) = ½(0.0096) = 0.0048 J = 4.8 mJ.

4. A dielectric is inserted into an isolated capacitor (battery disconnected first, so Q is fixed). The voltage:

With Q fixed, capacitance rises to κC₀, so V = Q/C falls to V₀/κ.

5. A κ = 2 dielectric is inserted into a capacitor that stays connected to its battery (V held fixed). The stored energy:

With V fixed, C rises to κC₀ = 2C₀, and U = ½CV² rises proportionally with C — the battery supplies the extra energy.

6. A parallel-plate capacitor has A = 0.04 m² and d = 0.001 m. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: its capacitance is ___ pF.

(b) If d were instead doubled (A unchanged), the new capacitance would be:

(a) C = ε₀A/d = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(0.04)/(0.001) = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(40) = 354×10⁻¹² F = 354 pF. (b) C ∝ 1/d, so doubling d halves C.

7. A 10 μF capacitor is charged to 30 V. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the energy stored is ___ J.

(b) If the voltage were instead doubled (capacitance unchanged), the new stored energy would be:

(a) U = ½CV² = ½(10×10⁻⁶)(30)² = ½(10×10⁻⁶)(900) = ½(0.009) = 0.0045 J = 4.5 mJ. (b) U ∝ V², so doubling V quadruples U.

8. An isolated capacitor (Q fixed) has C₀ = 8 pF. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: after a κ = 5 dielectric is inserted, the new capacitance is ___ pF.

(b) The electric field between the plates, compared to before the dielectric was inserted:

(a) C = κC₀ = 5(8 pF) = 40 pF. (b) With Q fixed, V falls to V₀/5, and since E = V/d with d unchanged, E also falls to E₀/5.

9. A capacitor stays connected to a battery (V fixed) and initially holds Q₀ = 3 nC. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: after a κ = 6 dielectric is inserted, the new charge is ___ nC.

(b) The energy stored in the capacitor, compared to before the dielectric was inserted:

(a) With V fixed, Q = CV rises to κQ₀ = 6(3 nC) = 18 nC. (b) U = ½CV² rises along with C (V fixed), and that extra energy is drawn from the battery as it pushes the extra charge onto the plates.

10. A capacitor has C = 8 μF and V = 25 V. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the charge stored is ___ μC.

(b) If the charge on this same capacitor (C unchanged) were instead doubled, the voltage would:

(a) Q = CV = (8 μF)(25 V) = 200 μC. (b) With C fixed, V = Q/C is directly proportional to Q, so doubling Q doubles V.

1. A parallel-plate capacitor has A = 0.02 m² and d = 0.0025 m. What is its capacitance? (ε₀ = 8.85×10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²))

C = ε₀A/d = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(0.02)/(0.0025) = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(8) = 70.8×10⁻¹² F ≈ 70.8 pF.

2. If a parallel-plate capacitor's plate area A is tripled while separation d stays fixed, its capacitance:

C = ε₀A/d is directly proportional to A, so tripling the area triples the capacitance.

3. A 4 μF capacitor is charged to 60 V. How much energy does it store?

U = ½CV² = ½(4×10⁻⁶)(60)² = ½(4×10⁻⁶)(3600) = ½(0.0144) = 0.0072 J = 7.2 mJ.

4. A dielectric is inserted into an isolated capacitor (battery disconnected first, so Q is fixed). The electric field between the plates:

With Q fixed, V falls to V₀/κ, and since E = V/d with d unchanged, E falls to E₀/κ as well.

5. A κ = 3 dielectric is inserted into a capacitor that stays connected to its battery (V held fixed). The stored energy:

With V fixed, C rises to κC₀ = 3C₀, and U = ½CV² rises proportionally with C — the battery supplies the extra energy.

6. A parallel-plate capacitor has A = 0.05 m² and d = 0.002 m. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: its capacitance is ___ pF.

(b) If d were instead tripled (A unchanged), the new capacitance would be:

(a) C = ε₀A/d = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(0.05)/(0.002) = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(25) = 221.25×10⁻¹² F ≈ 221 pF. (b) C ∝ 1/d, so tripling d cuts C to a third.

7. A 5 μF capacitor is charged to 60 V. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the energy stored is ___ J.

(b) If the voltage were instead tripled (capacitance unchanged), the new stored energy would be:

(a) U = ½CV² = ½(5×10⁻⁶)(60)² = ½(5×10⁻⁶)(3600) = ½(0.018) = 0.009 J = 9 mJ. (b) U ∝ V², so tripling V multiplies U by 9.

8. An isolated capacitor (Q fixed) has C₀ = 6 pF. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: after a κ = 7 dielectric is inserted, the new capacitance is ___ pF.

(b) The voltage across the plates, compared to before the dielectric was inserted:

(a) C = κC₀ = 7(6 pF) = 42 pF. (b) With Q fixed, V = Q/C falls to V₀/7 once C rises sevenfold.

9. A capacitor stays connected to a battery (V fixed) and initially holds Q₀ = 4 nC. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: after a κ = 5 dielectric is inserted, the new charge is ___ nC.

(b) The energy stored in the capacitor, compared to before the dielectric was inserted:

(a) With V fixed, Q = CV rises to κQ₀ = 5(4 nC) = 20 nC. (b) U = ½CV² rises along with C (V fixed), and that extra energy is drawn from the battery as it pushes the extra charge onto the plates.

10. A capacitor has C = 12 μF and V = 15 V. Answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: the charge stored is ___ μC.

(b) If the charge on this same capacitor (C unchanged) were instead tripled, the voltage would:

(a) Q = CV = (12 μF)(15 V) = 180 μC. (b) With C fixed, V = Q/C is directly proportional to Q, so tripling Q triples V.

1. What is |⟨5, 12⟩|?

|⟨5,12⟩| = √(5² + 12²) = √(25+144) = √169 = 13.

2. Fill in the blank: for v = ⟨2, 3, 6⟩, |v| = ___

|v| = √(2² + 3² + 6²) = √(4+9+36) = √49 = 7.

3. For u = ⟨u₁, u₂, u₃⟩ and v = ⟨v₁, v₂, v₃⟩, u · v equals:

The dot product multiplies matching components and adds them into a single scalar — never a vector.

4. For u = ⟨u₁, u₂, u₃⟩ and v = ⟨v₁, v₂, v₃⟩, u × v equals:

The first component is u₂v₃ − u₃v₂ — flipping that sign or reordering the three components produces the wrong vector entirely.

5. Geometrically, |u × v| represents:

|u × v| = |u||v|sin θ — base times height of the parallelogram the two vectors span.

6. For u = ⟨2, -1⟩ and v = ⟨3, 1⟩, answer both parts:

(a) u + v equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: 3u − v = ___ (format: <a,b>)

(a) u + v = ⟨2+3, -1+1⟩ = ⟨5, 0⟩. (b) 3u = ⟨6, -3⟩, so 3u − v = ⟨6−3, -3−1⟩ = ⟨3, -4⟩.

7. For u = ⟨6, 3⟩ and v = ⟨1, -2⟩, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: u · v = ___

(b) Based on that, u and v are:

(a) u · v = (6)(1) + (3)(-2) = 6 − 6 = 0. (b) A zero dot product between two nonzero vectors means they are orthogonal.

8. For u = ⟨2, 0⟩ and v = ⟨0, -3⟩, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: u · v = ___

(b) The angle θ between u and v is:

(a) u · v = (2)(0) + (0)(-3) = 0. (b) cos θ = 0/(2·3) = 0, so θ = 90° — one vector lies purely on the x-axis, the other purely on the y-axis.

9. For u = ⟨1, 2, 0⟩ and v = ⟨0, 1, 3⟩, answer both parts:

(a) The x-component of u × v (u₂v₃ − u₃v₂) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: u × v = ___ (format: <a,b,c>)

(a) u₂v₃ − u₃v₂ = (2)(3) − (0)(1) = 6. (b) Full computation: ⟨(2)(3)−(0)(1), (0)(0)−(1)(3), (1)(1)−(2)(0)⟩ = ⟨6, -3, 1⟩.

10. A plane passes through the point (2, -1, 0) with normal vector ⟨1, 3, -2⟩. Answer both parts:

(a) The general plane equation, given a point (x₀,y₀,z₀) and normal ⟨a,b,c⟩, is:

(b) Fill in the blank: simplified, this plane's equation is x + 3y − 2z = ___

1(x−2) + 3(y+1) − 2(z−0) = 0 → x − 2 + 3y + 3 − 2z = 0 → x + 3y − 2z + 1 = 0 → x + 3y − 2z = -1.

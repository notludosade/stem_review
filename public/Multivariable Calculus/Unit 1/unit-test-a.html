1. What is |⟨6, 8⟩|?

|⟨6,8⟩| = √(6² + 8²) = √(36+64) = √100 = 10.

2. Fill in the blank: for v = ⟨2, -1, 2⟩, |v| = ___

|v| = √(2² + (-1)² + 2²) = √(4+1+4) = √9 = 3.

3. For u = ⟨u₁, u₂, u₃⟩ and v = ⟨v₁, v₂, v₃⟩, u · v equals:

The dot product multiplies matching components and adds — always a single scalar, never a vector.

4. For u = ⟨u₁, u₂, u₃⟩ and v = ⟨v₁, v₂, v₃⟩, u × v equals:

The middle component is u₃v₁ − u₁v₃, not u₁v₃ − u₃v₁ — a sign easy to flip by mixing up the cyclic pattern.

5. Geometrically, |u × v| equals:

|u × v| = |u||v|sin θ, which is base times height for the parallelogram the two vectors span.

6. For u = ⟨1, 3⟩ and v = ⟨-2, 4⟩, answer both parts:

(a) u + v equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: 2u − v = ___ (format: <a,b>)

(a) u + v = ⟨1+(-2), 3+4⟩ = ⟨-1, 7⟩. (b) 2u = ⟨2, 6⟩, so 2u − v = ⟨2−(-2), 6−4⟩ = ⟨4, 2⟩.

7. For u = ⟨5, -2⟩ and v = ⟨3, 4⟩, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: u · v = ___

(b) Based on that, u and v are:

(a) u · v = (5)(3) + (-2)(4) = 15 − 8 = 7. (b) Since u · v = 7 ≠ 0, the vectors are not orthogonal.

8. For u = ⟨1, 0⟩ and v = ⟨0, 5⟩, answer both parts:

(a) Fill in the blank: u · v = ___

(b) The angle θ between u and v is:

(a) u · v = (1)(0) + (0)(5) = 0. (b) cos θ = 0/(1·5) = 0, so θ = 90° — u and v are orthogonal, matching their directions along the two axes.

9. For u = ⟨2, 1, 0⟩ and v = ⟨0, 3, 1⟩, answer both parts:

(a) The x-component of u × v (u₂v₃ − u₃v₂) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: u × v = ___ (format: <a,b,c>)

(a) u₂v₃ − u₃v₂ = (1)(1) − (0)(3) = 1. (b) Full computation: ⟨(1)(1)−(0)(3), (0)(0)−(2)(1), (2)(3)−(1)(0)⟩ = ⟨1, -2, 6⟩.

10. A plane passes through the point (1, 2, 3) with normal vector ⟨2, 0, -1⟩. Answer both parts:

(a) The general plane equation, given a point (x₀,y₀,z₀) and normal ⟨a,b,c⟩, is:

(b) Fill in the blank: simplified, this plane's equation is 2x − z = ___

2(x−1) + 0(y−2) − 1(z−3) = 0 → 2x − 2 − z + 3 = 0 → 2x − z + 1 = 0 → 2x − z = -1.

Optimization with Lagrange Multipliers

At a constrained extremum, objective and constraint level curves are tangent.

← Lesson 22 Lesson 24 → Contents Glossary →

Lagrange ConditionTo optimize f subject to g=c, solve ∇f=λ∇g together with g=c, then compare all candidates.
Worked ExampleMaximize f=xy on x²+y²=1. Equations y=2λx and x=2λy imply x=±y. The maximum value 1/2 occurs at (±1/√2, ±1/√2) with matching signs.
Why This MattersEngineering design rarely permits free movement; cost, material, energy, and geometry impose constraints.

1. Which of these is an example of unstructured data?

Free text has no predefined fields or format, unlike a column of numbers, flags, or dates that fit neatly into rows and columns.

2. Fill in the blank: data describing a quality or category, such as color or country, rather than a numeric amount, is called ___ data.

Qualitative (categorical) data captures a label or category, in contrast to quantitative data, which captures a measurable amount.

3. Which spreadsheet function counts only cells containing numeric values, ignoring blanks and text?

COUNT only tallies numeric cells; COUNTA counts any non-blank cell regardless of type, including text.

4. Fill in the blank: =___(range, criteria) counts only the cells in range that match criteria.

COUNTIF adds a condition on top of a plain count, tallying only cells meeting the given criteria.

5. A Students table needs a primary key. Which column is the right choice?

A primary key must uniquely identify every row and never be blank — Student ID is the only column here that guarantees that.

6. Fill in the blank: storing a Customer ID in the Orders table, instead of repeating each customer's full name and address on every order row, is an example of ___ — splitting data into linked tables so any fact is stored in exactly one place.

Normalization avoids repeating the same fact in multiple rows by linking tables through keys instead of duplicating data.

7. A small company has nine employees earning $40,000 each and one executive earning $1,040,000, making the mean salary $140,000. Why is the median a more representative "typical" salary here?

The mean is sensitive to extreme values; the median, based on rank order, stays anchored to where most values actually sit.

8. Fill in the blank: the measure of spread computed as Q3 minus Q1, which — like the median — resists distortion by outliers unlike the plain range or standard deviation, is the ___.

The interquartile range only looks at the middle 50% of the data, so extreme outliers barely move it.

9. A dataset's "age" column contains several entries of "999" where the real age was never recorded. Why is this more dangerous than a true blank cell?

A sentinel value hides in plain sight as a plausible number, unlike a blank cell that most tools already know to treat specially.

10. Fill in the blank: a survey's income question is disproportionately skipped by the very highest earners — the chance of the value being missing is related to the value itself. This category of missingness is abbreviated ___.

MAR (missing at random, conditional on other observed data) fits when missingness relates to the value itself rather than being purely random.

11. A retailer wants to show how its total monthly revenue changed over the past two years. Which chart type fits this question best?

A line chart is built for showing change over a continuous axis like time — exactly the trend being asked about.

12. Fill in the blank: an analyst wants to show the relationship between hours of sunlight and daily ice cream sales across many days. The chart type best suited to this is a ___.

A scatter plot shows each observation as a point along two numeric axes, making a relationship between two variables visible directly.

13. Which of these is an indirect identifier (quasi-identifier) rather than a direct identifier?

A ZIP code alone rarely identifies someone, but combined with other quasi-identifiers like birth date it can — that's what makes it indirect rather than direct.

14. Fill in the blank: any data that can identify a specific individual, either on its own or combined with other data, is called ___.

PII covers both direct identifiers on their own and quasi-identifiers that identify someone only in combination.

15. Which data role most directly builds and maintains the pipelines and databases that get raw data into a queryable, analysis-ready state?

Data Engineers focus on the infrastructure and pipelines that move and prepare data, distinct from analyzing it once it's ready.

16. Fill in the blank: documenting every cleaning decision you made on a dataset, and why, in a running ___ is considered a best practice worth including in a data portfolio.

A cleaning log makes cleaning decisions transparent and reproducible, which matters both for collaborators and for a portfolio reviewer.

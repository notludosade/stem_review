1. Light with frequency 5×10¹⁴ Hz travels through vacuum. What is its wavelength?

λ = c/f = (3×10⁸)/(5×10¹⁴) = 6×10⁻⁷ m = 600 nm.

2. Huygens' principle constructs a new wavefront from secondary wavelets. What geometric surface defines that new wavefront?

The tangent envelope touching every wavelet — not a midpoint or single wavelet — is the new wavefront.

3. Light in air (n = 1.00) strikes glass (n = 1.50) at an incidence angle of 50°. What is the refraction angle?

sinθ₂ = (1.00/1.50) sin50° = (0.667)(0.766) ≈ 0.511, so θ₂ ≈ 30.7°.

4. Fill in the blank: the time required for one complete wave cycle is called its ___.

The period T is the time for one cycle; frequency f = 1/T is its reciprocal.

5. Which fact about reflected wavelets, in the Huygens construction, is what keeps the angle of reflection exactly equal to the angle of incidence?

No speed mismatch occurs for reflection since the wave never crosses into a new medium — that's why there's no bending, unlike refraction.

6. A wave has frequency f = 25 Hz. Answer both parts:

(a) What is its period?

(b) Fill in the blank: if this wave travels at 340 m/s, its wavelength is ___ m.

(a) T = 1/f = 1/25 = 0.04 s. (b) λ = v/f = 340/25 = 13.6 m.

7. Huygens' construction is applied at the edge of a narrow opening (a slit) rather than in open space. Answer both parts:

(a) What phenomenon does this predict, that a strict straight-ray picture would not?

(b) Fill in the blank: the surface tangent to every secondary wavelet is called the ___.

(a) Applying wavelet sources right at a slit's edge predicts bending into regions a ray picture forbids — diffraction, covered fully in Unit 2. (b) This tangent surface, the envelope, is the new wavefront.

8. Light travels from water (n = 1.33) into air (n = 1.00) at an incidence angle of 25°, measured from the normal inside the water. Answer both parts:

(a) Compared to the incidence angle, the refraction angle in air will be:

(b) Fill in the blank: sinθ₂ = (1.33/1.00) sin25° ≈ ___ (round to 2 decimal places).

(a) Leaving a denser medium for a less-dense one always bends the ray away from the normal. (b) sinθ₂ = 1.33 × sin25° = 1.33 × 0.423 ≈ 0.56, giving θ₂ ≈ 34.1°.

9. A medium has index of refraction n = 2.00. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the speed of light in this medium?

(b) Fill in the blank: a medium's index of refraction n is always ___ 1 (greater than, less than, or equal to).

(a) v = c/n = (3×10⁸)/2.00 = 1.5×10⁸ m/s. (b) Since light never travels faster in a medium than in vacuum, n = c/v is always ≥ 1.

10. A light ray strikes a water surface, producing both a reflected ray and a refracted ray. Answer both parts:

(a) Which ray's direction is governed by Snell's law, n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂?

(b) Fill in the blank: the law stating the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection is the law of ___.

(a) Snell's law governs refraction specifically; reflection instead follows the simpler law of reflection. (b) This is the law of reflection, θᵢ = θᵣ.

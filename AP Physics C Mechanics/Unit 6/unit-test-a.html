1. The general solution to d²x/dt² = −ω²x is:

A second-order equation needs two constants; A sin(ωt) is only the special case where φ = −π/2, matching x(0) = 0.

2. Fill in the blank: a mass m = 5 kg is attached to a spring with k = 180 N/m. Its angular frequency ω = ___ rad/s.

ω = √(k/m) = √(180/5) = √36 = 6 rad/s.

3. At maximum displacement (x = A) during SHM, the energy split is:

At x = A, velocity is momentarily zero, so all the energy ½kA² is stored as potential energy.

4. Which damping regime returns to equilibrium without any oscillation, but more slowly than the fastest possible non-oscillating return?

Overdamped systems return to equilibrium without oscillating, but more slowly than critical damping, which is the fastest non-oscillating case.

5. Fill in the blank: resonance — a dramatic buildup in amplitude — occurs when the driving frequency matches the system's ___ frequency.

Driving a system at or near its own natural frequency lets energy build up efficiently cycle after cycle.

6. Consider x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ). Answer both parts:

(a) d²x/dt² equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: if the period of oscillation is T = (2π)/3 s, then ω = ___ rad/s.

(a) Two derivatives of cosine bring back −ω² times the original function. (b) ω = 2π/T = 2π/(2π/3) = 3 rad/s.

7. A mass oscillates with amplitude A = 0.15 m, spring constant k = 60 N/m, and mass m = 2.4 kg. Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula gives the maximum speed in SHM?

(b) Fill in the blank: vmax = ___ m/s.

(a) v_max = Aω. (b) ω = √(k/m) = √(60/2.4) = √25 = 5 rad/s, so v_max = 0.15(5) = 0.75 m/s.

8. A spring has k = 40 N/m and amplitude A = 0.3 m. Answer both parts:

(a) The total mechanical energy of the oscillator is given by:

(b) Fill in the blank: E = ___ J.

(a) E = ½kA². (b) E = ½(40)(0.3²) = ½(40)(0.09) = 1.8 J.

9. An object is released from rest at its maximum positive displacement. Answer both parts:

(a) What is φ in x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ) for this initial condition?

(b) If k = 98 N/m and m = 2 kg, fill in the blank: ω = ___ rad/s.

(a) x(0) = A cos(φ) = A requires cos(φ) = 1, so φ = 0. (b) ω = √(98/2) = √49 = 7 rad/s.

(b) Fill in the blank: the exponential factor e−γt in an underdamped solution causes the oscillation's ___ to shrink over time.

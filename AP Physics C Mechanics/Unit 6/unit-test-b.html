1. Why is x(t) = A sin(ωt) not the correct general solution to d²x/dt² = −ω²x?

The general solution x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ) needs a phase constant to match arbitrary initial conditions; A sin(ωt) only works when x(0) = 0.

2. Fill in the blank: a mass m = 8 kg is attached to a spring with k = 200 N/m. Its angular frequency ω = ___ rad/s.

ω = √(k/m) = √(200/8) = √25 = 5 rad/s.

3. At the equilibrium position (x = 0) during SHM, the energy split is:

At x = 0, U = ½kx² = 0 and speed is at its maximum, so all the energy is kinetic.

4. Which damping regime returns to equilibrium fastest while still avoiding oscillation?

Critical damping is the boundary case: the fastest possible return to equilibrium without any oscillation.

5. Fill in the blank: resonance — a dramatic buildup in amplitude — occurs when the driving frequency matches the system's ___ frequency.

Driving a system at or near its own natural frequency lets energy build up efficiently cycle after cycle.

6. Consider x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ). Answer both parts:

(a) d²x/dt² equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: if the period of oscillation is T = π/2 s, then ω = ___ rad/s.

(a) Two derivatives of cosine bring back −ω² times the original function. (b) ω = 2π/T = 2π/(π/2) = 4 rad/s.

7. A mass oscillates with amplitude A = 0.2 m, spring constant k = 90 N/m, and mass m = 3.6 kg. Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula gives the maximum speed in SHM?

(b) Fill in the blank: vmax = ___ m/s.

(a) v_max = Aω. (b) ω = √(90/3.6) = √25 = 5 rad/s, so v_max = 0.2(5) = 1 m/s.

8. A spring has k = 25 N/m and amplitude A = 0.4 m. Answer both parts:

(a) The total mechanical energy of the oscillator is given by:

(b) Fill in the blank: E = ___ J.

(a) E = ½kA². (b) E = ½(25)(0.4²) = ½(25)(0.16) = 2 J.

9. An object is released from rest at its maximum negative displacement (x₀ = −A). Answer both parts:

(a) What is φ in x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ) for this initial condition?

(b) If k = 72 N/m and m = 2 kg, fill in the blank: ω = ___ rad/s.

(a) x(0) = A cos(φ) = −A requires cos(φ) = −1, so φ = π. (b) ω = √(72/2) = √36 = 6 rad/s.

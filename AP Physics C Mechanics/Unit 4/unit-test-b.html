1. Fill in the blank: a force F(t) = 80t N acts on an object from t = 0 to t = 0.3 s. The impulse delivered is ___ N·s.

J = ∫₀^0.3 80t dt = 40t² |₀^0.3 = 40(0.09) = 3.6 N·s.

2. F = dp/dt reduces to the familiar ΣF = ma only when:

d(mv)/dt = m(dv/dt) + v(dm/dt); the second term vanishes only when dm/dt = 0, leaving F = m(dv/dt) = ma.

3. Which law, integrated over the interaction time, gives Δp₁ = −Δp₂ for an isolated two-object system?

F₁₂(t) = −F₂₁(t) at every instant by Newton's third law; integrating both sides over time gives Δp₁ = −Δp₂.

4. For a continuous object, the discrete sum xcm = Σmixi/Σmi generalizes to which integral?

Each infinitesimal mass element dm at position x replaces a discrete m_ix_i term, divided by total mass M = ∫dm.

5. Fill in the blank: expanding d(mv)/dt into m(dv/dt) + v(dm/dt) uses the ___, since both m and v can depend on time.

The product rule differentiates p = mv when neither factor is held constant, which is why a rocket's thrust term appears.

6. A 5 kg cart moving at 8 m/s collides and sticks to a stationary 3 kg cart. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the total momentum before the collision?

(b) Fill in the blank: their combined final velocity is ___ m/s.

(a) p = 5(8) + 3(0) = 40 kg·m/s. (b) v_f = p/(m₁+m₂) = 40/8 = 5 m/s.

7. A 2 kg ball moving at 9 m/s elastically collides with a stationary 4 kg ball. Answer both parts:

(a) Which pair of equations gives the two objects' final velocities in an elastic collision?

(b) Fill in the blank: the 4 kg ball's final velocity v₂′ = ___ m/s.

(a) These formulas jointly satisfy both momentum and kinetic energy conservation. (b) v₂′ = ((4−2)(0) + 2(2)(9))/6 = 36/6 = 6 m/s.

8. A 0.8 kg object initially moving at 3 m/s receives an impulse of 8 N·s. Answer both parts:

(a) Impulse J equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the object's final velocity is ___ m/s.

(a) J = Δp by the impulse-momentum theorem. (b) 8 = 0.8(v_f − 3) → v_f − 3 = 10 → v_f = 13 m/s.

9. A rod of length 4 m has linear density λ(x) = 3x kg/m, measured from x = 0. Answer both parts:

(a) Which integral gives the rod's total mass M?

(b) Fill in the blank: M = ___ kg.

(a) M = ∫λ(x)dx = ∫₀⁴ 3x dx. (b) M = 1.5x² |₀⁴ = 1.5(16) = 24 kg.

10. A rocket has exhaust speed ve = 3000 m/s, initial mass m₀ = 6000 kg, and final mass mf = 3000 kg (so m₀/mf = 2; use ln 2 ≈ 0.693). Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula gives the rocket's change in speed?

(b) Fill in the blank: Δv ≈ ___ m/s.

(a) The Tsiolkovsky rocket equation. (b) Δv = 3000 × 0.693 ≈ 2079 m/s.

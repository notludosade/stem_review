1. Fill in the blank: a force F(t) = 60t N acts on an object from t = 0 to t = 0.4 s. The impulse delivered is ___ N·s.

J = ∫₀^0.4 60t dt = 30t² |₀^0.4 = 30(0.16) = 4.8 N·s.

2. Why is F = dp/dt considered more general than ΣF = ma?

d(mv)/dt = m(dv/dt) + v(dm/dt) by the product rule; only when dm/dt = 0 does this collapse to ma.

3. Momentum conservation in an isolated two-object system follows most directly from:

F₁₂(t) = −F₂₁(t) at every instant; integrating both sides over time gives Δp₁ = −Δp₂.

4. For a continuous object, the discrete sum xcm = Σmixi/Σmi generalizes to which integral?

Each infinitesimal mass element dm at position x replaces a discrete m_ix_i term, divided by total mass M = ∫dm.

5. Fill in the blank: expanding d(mv)/dt into m(dv/dt) + v(dm/dt) uses the ___, since both m and v can depend on time.

The product rule differentiates p = mv when neither factor is held constant, which is why a rocket's thrust term appears.

6. A 4 kg cart moving at 6 m/s collides and sticks to a stationary 2 kg cart. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the total momentum before the collision?

(b) Fill in the blank: their combined final velocity is ___ m/s.

(a) p = 4(6) + 2(0) = 24 kg·m/s. (b) v_f = p/(m₁+m₂) = 24/6 = 4 m/s.

7. A 1 kg ball moving at 6 m/s elastically collides with a stationary 2 kg ball. Answer both parts:

(a) Which pair of equations gives the two objects' final velocities in an elastic collision?

(b) Fill in the blank: the 2 kg ball's final velocity v₂′ = ___ m/s.

(a) These formulas jointly satisfy both momentum and kinetic energy conservation. (b) v₂′ = ((2−1)(0) + 2(1)(6))/3 = 12/3 = 4 m/s.

8. A 0.5 kg object initially moving at 2 m/s receives an impulse of 6 N·s. Answer both parts:

(a) Impulse J equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the object's final velocity is ___ m/s.

(a) J = Δp by the impulse-momentum theorem. (b) 6 = 0.5(v_f − 2) → v_f − 2 = 12 → v_f = 14 m/s.

9. A rod of length 3 m has linear density λ(x) = 2x kg/m, measured from x = 0. Answer both parts:

(a) Which integral gives the rod's total mass M?

(b) Fill in the blank: M = ___ kg.

(a) M = ∫λ(x)dx = ∫₀³ 2x dx. (b) M = x² |₀³ = 9 kg.

10. A rocket has exhaust speed ve = 2500 m/s, initial mass m₀ = 8000 kg, and final mass mf = 4000 kg (so m₀/mf = 2; use ln 2 ≈ 0.693). Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula gives the rocket's change in speed?

(b) Fill in the blank: Δv ≈ ___ m/s.

(a) The Tsiolkovsky rocket equation. (b) Δv = 2500 × 0.693 ≈ 1732.5 m/s.

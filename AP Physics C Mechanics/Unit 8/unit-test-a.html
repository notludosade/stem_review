1. A problem asks only for a cart's speed after sliding down a frictionless hill of known height h, with the exact hill shape not given. The fastest method is:

mgh = ½mv² gives the answer directly — the hill's exact shape never enters the calculation.

2. Fill in the blank: the derivation of the work-energy theorem changes the integration variable from x to v, which is why the resulting theorem contains no direct information about ___.

Switching the variable of integration away from x (and never introducing t) is exactly why energy methods can't directly answer "how long."

3. Fill in the blank: a wheel of radius 0.3 m rolls without slipping with angular velocity 8 rad/s. Its translational speed is ___ m/s.

v = Rω = 0.3(8) = 2.4 m/s.

4. Of a solid sphere, a solid cylinder, and a thin hoop, all released from the same height on the same incline, which reaches the bottom first?

The solid sphere has the smallest shape factor c = 2/5, giving it the largest acceleration a = g sinθ/(1+c).

5. Which best describes the relationship between AP Physics 1 and this course?

Every Physics 1 formula reappears here as a special case of a more general calculus-based result.

6. A thin hoop (c = 1) rolls from rest down a 5 m tall incline (g = 9.8 m/s²). Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula gives its final speed at the bottom?

(b) Fill in the blank: v = ___ m/s.

(a) Mgh = ½Mv²(1+c) → v = √(2gh/(1+c)). (b) v = √(2(9.8)(5)/2) = √49 = 7 m/s.

7. A solid sphere (c = 2/5) rolls from rest down a 30° incline (g = 9.8 m/s², sin 30° = 0.5). Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula gives its acceleration?

(b) Fill in the blank: a = ___ m/s².

(a) Mg sinθ − f = Ma and fR = Iα combine to give a = g sinθ/(1+c). (b) a = 9.8(0.5)/1.4 = 4.9/1.4 = 3.5 m/s².

9. A solid sphere (c = 0.4) and a thin hoop (c = 1) roll down the same incline from rest. Answer both parts:

(a) Which has the smaller shape factor c, and therefore reaches the bottom faster?

(b) Fill in the blank: the ratio asphere/ahoop = ___ (to 2 decimal places).

(a) Smaller c means larger a = g sinθ/(1+c). (b) a_sphere/a_hoop = (1+c_hoop)/(1+c_sphere) = 2/1.4 ≈ 1.43.

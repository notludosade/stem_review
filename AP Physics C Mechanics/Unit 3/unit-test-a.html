1. Fill in the blank: a force F(x) = 5x N acts on an object from x = 0 to x = 3 m. The work done is ___ J.

W = ∫₀³ 5x dx = 2.5x² |₀³ = 2.5(9) = 22.5 J.

2. A 2 kg object starts at rest, and a net force does 16 J of work on it. What is its final speed?

W = ½mv² − 0 → 16 = ½(2)v² → v² = 16 → v = 4 m/s.

3. In deriving the work-energy theorem, ∫a dx is rewritten as ∫v dv. Which substitution makes this possible?

Since v = dx/dt, dx = v dt. Substituting this into a dx gives a(v dt) = v(a dt) = v dv, since dv = a dt.

4. A potential energy function is U(x) = 6x². What is F(2)?

F(x) = −dU/dx = −12x. F(2) = −24 N.

5. Fill in the blank: a force whose work depends on the path taken, not just the start and end points, is called ___.

Non-conservative forces (like kinetic friction) do path-dependent work, which is exactly why no single-valued potential energy function can be built for them.

6. A spring has k = 100 N/m and is stretched from its natural length to x = 0.3 m. Answer both parts:

(a) The work done stretching a spring from its natural length to displacement x is given by:

(b) Fill in the blank: the work done is ___ J.

(a) W = ∫₀ˣ kx′ dx′ = ½kx². (b) W = ½(100)(0.3²) = ½(100)(0.09) = 4.5 J.

7. An object of mass 5 kg, initially at rest, has 90 J of net work done on it. Answer both parts:

(a) Which equation relates the net work W to the final speed v?

(b) Fill in the blank: the final speed is ___ m/s.

(a) The work-energy theorem: W = ½mv² − ½mv₀². (b) With v₀ = 0: 90 = ½(5)v² → v² = 36 → v = 6 m/s.

8. Answer both parts:

(a) Which of the following is a conservative force?

(b) A potential energy function is U(x) = 3x² − 2x. Fill in the blank: F(1) = ___ N.

(a) Gravity's work depends only on the height change, never on the path — the definition of conservative. (b) F(x) = −dU/dx = −(6x − 2). F(1) = −(6 − 2) = −4 N.

9. A potential energy function is U(x) = x² − 4x + 5. Answer both parts:

(a) Its one equilibrium point is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the equilibrium occurs at x = ___ m.

(a) U(x) = x² − 4x + 5 opens upward, so its critical point is a minimum — a stable equilibrium. (b) dU/dx = 2x − 4 = 0 → x = 2.

10. A force F(x) = 6x − 4 (in newtons) acts on an object from x = 1 m to x = 4 m. Answer both parts:

(a) Which expression gives the work done?

(b) Fill in the blank: W = ___ J.

(a) Work is the definite integral of force over the displacement. (b) ∫(6x − 4)dx = 3x² − 4x. At x = 4: 48 − 16 = 32. At x = 1: 3 − 4 = −1. W = 32 − (−1) = 33 J.

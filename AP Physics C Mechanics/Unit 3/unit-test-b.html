1. Fill in the blank: a force F(x) = 4x N acts on an object from x = 0 to x = 5 m. The work done is ___ J.

W = ∫₀⁵ 4x dx = 2x² |₀⁵ = 2(25) = 50 J.

2. A 3 kg object starts at rest, and a net force does 24 J of work on it. What is its final speed?

W = ½mv² − 0 → 24 = ½(3)v² → v² = 16 → v = 4 m/s.

3. In deriving the work-energy theorem, the substitution a dx = v dv is used. Why does dx = v dt in the first place?

Velocity is defined as v = dx/dt, so rearranging directly gives dx = v dt — the substitution that turns a dx into v dv.

4. A potential energy function is U(x) = 5x². What is F(3)?

F(x) = −dU/dx = −10x. F(3) = −30 N.

5. Fill in the blank: a force is called ___ if the work it does depends on the specific path taken, not just the start and end points.

Non-conservative forces (like kinetic friction) do path-dependent work, which is exactly why no single-valued potential energy function can describe them.

6. A spring has k = 80 N/m and is stretched from its natural length to x = 0.5 m. Answer both parts:

(a) The work done stretching a spring from its natural length to displacement x is given by:

(b) Fill in the blank: the work done is ___ J.

(a) W = ∫₀ˣ kx′ dx′ = ½kx². (b) W = ½(80)(0.5²) = ½(80)(0.25) = 10 J.

7. An object of mass 4 kg, initially at rest, has 72 J of net work done on it. Answer both parts:

(a) Which equation relates the net work W to the final speed v?

(b) Fill in the blank: the final speed is ___ m/s.

(a) The work-energy theorem: W = ½mv² − ½mv₀². (b) With v₀ = 0: 72 = ½(4)v² → v² = 36 → v = 6 m/s.

8. Answer both parts:

(a) Which of the following is a conservative force?

(b) A potential energy function is U(x) = 2x² − 3x. Fill in the blank: F(2) = ___ N.

(a) Gravitation's work depends only on the change in separation, never on the path — the definition of conservative. (b) F(x) = −dU/dx = −(4x − 3). F(2) = −(8 − 3) = −5 N.

9. A potential energy function is U(x) = x² − 6x + 8. Answer both parts:

(a) Its one equilibrium point is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the equilibrium occurs at x = ___ m.

(a) U(x) = x² − 6x + 8 opens upward, so its critical point is a minimum — a stable equilibrium. (b) dU/dx = 2x − 6 = 0 → x = 3.

10. A force F(x) = 4x − 2 (in newtons) acts on an object from x = 1 m to x = 3 m. Answer both parts:

(a) Which expression gives the work done?

(b) Fill in the blank: W = ___ J.

(a) Work is the definite integral of force over the displacement. (b) ∫(4x − 2)dx = 2x² − 2x. At x = 3: 18 − 6 = 12. At x = 1: 2 − 2 = 0. W = 12 − 0 = 12 J.

1. Two masses experience a gravitational force of 90 N at separation r₀. What is the force at separation 3r₀?

F ∝ 1/r², so tripling r divides F by 3² = 9: 90/9 = 10 N.

2. Fill in the blank: gravity is a ___ force, always directed along the line joining the two masses — which is why it exerts zero torque about that line.

A central force is always parallel to the position vector r, guaranteeing τ = r × F = 0.

3. Fill in the blank: a planet orbits at radius r₀ with period T₀ = 2 years. Another planet orbits the same star at radius 9r₀. Its period is ___ years.

T² ∝ r³, so (T/T₀)² = 9³ = 729 → T/T₀ = 27 → T = 27(2) = 54 years.

4. An object has total mechanical energy E < 0 relative to a planet. This means the object is:

Negative total energy means the object doesn't have enough kinetic energy to overcome the (negative) potential energy well and reach r → ∞ — it's bound.

5. Which formula gives escape velocity from a planet's surface (mass M, radius R)?

v_esc comes from setting total energy E = 0: ½mv² − GMm/R = 0 → v = √(2GM/R). (√(GM/R) is the circular orbital speed, a different quantity.)

6. Two masses experience a force of 64 N at separation r₀. Answer both parts:

(a) By what factor does the force scale when the separation increases to 4r₀?

(b) Fill in the blank: the force at 4r₀ is ___ N.

(a) F ∝ 1/r², so a 4× distance scales force by 1/4² = 1/16. (b) 64/16 = 4 N.

7. An orbiting body has aphelion distance 5 times its perihelion distance. Answer both parts:

(a) Why does the body's speed increase as it approaches perihelion?

(b) Fill in the blank: vperihelion/vaphelion = ___.

(a) Conservation of angular momentum forces v to grow as r shrinks. (b) mv_pr_p = mv_ar_a → v_p/v_a = r_a/r_p = 5.

8. Answer both parts:

(a) Which formula relates orbital period T to orbital radius r for a circular orbit?

(b) Fill in the blank: if a second orbit has radius 4× the first, its period is ___× the first period.

(a) Setting gravity equal to the centripetal force requirement and solving for T² gives T² = (4π²/GM)r³. (b) (T₂/T₁)² = 4³ = 64 → T₂/T₁ = 8.

(b) A planet has 2GM/R = 1.44 × 10⁸ m²/s². Fill in the blank: its escape velocity is ___ km/s.

(a) v_esc = √(2GM/R) — larger R in the denominator means smaller v_esc. (b) v_esc = √(1.44×10⁸) = 12,000 m/s = 12 km/s.

10. An object has KE = 500 J and gravitational potential energy U = −800 J at some point. Answer both parts:

(a) Is this object bound or unbound?

(b) Fill in the blank: the object's total mechanical energy is ___ J.

(a) E = KE + U = 500 + (−800) = −300 J, negative, so the object is bound. (b) E = −300 J.

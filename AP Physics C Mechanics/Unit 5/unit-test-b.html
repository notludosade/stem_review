1. Which rule determines the direction of the torque vector τ = r × F?

τ = r × F is a cross product, and the right-hand rule gives its direction: curl from r to F, thumb points along τ.

2. Fill in the blank: a uniform rod of mass 15 kg and length 3 m rotates about one end. Its moment of inertia is ___ kg·m².

I = ⅓ML² = ⅓(15)(3²) = ⅓(15)(9) = 45 kg·m².

3. Fill in the blank: a wheel has θ(t) = 3t² − 5t (radians). Its angular velocity at t = 4 s is ___ rad/s.

ω(t) = dθ/dt = 6t − 5. ω(4) = 24 − 5 = 19 rad/s.

4. Fill in the blank: a net torque of 24 N·m acts on a wheel with moment of inertia 6 kg·m². Its angular acceleration is ___ rad/s².

α = τ/I = 24/6 = 4 rad/s².

5. Under what condition is L = Iω conserved for a rotating system?

Since τ_net = dL/dt, L is constant exactly when net external torque is zero — I and ω can each still change, as long as their product doesn't.

6. A uniform rod has mass 10 kg and length 3 m, rotating about one end. Answer both parts:

(a) Which integral gives its moment of inertia?

(b) Fill in the blank: I = ___ kg·m².

(a) Each mass element dm = (M/L)dx contributes x²dm. (b) I = ⅓ML² = ⅓(10)(9) = 30 kg·m².

7. A wheel has θ(t) = t³ − 6t (radians). Answer both parts:

(a) What is ω(t)?

(b) Fill in the blank: α(3) = ___ rad/s².

(a) ω(t) = 3t² − 6. (b) α(t) = 6t, so α(3) = 18 rad/s².

8. A wheel with moment of inertia 10 kg·m² has angular acceleration 2.5 rad/s². Answer both parts:

(a) Which equation relates net torque to angular acceleration?

(b) Fill in the blank: the net torque is ___ N·m.

(a) τ_net = Iα, the rotational form of Newton's second law. (b) τ = 10(2.5) = 25 N·m.

9. A skater spins at ω₀ = 3 rad/s with I₀ = 6 kg·m², then pulls her arms in to reach If = 1.5 kg·m². Answer both parts:

(a) Why is her angular momentum conserved during this motion?

(b) Fill in the blank: her new angular velocity is ___ rad/s.

(a) With no net external torque about the spin axis, L = Iω stays fixed. (b) I₀ω₀ = I_fω_f → 6(3) = 1.5(ω_f) → ω_f = 12 rad/s.

10. A wheel spins clockwise, as viewed from where you're standing. Answer both parts:

(a) By the right-hand rule, its angular momentum vector points:

(b) If this wheel has I = 4 kg·m² and ω = 5 rad/s, fill in the blank: |L| = ___ kg·m²/s.

(a) Curling the right hand's fingers clockwise (as seen by the viewer) points the thumb into the page, away from the viewer. (b) |L| = Iω = 4(5) = 20 kg·m²/s.

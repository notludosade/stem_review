1. Curling your right hand's fingers from the position vector r toward the force F, your thumb points:

This is the standard right-hand rule for the cross product τ = r × F.

2. Fill in the blank: a uniform rod of mass 9 kg and length 3 m rotates about one end. Its moment of inertia is ___ kg·m².

I = ⅓ML² = ⅓(9)(3²) = ⅓(9)(9) = 27 kg·m².

3. Fill in the blank: a wheel has θ(t) = 4t² − 2t (radians). Its angular velocity at t = 3 s is ___ rad/s.

ω(t) = dθ/dt = 8t − 2. ω(3) = 24 − 2 = 22 rad/s.

4. Fill in the blank: a net torque of 15 N·m acts on a wheel with moment of inertia 3 kg·m². Its angular acceleration is ___ rad/s².

α = τ/I = 15/3 = 5 rad/s².

5. Angular momentum L = Iω is conserved whenever:

Since τ_net = dL/dt, L is constant exactly when net external torque is zero — I and ω can each still change, as long as their product doesn't.

6. A uniform rod has mass 12 kg and length 2 m, rotating about one end. Answer both parts:

(a) Which integral gives its moment of inertia?

(b) Fill in the blank: I = ___ kg·m².

(a) Each mass element dm = (M/L)dx contributes x²dm. (b) I = ⅓ML² = ⅓(12)(4) = 16 kg·m².

7. A wheel has θ(t) = t³ − 4t (radians). Answer both parts:

(a) What is ω(t)?

(b) Fill in the blank: α(2) = ___ rad/s².

(a) ω(t) = 3t² − 4. (b) α(t) = 6t, so α(2) = 12 rad/s².

8. A wheel with moment of inertia 8 kg·m² has angular acceleration 3.5 rad/s². Answer both parts:

(a) Which equation relates net torque to angular acceleration?

(b) Fill in the blank: the net torque is ___ N·m.

(a) τ_net = Iα, the rotational form of Newton's second law. (b) τ = 8(3.5) = 28 N·m.

9. A skater spins at ω₀ = 4 rad/s with I₀ = 5 kg·m², then pulls her arms in to reach If = 2 kg·m². Answer both parts:

(a) Why is her angular momentum conserved during this motion?

(b) Fill in the blank: her new angular velocity is ___ rad/s.

(a) With no net external torque about the spin axis, L = Iω stays fixed. (b) I₀ω₀ = I_fω_f → 5(4) = 2(ω_f) → ω_f = 10 rad/s.

10. A wheel spins counterclockwise, as viewed from where you're standing. Answer both parts:

(a) By the right-hand rule, its angular momentum vector points:

(b) If this wheel has I = 3 kg·m² and ω = 7 rad/s, fill in the blank: |L| = ___ kg·m²/s.

(a) Curling the right hand's fingers counterclockwise points the thumb out of the page, toward the viewer. (b) |L| = Iω = 3(7) = 21 kg·m²/s.

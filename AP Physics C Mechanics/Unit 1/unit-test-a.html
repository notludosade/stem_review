1. Given x(t) = 4t³ − 5t, what is v(t)?

By the power rule: d/dt(4t³) = 12t², d/dt(−5t) = −5. So v(t) = 12t² − 5.

2. Which expression correctly defines acceleration in terms of position x(t)?

Acceleration is the derivative of velocity, and velocity is the derivative of position — so acceleration is the second derivative of position, d²x/dt². dx/dt alone is velocity, not acceleration.

3. Fill in the blank: a particle has velocity v(t) = 6t − 9 m/s. It is momentarily at rest at t = ___ s.

Set v(t) = 0: 6t − 9 = 0 → t = 1.5 s.

4. Which of the following correctly relates a(t) to v(t)?

Since a = dv/dt, integrating a(t) recovers v(t) up to a constant, which an initial condition like v(0) = v₀ pins down. This works for any a(t), not just constant acceleration.

5. Fill in the blank: recovering v(t) from a(t) requires finding an ___ of a(t), then using an initial condition to fix the constant.

Since a(t) = dv/dt, reversing that relationship means v(t) must be an antiderivative of a(t).

6. Given x(t) = 2t³ − 9t² + 5, answer both parts:

(a) What is v(t)?

(b) Fill in the blank: a(2) = ___ m/s².

(a) v(t) = 6t² − 18t. (b) a(t) = 12t − 18, so a(2) = 24 − 18 = 6 m/s².

7. A particle has constant acceleration a(t) = 3 m/s² and initial velocity v₀ = 2 m/s. Answer both parts:

(a) What is v(t)?

(b) If x₀ = 0, fill in the blank: x(4) = ___ m.

(a) v(t) = v₀ + at = 2 + 3t. (b) x(t) = x₀ + v₀t + ½at² = 2t + 1.5t², so x(4) = 2(4) + 1.5(16) = 8 + 24 = 32 m.

8. A particle's position is r(t) = (3t) î + (2t²) ĵ (meters). Answer both parts:

(a) What is v(t)?

(b) Fill in the blank: the particle's speed at t = 1 s is ___ m/s.

(a) Differentiate each component: d/dt(3t) = 3, d/dt(2t²) = 4t, so v(t) = 3î + 4tĵ. (b) At t = 1: v = (3, 4), speed = √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 m/s.

9. An object has acceleration a(t) = 2t m/s² from t = 0 to t = 3 s. Answer both parts:

(a) The object's change in velocity Δv over this interval equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: Δv = ___ m/s.

(a) Since a = dv/dt, Δv over an interval is the definite integral of a(t), which is the area under its graph. (b) Δv = ∫₀³ 2t dt = t²|₀³ = 9 m/s.

10. An object has velocity v(t) = 4 − 2t m/s over the interval t = 0 to t = 3 s (it reverses direction at t = 2 s). Answer both parts:

(a) What is the object's displacement Δx over [0, 3]?

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled (not displacement) over [0, 3] is ___ m.

(a) Δx = ∫₀³ (4 − 2t) dt = [4t − t²]₀³ = 12 − 9 = 3 m. (b) Since v(t) = 0 at t = 2, split the integral: from 0 to 2, Δx = 8 − 4 = 4 m; from 2 to 3, Δx = (12 − 9) − 4 = −1 m. Total distance = |4| + |−1| = 5 m, larger than the net displacement because the object backtracked.

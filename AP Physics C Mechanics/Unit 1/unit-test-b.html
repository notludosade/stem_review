1. Given x(t) = 3t⁴ − 2t, what is v(t)?

By the power rule: d/dt(3t⁴) = 12t³, d/dt(−2t) = −2. So v(t) = 12t³ − 2.

2. A particle's velocity is v(t) = 5t² − 3t. Which expression gives its acceleration a(t)?

Acceleration is the derivative of velocity: a(t) = dv/dt = 10t − 3, by the power rule term by term.

3. Fill in the blank: a particle has velocity v(t) = 8t − 20 m/s. It is momentarily at rest at t = ___ s.

Set v(t) = 0: 8t − 20 = 0 → t = 2.5 s.

4. Which of the following correctly relates v(t) to x(t)?

Since v = dx/dt, integrating v(t) recovers x(t) up to a constant, which an initial condition like x(0) = x₀ pins down — for any v(t), not just constant velocity.

5. Fill in the blank: recovering x(t) from v(t) requires finding an ___ of v(t), then using an initial condition to fix the constant.

Since v(t) = dx/dt, reversing that relationship means x(t) must be an antiderivative of v(t).

6. Given x(t) = 3t³ − 6t² + 2, answer both parts:

(a) What is v(t)?

(b) Fill in the blank: a(3) = ___ m/s².

(a) v(t) = 9t² − 12t. (b) a(t) = 18t − 12, so a(3) = 54 − 12 = 42 m/s².

7. A particle has constant acceleration a(t) = 5 m/s² and initial velocity v₀ = 1 m/s. Answer both parts:

(a) What is v(t)?

(b) If x₀ = 0, fill in the blank: x(3) = ___ m.

(a) v(t) = v₀ + at = 1 + 5t. (b) x(t) = x₀ + v₀t + ½at² = t + 2.5t², so x(3) = 3 + 2.5(9) = 3 + 22.5 = 25.5 m.

8. A particle's position is r(t) = (4t) î + (3t²) ĵ (meters). Answer both parts:

(a) What is v(t)?

(b) Fill in the blank: the particle's speed at t = 0.5 s is ___ m/s.

(a) Differentiate each component: d/dt(4t) = 4, d/dt(3t²) = 6t, so v(t) = 4î + 6tĵ. (b) At t = 0.5: v = (4, 3), speed = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 m/s.

9. An object has acceleration a(t) = 4t m/s² from t = 0 to t = 2 s. Answer both parts:

(a) The object's change in velocity Δv over this interval equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: Δv = ___ m/s.

(a) Since a = dv/dt, Δv over an interval is the definite integral of a(t), which is the area under its graph. (b) Δv = ∫₀² 4t dt = 2t²|₀² = 8 m/s.

10. An object has velocity v(t) = 6 − 2t m/s over the interval t = 0 to t = 4 s (it reverses direction at t = 3 s). Answer both parts:

(a) What is the object's displacement Δx over [0, 4]?

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled (not displacement) over [0, 4] is ___ m.

(a) Δx = ∫₀⁴ (6 − 2t) dt = [6t − t²]₀⁴ = 24 − 16 = 8 m. (b) Since v(t) = 0 at t = 3, split the integral: from 0 to 3, Δx = 18 − 9 = 9 m; from 3 to 4, Δx = (24 − 16) − 9 = −1 m. Total distance = |9| + |−1| = 10 m, larger than the net displacement because the object backtracked.

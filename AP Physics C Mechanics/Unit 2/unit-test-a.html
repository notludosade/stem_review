1. Solving ΣF = m d²x/dt² for x(t) requires how many independent initial conditions, and why?

Integrating a(t) once to get v(t) introduces one constant; integrating again to get x(t) introduces a second. Two known values — typically x₀ and v₀ — are needed to fix both.

2. If ΣF = 0 for an object, the equation m d²x/dt² = 0 implies:

Integrating d²x/dt² = 0 once gives dx/dt = constant, which may or may not be zero — this is Newton's first law falling directly out of the ΣF = 0 case of the governing differential equation.

3. Fill in the blank: an object of mass 5 kg falls under gravity (g = 9.8 m/s²) with linear drag coefficient b = 2 kg/s. Its terminal velocity is ___ m/s.

v_term = mg/b = 5(9.8)/2 = 24.5 m/s.

4. Which calculus technique is used to solve m dv/dt = −bv for v(t)?

Rearranging to dv/v = −(b/m)dt separates the variables onto opposite sides so each can be integrated independently.

5. Fill in the blank: an equation like x₁ + x₂ = L that ties two connected objects' positions together is called a ___ equation.

A constraint equation captures a geometric restriction — here, fixed string length — that, once differentiated, links the objects' velocities and accelerations too.

6. A 2 kg object experiences a constant net force of 10 N, starting with v₀ = 3 m/s and x₀ = 0. Answer both parts:

(a) What is v(4), the velocity after 4 s?

(b) Fill in the blank: x(4) = ___ m.

a = F/m = 10/2 = 5 m/s². (a) v(4) = 3 + 5(4) = 23 m/s. (b) x(4) = 3(4) + ½(5)(4²) = 12 + 40 = 52 m.

7. An object moving through a resistive medium has only linear drag acting on it, m dv/dt = −bv, with v₀ = 10 m/s, b = 1 kg/s, m = 2 kg. Answer both parts:

(a) What form does v(t) take?

(b) Fill in the blank: v(2) = ___ m/s (to two decimal places).

(a) Separation of variables gives exponential decay, v(t) = v₀e^(−(b/m)t). (b) b/m = 0.5, so v(2) = 10e^(−1) ≈ 3.68 m/s.

8. An Atwood machine has m₁ = 4 kg and m₂ = 6 kg (g = 9.8 m/s²). Answer both parts:

(a) The magnitude of the system's acceleration is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the string tension is ___ N.

(a) a = (m₂ − m₁)g/(m₁ + m₂) = (2)(9.8)/10 = 1.96 m/s². (b) T = m₁(g + a) = 4(9.8 + 1.96) = 4(11.76) = 47.04 N.

9. A 3 kg block sits on a frictionless table and is connected by a string over a pulley at the table's edge to a hanging 2 kg mass (g = 9.8 m/s²). Answer both parts:

(a) Which expression gives the system's acceleration?

(b) Fill in the blank: the system's acceleration is ___ m/s².

Only m₂'s weight drives the system (the table's normal force removes gravity from m₁'s equation along the direction of motion): a = m₂g/(m₁ + m₂) = 2(9.8)/5 = 3.92 m/s².

10. For two objects connected by a single ideal (massless string, frictionless pulley) connection, answer both parts:

(a) Why is it correct to use one tension value T for both objects' equations, rather than two different tensions?

(b) Fill in the blank: if m₁'s acceleration has magnitude 2.4 m/s², the magnitude of m₂'s acceleration is ___ m/s².

(a) An ideal (massless) string over a frictionless, massless pulley carries the same tension throughout. (b) The constraint a₁ = −a₂ (equal magnitude, opposite sign) means m₂'s acceleration also has magnitude 2.4 m/s².

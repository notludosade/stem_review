Projects

A project is an open-ended capstone brief with guided reflection — not another quiz. Each one exists to see whether you've actually proved yourself on the courses behind it, so it stays locked in this browser until you've passed every one of their course exams. Each project belongs to a pathway, but you don't need to visit the pathway to attempt it — the lock status below is computed straight from your saved progress.

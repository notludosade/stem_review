1. According to this unit's checklist, what should be evaluated first when choosing a compute model for a workload?

Traffic pattern is the first, most decisive question when choosing between VMs, containers, and serverless.

2. Fill in the blank: a spiky internal API with long idle stretches between bursts is best matched to ___ compute, since scale-to-zero avoids paying for idle capacity.

Serverless's scale-to-zero property fits a spiky, mostly-idle traffic pattern especially well.

3. A steady-state order database with constant transactional load is best matched to which storage approach?

Block storage's low-latency small I/O, paired with a managed database, fits steady transactional workloads.

4. Why is copying a huge streaming company's public architecture diagram risky for a small startup's internal tool?

An architecture should be evaluated against the actual workload's needs, not copied wholesale from a different scale and problem.

5. What is vendor lock-in?

Lock-in describes real switching difficulty and cost, not a legal or contractual restriction.

6. Two companies plan a migration. Answer both parts:

(a) A company on standard VMs and an open-source database faces what kind of migration?

(b) A company built around a proprietary serverless event system faces what kind of migration?

Standardized technology transfers with modest effort; proprietary interfaces are baked into the application code itself, making migration much harder.

7. Fill in the blank: this course argues that more managed ___ from a provider often comes with more lock-in, echoing Unit 1's IaaS/PaaS/SaaS spectrum.

The more a provider manages, the more convenient a service tends to be — often at the cost of more proprietary dependence.

8. What is multi-cloud?

Multi-cloud is a deliberate strategy to reduce dependence on any single provider, at the cost of added complexity.

9. Why does this course argue that accepting some lock-in isn't automatically a mistake?

The mistake isn't accepting lock-in — it's not knowing how much a decision creates or whether the tradeoff is worth it.

10. Fill in the blank: automated deployment pipelines and running many containers together in production are topics left for a follow-on ___ course, not covered in depth here.

This course covers the infrastructure building blocks; CI/CD and container orchestration are explicitly out of scope, left for a follow-on DevOps course.

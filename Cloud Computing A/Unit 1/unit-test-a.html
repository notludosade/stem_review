1. What does it mean for an organization to run infrastructure "on-prem"?

On-premises means owning and operating the physical hardware yourself, rather than renting it from a provider.

2. Fill in the blank: the process of estimating future demand and buying enough hardware to meet it, without buying so much that most of it sits idle, is called ___.

Capacity planning is especially hard on-prem, since hardware has a long lead time and demand is only a guess.

3. Which of these best defines cloud computing?

The defining traits are renting metered infrastructure and adjusting usage quickly, not any particular user-facing technology.

4. Fill in the blank: a large upfront purchase of a long-lived asset, like buying a server outright, is classified in accounting as ___.

CapEx is a big upfront cost, in contrast to OpEx's smaller, ongoing, usage-based payments.

5. Under the shared responsibility model, which task is always the cloud provider's job, regardless of service model?

Physical facility and hardware security stay with the provider no matter which service model (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) is in use.

6. A company's data center loses power due to a provider hardware fault, and separately, a company employee leaves a cloud storage bucket publicly readable by mistake. Answer both parts:

(a) The power fault is an example of a failure in security or reliability...

(b) The publicly readable bucket is an example of a failure in security...

Physical infrastructure faults are "of the cloud"; misconfigurations the customer makes on top of that infrastructure are "in the cloud."

7. Fill in the blank: the service model that rents raw virtual machines and networking, leaving the customer to install and patch their own operating system, is called ___.

IaaS leaves the OS layer and everything above it to the customer, unlike PaaS or SaaS.

8. What distinguishes public cloud from private cloud?

Multi-tenancy on shared hardware, isolated by software, is the defining trait of public cloud.

9. Why might a hospital network choose private cloud over public cloud for its patient records system?

Tighter physical and regulatory control, not raw speed, is the usual reason to trade away public cloud's shared-hardware benefits.

10. Fill in the blank: deliberately splitting one system across both private/on-prem and public cloud infrastructure, connected and working together, is called ___ cloud.

Hybrid cloud matches each workload to whichever deployment model actually fits its requirements.

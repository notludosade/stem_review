Linear Algebra A Glossary

← Contents

Span

All linear combinations of given vectors.

Basis

Independent spanning set.

Rank

Dimension of column space.

Determinant

Signed volume scale of a square matrix.

Eigenvalue

Scale λ in Av=λv.

Eigenspace

Null space of A−λI.

Diagonalization

Factorization A=PDP⁻¹.

Stochastic matrix

Matrix of transition probabilities.

PCA

Projection onto covariance eigenvectors ordered by variance.

Eigenvalues and the Characteristic Polynomial

Some directions survive a transformation without turning; eigenvalues record their scale factors.

← Lesson 15 Lesson 17 → Contents Glossary →

DefinitionA nonzero vector v is an eigenvector of A when Av=λv. Its eigenvalue λ solves det(A−λI)=0, the characteristic equation.
Worked ExampleFor A=[[2,1],[1,2]], det(A−λI)=(2−λ)²−1=(λ−3)(λ−1). Eigenvalues are 3 and 1.
Why This MattersEigenvalues expose long-term dynamics, stable directions, and dimensions that matter most in data.

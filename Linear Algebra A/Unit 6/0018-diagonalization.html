Diagonalization

A basis of eigenvectors changes A into a diagonal matrix and makes repeated powers easy.

← Lesson 17 Lesson 19 → Contents Glossary →

DefinitionA is diagonalizable when A=PDP⁻¹, where columns of P are a basis of eigenvectors and D contains matching eigenvalues on its diagonal.
Power ShortcutAⁿ=PDⁿP⁻¹. Raising D to a power only raises each diagonal entry.
Why This MattersDiagonalization turns coupled transformations into independent scalings—the engine behind Markov chains and PCA.

1. For A = [[0, 3], [−2, 5]] and B = [[4, −1], [1, 1]], what is A + B?

Add entry-by-entry: (0 + 4, 3 + −1; −2 + 1, 5 + 1) = (4, 2; −1, 6).

2. Fill in the blank: for M = [[2, 5], [−1, 0]], the scalar multiple 3M = ___.

Scale every entry by 3: (3·2, 3·5; 3·−1, 3·0) = (6, 15; −3, 0).

3. For A = [[2, −1], [0, 6], [3, 5]] (a 3×2 matrix), answer both parts:

(a) What are the dimensions of Aᵀ?

(b) Fill in the blank: the entry in row 2, column 2 of Aᵀ equals ___.

(a) Transposing swaps rows and columns: 3×2 becomes 2×3. (b) Entry (2,2) of Aᵀ is entry (2,2) of A, which is 6.

4. For A = [[2, 0], [1, −3]] and B = [[1, 5], [−2, 4]], answer both parts:

(a) What is entry (1,2) of AB?

(b) Fill in the blank: entry (2,1) of AB = ___.

(a) Row 1 of A, (2,0), dotted with column 2 of B, (5,4): (2)(5)+(0)(4) = 10. (b) Row 2 of A, (1,−3), dotted with column 1 of B, (1,−2): (1)(1)+(−3)(−2) = 1 + 6 = 7.

5. If A is 3×2 and B is 2×5, how do AB and BA compare?

A's columns (2) match B's rows (2), so AB exists as 3×5. For BA, B's columns (5) would need to match A's rows (3) — they don't, so BA isn't defined.

6. Answer both parts:

(a) For any compatible matrix A, what is AI?

(b) Fill in the blank: I₂, the 2×2 identity matrix, has ___ zero entries.

(a) I is the multiplicative identity, so AI = A regardless of order. (b) I₂ = [[1,0],[0,1]] has 2 ones and 2 zeros.

7. Fill in the blank: only ___ matrices are even eligible to have an inverse.

A non-square matrix can't satisfy AA⁻¹ = A⁻¹A = I, since AA⁻¹ and A⁻¹A wouldn't even have matching dimensions — invertibility only applies to square matrices.

8. For A = [[3, 1], [2, 1]], answer both parts:

(a) What is det(A) = ad − bc?

(b) Fill in the blank: A⁻¹ = ___.

(a) ad − bc = (3)(1) − (1)(2) = 1. (b) A⁻¹ = (1/1)·[[1, −1], [−2, 3]] = [[1, −1], [−2, 3]].

9. Which of these matrices is singular (has no inverse)?

ad − bc = (−1)(−6) − (3)(2) = 6 − 6 = 0 — row 2 is exactly −2 times row 1, producing a zero determinant and no inverse.

10. For A = [[0, 2], [1, 1]] and B = [[3, 0], [−1, 2]], answer both parts:

(a) What is A + B?

(b) Fill in the blank: AB = ___.

(a) Add entry-by-entry: (0+3, 2+0; 1+−1, 1+2) = (3, 2; 0, 3). (b) Row 1 of A, (0,2), dotted with each column of B: (0·3+2·−1, 0·0+2·2) = (−2, 4). Row 2 of A, (1,1): (1·3+1·−1, 1·0+1·2) = (2, 2). AB = [[−2, 4], [2, 2]].

# Real Analysis A Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Understanding Analysis_ — Stephen Abbott](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4939-2712-8)
  The standard first-course text: motivates every definition with the calculus question it's fixing, before formalizing it. Use for: the primary worked narrative behind nearly every lesson in this course.
- [Book: _Introduction to Real Analysis_ — William F. Trench](https://digitalcommons.trinity.edu/mono/7/)
  Free, legally-hosted full textbook, more terse and traditionally-organized than Abbott. Use for: a second explanation when Abbott's narrative style isn't clicking, and for extra practice problems with solutions.
- [Book: _Principles of Mathematical Analysis_ — Walter Rudin](https://archive.org/details/PrinciplesOfMathematicalAnalysis)
  The classic, famously terse graduate-adjacent reference ("baby Rudin"). Use for: seeing the same theorems stated in their most compressed, canonical form once a topic already feels solid — not a first explanation.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare 18.100A: Real Analysis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-100a-real-analysis-fall-2020/)
  Full lecture notes and video for a first real analysis course covering exactly this course's scope. Use for: an alternate spoken walkthrough of any proof that isn't clicking from text alone.

## Wisdom (Communities)

1. A license plate has 3 letters followed by 2 digits, each character chosen independently (26 letters, 10 digits, repetition allowed). How many license plates are possible?

Each of the 5 characters is an independent choice: 26 · 26 · 26 · 10 · 10 = 1,757,600, by the multiplication principle.

2. A student must take exactly one science section: 3 chemistry sections or 5 biology sections (no section counts as both). How many ways can they satisfy this requirement?

The two groups of sections are mutually exclusive alternatives, so the addition principle applies: 3 + 5 = 8.

3. In how many ways can 3 of 7 distinct trophies be arranged in a row, where order matters?

Order matters, so this is a permutation: P(7,3) = 7 · 6 · 5 = 210.

4. Fill in the blank: 7! = ___.

7! = 7·6·5·4·3·2·1 = 5,040.

5. A lottery draws 6 numbers from a pool of 49; order is irrelevant. Which expression counts the number of possible outcomes?

Since the drawn numbers' order doesn't matter, this is a combination, C(49,6), not a permutation.

6. A 5-book set, with no distinct positions, is chosen from 12 available books. Answer both parts:

(a) Which counting tool applies?

(b) Fill in the blank: C(12,5) = ___.

(a) With no distinct positions, reordering the 5 chosen books produces the same set, so this counts combinations, not permutations. (b) C(12,5) = 12!/(5!·7!) = 792.

7. Consider the fact that C(7,3) = C(6,2) + C(6,3). Answer both parts:

(a) Which identity explains this?

(b) Fill in the blank: by the symmetry identity, C(10,3) = C(10,___).

(a) Pascal's identity splits "choose r from n" into whether a fixed item is included or excluded, matching this exact pattern. (b) Symmetry gives C(10,3) = C(10, 10−3) = C(10,7).

8. Row 5 of Pascal's triangle is 1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the coefficient of x³y² in the expansion of (x+y)⁵?

(b) Fill in the blank: the full expansion is x⁵ + 5x⁴y + 10x³y² + ___x²y³ + 5xy⁴ + y⁵.

(a)–(b) The Binomial Theorem pairs row 5's coefficients directly with each term; by symmetry the coefficient of x²y³ matches x³y²'s coefficient, C(5,2) = C(5,3) = 10.

9. A password has 5 characters, each independently chosen from 26 letters, repetition allowed. Answer both parts:

(a) How many such passwords are possible?

(b) Fill in the blank: if repetition were NOT allowed, the count becomes P(26,5) = 26 × 25 × 24 × 23 × ___.

(a) 26⁵ = 11,881,376 by the multiplication principle with repetition allowed. (b) Without repetition, each successive character loses one more option: 26, 25, 24, 23, 22.

10. A 4-person team is chosen from 9 candidates, with no distinct roles. Answer both parts:

(a) What is the correct count?

(b) Fill in the blank: by symmetry, C(9,4) = C(9,___).

(a) With no distinct roles, C(9,4) = 9!/(4!·5!) = 126. (b) Symmetry gives C(9,4) = C(9, 9−4) = C(9,5).

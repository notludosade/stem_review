1. The average value of f(x) = x² + 1 on [0, 3] is:

favg = (1/3)∫₀³(x² + 1)dx = (1/3)[x³/3 + x]₀³ = (1/3)(9 + 3) = (1/3)(12) = 4 — dividing the integral's value of 12 by the interval width of 3, not leaving it undivided (12), dividing by the wrong width (6), or dropping the "+1" term's contribution (3).

2. A particle moves with velocity v(t) = t² − t − 2 ft/sec on [0, 3]. Answer both parts.

(a) The displacement of the particle over [0, 3] is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled over [0, 3] is ___ (as a fraction)

(a) Displacement is the plain integral ∫₀³v(t)dt = F(3) − F(0), where F(t) = t³/3 − t²/2 − 2t: F(3) = −3/2 and F(0) = 0, so displacement = −3/2. (b) v(t) = (t − 2)(t + 1) is negative on [0, 2) and positive on (2, 3], so distance = |F(2) − F(0)| + |F(3) − F(2)| = 10/3 + 11/6 = 31/6 — more than the magnitude of the displacement because the particle reversed direction at t = 2.

3. Fill in the blank: Water flows into a tank at r(t) = 4√t gallons/minute. The amount that flows in during the first 9 minutes is ___ gallons

∫₀⁹ 4√t dt = (8/3)t^(3/2) evaluated from 0 to 9 = (8/3)(27) = 72 gallons — the Net Change Theorem applied directly to the given rate function.

4. Find the area between y = 6 − x² and y = x. Answer both parts.

(a) The intersection points of y = 6 − x² and y = x are at x =:

(b) Fill in the blank: the area between the curves is ___ (as a fraction)

(a) Setting 6 − x² = x gives x² + x − 6 = (x + 3)(x − 2) = 0, so x = −3 and x = 2. (b) Testing x = 0 shows the parabola sits above the line on this interval, so Area = ∫₋₃²[(6 − x²) − x]dx = [6x − x³/3 − x²/2]₋₃² = 22/3 − (−27/2) = 125/6.

5. On an interval where two curves cross exactly three times, the area between them requires:

Three crossings create two subintervals, and which curve sits on top can differ between them — each piece needs its own top-minus-bottom integrand and its own evaluation, then the pieces are added.

6. The base region is bounded by y = x² and the x-axis on [0, 2]. Cross sections perpendicular to the x-axis are squares. Answer both parts.

(a) A(x), the cross-sectional area at a given x, equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: the volume of the solid is ___ (as a fraction)

(a) The base's width is s(x) = x² − 0 = x², and a square cross section has area A(x) = s(x)² = (x²)² = x⁴. (b) V = ∫₀² x⁴ dx = [x⁵/5]₀² = 32/5.

7. Fill in the blank: Revolving the region under y = x² on [0, 2] around the x-axis gives a volume of ___ (use "pi" for π)

R(x) = x², so V = π∫₀²(x²)²dx = π∫₀²x⁴dx = π[x⁵/5]₀² = 32π/5.

8. Revolve the region between y = x (outer) and y = x² (inner) on [0, 1] around the x-axis. Answer both parts.

(a) The volume integral (before evaluating) is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the volume is ___ (as a fraction, use "pi" for π)

(a) R(x) = x (outer) and r(x) = x² (inner), so the integrand is R(x)² − r(x)² = x² − x⁴ — not the unsquared radii, the subtraction in the wrong order, or an addition instead of subtraction. (b) V = π∫₀¹[x² − x⁴]dx = π[x³/3 − x⁵/5]₀¹ = π[1/3 − 1/5] = 2π/15.

9. The arc length formula for a smooth curve y = f(x) on [a, b] is:

A tiny piece of curve has horizontal leg dx and vertical leg f′(x)dx, so its length is √(dx² + [f′(x)dx]²) = dx·√(1 + [f′(x)]²) by the Pythagorean theorem — not the derivative alone, the original function squared, or the derivative squared alone.

10. A particle's position is x(t) = 6t², y(t) = 4t³ for t in [0, 1]. Answer both parts.

(a) The particle's speed, √([x′(t)]² + [y′(t)]²), simplifies to:

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled over [0, 1] is ___ (exact form)

(a) x′(t) = 12t and y′(t) = 12t², so speed = √(144t² + 144t⁴) = √(144t²(1 + t²)) = 12t√(1 + t²) for t ≥ 0 — not with the wrong power of t outside the root, a missing t factor, or a doubled coefficient. (b) Distance = ∫₀¹12t√(1 + t²)dt; substituting w = 1 + t² (dw = 2t dt) gives 6∫₁²√w dw = 4[w^(3/2)]₁² = 4(2√2 − 1) = 8√2 − 4.

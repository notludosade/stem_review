# AP Calculus BC Resources

## Knowledge

- [AP Calculus AB and BC Course and Exam Description (College Board, PDF)](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-calculus-ab-and-bc-course-and-exam-description.pdf)
  The authoritative source: official Unit 1–10 framework, every learning objective, the Mathematical Practices, and full exam format. This is the backbone every lesson's scope and ordering is checked against. See also the [AP Calculus BC course page](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-calculus-bc) and the [exam page](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-calculus-bc/exam) (past free-response questions and scoring guidelines).
- [Khan Academy — AP®︎/College Calculus BC](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/ap-calculus-bc)
  Free, unit-by-unit, explicitly aligned to the College Board CED (same 10 units, same ordering). Use for: bite-sized re-explanations and instant-feedback practice drilling between lessons here.
- [Paul's Online Math Notes — Calculus I](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/classes/calci/calci.aspx) and [Calculus II](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/classes/calcII/calcII.aspx)
  Terse, rigorous, no-fluff notes (Calc I covers limits/derivatives/early integration; Calc II covers series, parametric/polar — the BC-only material). Use for: a crisp second explanation, and the [cheat sheets](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/extras/cheatsheets_tables.aspx) as quick-lookup references while a lesson is fresh.
- [Professor Leonard — Calculus 1 (YouTube playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLF797E961509B4EB5) and [Calculus 2 (YouTube playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDesaqWTN6EQ2J4vgsN1HyBeRADEh4Cw-)
  Full lecture-style walkthroughs. Calc 1 covers limits through basic integration; Calc 2 covers series, Taylor polynomials, parametric/polar — the BC-specific back half. Use for: watching a topic taught step-by-step when reading alone isn't landing.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/APStudents](https://www.reddit.com/r/APStudents/)
  Large, active community of AP students specifically, including a running Calc BC audience each exam season. Use for: pacing questions, exam-format questions, comparing notes with other BC students.
- [r/calculus](https://www.reddit.com/r/calculus/)
  General but well-moderated calculus help community, not AP-specific. Use for: posting a specific problem you're stuck on and getting a worked explanation, including from people well past the AP level.

## Gaps

- No community found scoped specifically to AP Calculus BC (vs. general AP or general calculus). Revisit if r/APStudents turns out too focused on exam logistics rather than content help.

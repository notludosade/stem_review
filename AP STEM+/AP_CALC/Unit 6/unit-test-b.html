1. For f(x) = x + 1 on [0, 4] with n = 4 subintervals (Δx = 1), the right Riemann sum equals:

Right endpoints are x = 1, 2, 3, 4, with heights 2, 3, 4, 5. Summing (2 + 3 + 4 + 5)·1 = 14. (The left sum, using x = 0, 1, 2, 3, would give 10; the midpoint sum would give 12.)

2. Fill in the blank: ∫−22 x³ dx = ___, because the positive and negative regions of signed area exactly cancel.

An antiderivative is F(x) = x⁴/4. F(2) − F(−2) = 16/4 − 16/4 = 0. Since x³ is negative on [−2, 0) and positive on (0, 2] with matching magnitudes, the signed areas cancel exactly.

3. Let h(x) = ∫1x (3t² − 2) dt. Answer both parts:

(a) By FTC Part 1, h′(x) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: d/dx ∫0x⁴ sin(t) dt = sin(x⁴) · ___

(a) FTC Part 1 hands back the integrand evaluated at the upper bound x, directly: h′(x) = 3x² − 2 — the lower bound 1 never even enters. (b) The upper bound here is x⁴, not x, so the Chain Rule contributes a factor of d/dx[x⁴] = 4x³.

4. If ∫04 f dx = 6 and ∫49 f dx = −2, then ∫09 f dx equals:

Additivity over adjacent intervals adds the two pieces directly, even when one is negative: 6 + (−2) = 4.

5. ∫03 (4x + 1) dx equals:

An antiderivative of 4x + 1 is F(x) = 2x² + x. By FTC Part 2: F(3) − F(0) = (18 + 3) − 0 = 21.

6. A particle's velocity is v(t) = t − 6 (m/s) on [0, 12]. Answer both parts:

(a) The net displacement over [0, 12] is:

(b) Fill in the blank: the total distance traveled over [0, 12] is ___ meters.

(a) An antiderivative is F(t) = t²/2 − 6t. Net displacement = F(12) − F(0) = 0 − 0 = 0. (b) v(t) = 0 at t = 6, splitting the interval: |F(6) − F(0)| + |F(12) − F(6)| = |−18 − 0| + |0 − (−18)| = 18 + 18 = 36 meters, even though the particle ends up back where it started.

7. Let f(x) = 6x² + 2x. Answer both parts:

(a) The general antiderivative of f is:

(b) Fill in the blank: if F′(x) = 6x² + 2x and F(2) = 10, then C = ___.

(a) Reversing the Power Rule term by term: ∫6x² dx = 2x³, ∫2x dx = x², plus a constant of integration: F(x) = 2x³ + x² + C. (b) F(2) = 16 + 4 + C = 20 + C = 10, so C = −10.

8. For ∫ 6x⁵(x⁶ − 1)² dx, answer both parts:

(a) The correct substitution is:

(b) Fill in the blank: with that substitution, du = ___ dx.

(a) u = x⁶ − 1 is the "inside" piece being raised to a power, and its derivative is exactly the leftover factor outside the parentheses. (b) Differentiating u = x⁶ − 1 gives du = 6x⁵ dx.

9. For ∫ (x² + 4)/(x − 2) dx, answer both parts:

(a) Dividing, (x² + 4)/(x − 2) equals:

(b) Fill in the blank: so ∫ (x² + 4)/(x − 2) dx = x²/2 + 2x + ___·ln|x − 2| + C.

(a) Since (x − 2)(x + 2) = x² − 4, adding back 8 gives x² + 4, so (x² + 4)/(x − 2) = x + 2 + 8/(x − 2). (b) Integrating term by term: ∫(x + 2) dx = x²/2 + 2x, and ∫8/(x − 2) dx = 8·ln|x − 2|.

10. ∫1∞ 1/x³ dx:

An antiderivative of 1/x³ is −1/(2x²). ∫1∞ 1/x³ dx = limb→∞ [−1/(2b²) − (−1/2)] = 0 + 1/2 = 1/2. This matches the rule that ∫1∞ 1/xᵖ dx converges whenever p > 1, and here p = 3.

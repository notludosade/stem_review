# Resources: Career Applied Engineering

## Primary sources
- **NCEES** (ncees.org) — the organization administering the FE and PE exams; the authoritative source on exam structure, eligibility, and the licensure pathway.
- **NSPE Code of Ethics for Engineers** (nspe.org/resources/ethics/code-ethics) — the professional ethics standard referenced throughout the licensure and workplace-ethics lessons.
- **Project Management Institute (PMI)**, *A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)* — the standard reference for project management vocabulary (scope, WBS, risk register) used in this course, simplified to fundamentals.
- **Agile Manifesto** (agilemanifesto.org) — the short, original source text for agile values and principles, referenced directly rather than through a secondhand summary.

## Wisdom communities
- r/AskEngineers and r/engineering (Reddit) — practicing engineers discussing real workplace, interview, and career questions.
- r/ProjectManagement (Reddit) — for project-management-specific questions once the fundamentals in this course are solid.
- IEEE and ASME early-career/student member communities — discipline-specific professional societies with career resources, mentorship programs, and local chapters.

## Gaps
- No single unified textbook covers resumes, interviews, workplace practice, project management, and licensure together — this course synthesizes across career-advice sources, PMI/agile primary sources, and NCEES's own documentation, since the material is inherently interdisciplinary.
- Interview-specific content (especially technical whiteboard problems) varies significantly by company and discipline; this course teaches a general, transferable approach rather than company-specific practice questions.
